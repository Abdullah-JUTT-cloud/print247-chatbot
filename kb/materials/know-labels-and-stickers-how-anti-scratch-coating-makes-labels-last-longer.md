## Article

- Anti scratch coating forms a physical protective shield over labels and preserves label legibility and adhesion. This protective lamination protects printed text and barcodes from every scuff and environmental wear. All this ultimately makes labels and stickers last longer on any surface.
- When your product is delivered with a damaged or curled label, the consumer will instantly equate it with a cheap product inside. A scuffed logo instantly hurts your business reputation. Don't worry at all as the anti scratch coating solves this problem exactly. This clear but tough layer is placed above the label. This coating acts like an insurance policy that prevents costly returns.

## What Is Anti Scratch Coating?

- An anti scratch coating is a strong and transparent shield applied right over your fresh print. Manufacturers roll on this protective liquid finish before the ink fully dries on the press. When it sets, you have a hard barrier that takes all the physical abuse instead of the paper underneath. Using anti scratch coating for product labels protects your important product details and barcodes from daily warehouse wear and tear.

## Ways Anti Scratch Coating Makes Labels Last Longer

- Products vibrate and rub against each other inside the cardboard shipping containers during shipping. This friction creates scuff, tear paper edges, and scratches the essential barcode. This situation is completely unfavorable for brands. An anti-scratch coating puts a hard layer on your product packaging and makes it safe for shipping.
- Here are some ways this scratch resistant coating improves product durability.
- Fights off heavy friction: The strong clear shield stops scuffs caused by forklifts, tight boxes, and rough hands.
- Keeps small print readable: Scanners are able to read barcodes and tiny ingredient lists without errors.
- Stops colors from fading away: A clear barrier prevents ink from fading away, keeping your brand colors vibrant.
- Boosts product shelf life: Anti scratch coating for stickers keeps your goods looking brand new even after sitting in dusty warehouses for months.
- This coating is not less than a strategic financial tool. A label that stays clean in the entire usage cycle in the consumer's home acts as a continuous billboard.

## Where This Tough Clear Coating Works Best for Businesses?

- Some industries overall rely on this extra armor. If your company sells cosmetics, specialty foods, or drinks, your customers handle your products constantly. Therefore, your tags require serious protection. This is also true for heavy industrial tools left outdoors in the rain and mud. Any business dealing with outdoor gear should definitely request anti scratch coating for stickers. It bonds perfectly and stays tough on glass jars, metal cans, plastic tubes, and thick cardboard shippers.
