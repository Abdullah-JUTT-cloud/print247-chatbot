Bag extras include zipper, tear notch, hang hole, window, Euro hole,
degassing valve, child-resistant zipper, easy-open standard zipper
and press-to-close zipper. Child-Guard zippers are also offered.

Extras default to "none" in the quote slots, so a label or box order
is never held up waiting for a bag extra. Only ask about extras for
bags and pouches.

Blog add-on figures (for example zipper or valve amounts) are blog
estimates only and must never be quoted as Print247 prices. See
facts/pricing-approach.md.
