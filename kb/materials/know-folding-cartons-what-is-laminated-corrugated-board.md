## Article

- Packaging that's made from corrugated material is already famous for its high strength structure and protective nature. This corrugated packaging ensures the security of products during the hectic process like transportation, storage, and handling while still remaining lightweight and cost-effective.
- However, a few products still require a packaging that performs more functions than a standard corrugated box.
- These functions could be to resist moisture, bear frequent handling, support premium branding, or maintain a chic appearance. In these situations, manufacturers turn to laminated corrugated boards.
- Laminated corrugated board is a combination of structural strength of corrugated material with an additional laminated layer that enhances performance and appearance.
- The packaging material looks better, lasts longer, and offers more protection against environmental conditions as compared to simple corrugated boards.
- This makes the material to be commonly used in various industries like retail, e-commerce, food, electronics, and consumer goods.

## Understanding How Laminated Corrugated Board Is Made

- The foundation of laminated corrugated board is a standard corrugated sheet.
- This sheet already contains fluted layers that provide cushioning and structural support.
- An additional layer of lamination is then applied on the surface of the corrugated board.
- This layer can be designed for chic appeal, increase material strength, add moisture resistance, or protect printing.
- This combination creates packaging that performs better in demanding environments.

## Why Brands Choose Laminated Corrugated Packaging

- Maintaining a professional appearance is one of the toughest tasks for a packaging throughout the supply process.
- Because the packages are stacked, transported, stored, and handled multiple times before they reach the customer.
- Without any sort of protection to the surface, printed graphics can fade or get damaged.
- Lamination is the solution that can help the packaging to maintain its appearance and improve resistance to wear and tear.
- Many businesses opt to use laminated corrugated boards because they help the packaging look perfect structurally and visually in all stages of shipping.

## Added Resistance to Moisture and Environmental Factors

- Lamination can provide additional protection against moisture and humidity.
- This is ideal when products are stored in warehouses or when traveling through different weather conditions.
- Even though laminated corrugated board is not waterproof, but it still performs better when it comes in contact with moisture due to the surface coating.
