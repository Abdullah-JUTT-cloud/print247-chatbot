## What Does UV Coated Finish Mean?

- UV-coated finish is a crystal clear liquid material that is to be sprayed over printed ink and thus cured instantly by ultraviolet light, which in turn converts it into a solid, protective layer, which is hard and durable.
- Unlike the traditional varnishes, UV coating has a smooth, even surface with a glossy, matte or satin surface, which depends on the formula applied to the printed surface. It is suitable when it comes to using vinyl banners, coroplast signs, rigid PVC boards, and other types of signage.
