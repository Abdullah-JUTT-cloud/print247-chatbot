Mylar pouches use high-barrier film blocking oxygen, moisture, and
light; the hero calls them weatherproof and llms.txt calls them
smell-proof (campaign marketing wording, no test data in the repo).
Jerky bags are food-grade for direct contact. Eco-friendly inks are used.
Thickness: 5-7 mil is recommended only for sharp or heavy products
(jerky, hardware) to prevent punctures — a recommendation, not a film
spec, and not to be merged with the 80-150 micron pouch-film range.
Main finishes (16): metalized foil, metalized + clear front, plain
white, clear/transparent, kraft paper laminate, foil pouch, glossy,
soft-touch, holographic, spot UV, clear window, resealable zipper,
tear notch, hang hole, matte, child-resistant zipper. Jerky finishes
(12): high-barrier metalized foil, metalized + clear front, kraft paper
laminate, clear/transparent, clear window, matte, glossy, soft-touch,
spot UV, resealable zipper, tear notch, hang hole — child-resistant,
plain white, foil pouch and holographic are NOT on the jerky list.
No layer stack, micron spec, valve, or barrier test value is stated here.
