Materials by family, from the internal sales script. State these
as script rules when asked, not as site claims.

Bags and pouches: PET, PE, kraft and foil films.
Labels: PP white vinyl only.
Boxes: board, never film.

Food packaging: FDA-compliant materials, interior linings and inks.
Food-safe coatings include polyethylene, wax coatings, PLA and
aqueous coating. Supplements packaging uses FSC-compliant paperboard
for mushroom products (category page).

Mylar eco options: PLA (polylactic acid) and compostable multi-layer
structures. Water-based inks with nearly zero VOC emission are used
for mylar printing.
