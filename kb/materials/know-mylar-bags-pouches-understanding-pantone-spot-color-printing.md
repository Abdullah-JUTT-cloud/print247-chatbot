## Article

- We live in the world of printing and packaging, color isn't merely an aesthetic choice. Colors tend to speak directly to consumer's emotions and help shape brand perceptions. The colors you print on your product packaging directly impacts consumer psychology.
- Unlike many other printing methods, Pantone (Spot Color) printing uses pre mixed inks rather than layering CMYK dots.
- This printing method helps achieve exact and repeatable colors. It is an ideal printing choice for custom branding, logos, and specialty finishes such as neons and metallic.

## What is a Spot Color?

- A spot color is a solid field of pure and pre mixed inks applied on the Mylar surface. It is applied on the surface as a single solid impression. A spot color uses its own printing plate and ink well on the surface. It guarantees that the brand's specific color looks consistent.

## How Pantone (Spot Color) Printing Works on Mylar Bags?

- Let's take a look at how Pantone (Spot Color) printing works on mylar bags:.
- Spot colors lay down as thick and high density ink onto the non porous surface of mylar film. It provides a deep saturation that stands out instantly.
- Manufacturers use Rotogravure or Flexography printing methods to apply spot colors on mylar.
- Mylar often comes in clear, silver, or semi translucent form. A white ink is used as a base color before applying spot color on the surface of mylar film. This helps prevent the bag's background from changing your brand's specific colors.
- Spot colors are printed on the outside of the mylar bag. For scuff protection, you can print them between layers of film.

## Benefits of Using Pantone (Spot Color) Printing

- Pantone (Spot Color) printing offers several amazing benefits such as:.
- Pantone (Spot color) printing offers color consistency. It prevents even the slight color shift that mostly happens when layering CMYK dots.
- It helps protect brand identity by ensuring customers see the precise shades they are expected to see.
- Pantone (Spot color) printing allows brands to print pastel or metallic colors on mylar surface.

## Final Thoughts

- Pantone (Spot color) printing is the best choice when you aim to achieve consistent brand colors. This printing process avoids color changing and ensures the exact same hue on every print run. The printer you choose does not change the results. If your design needs only one or two colors, Pantone (spot color) printing is your best option. This printing process is more cost effective than other standard four color processes.
