## Article

- There are two qualities that must be present in every premium box. It should definitely protect the product inside and make it look more valuable.
- A premium box should do two things well. It should protect the product inside, and it should make the product look more valuable.
- Paper-wrapped greyboard is made for exactly that purpose.
- It combines a strong greyboard at the core with a decorative paper wrap over it. The base greyboard gives the box its shape and strength. And the outer paper wrap gives a unique style to it that customers actually see.

## What Is Paper-Wrapped Greyboard?

- Paper-wrapped greyboard is a rigid packaging material made by covering thick greyboard with printed or textured paper.
- The greyboard acts as the foundation of the box. It keeps the box strong and prevents it from bending during normal use.
- The paper wrap completely covers the greyboard, giving the box a clean and polished appearance. Customers never see the greyboard itself, but they immediately notice the sturdy feel it provides.
- This is why many luxury rigid boxes are built using paper-wrapped greyboard.

## Why Not Use Plain Greyboard?

- Greyboard is strong, but it is not designed to be seen.
- Its natural gray surface looks plain and does not reflect the image most brands want to create. By wrapping it with decorative paper, manufacturers can completely change the look of the box without changing its strength.
- The inside stays the same, but the outside can match any brand.
