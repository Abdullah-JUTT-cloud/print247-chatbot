## Article

- When you pick a packaging box from the shelf, have you ever questioned yourself about why does it feel good to hold in hands? It's not the design of the box that always builds a positive perception, the material also plays a big role in this overall ordeal.
- Many of the luxury boxes we see nowadays are made from paper-wrapped chipboard. It is a thick material with a decorative paper overwrapped on it. This material gives the box its durable structure while the paper layer gives it a clean and attractive look.
- This material stock option is used by many brands because it makes the packaging look elegant without changing the strong structure of the box.

## What Is Paper-Wrapped Chipboard?

- The paper-wrapped chipboard material is exactly as its name suggests. It is a thick chipboard sheet that is additionally covered with a textured or printed paper.
- After the paper is carefully wrapped around the board, the plain chipboard is no longer visible. What customers look at is a smooth, finished box that looks neat from every side.
- The outside can be minimalist or maximalist as required.

## Why Is It Used?

- Chipboard is strong but it doesn't appear to be that much attractive.
- Wrapping it with paper completely changes the overall appearance. This makes the box a perfect medium to pack luxury products while keeping the quality structure.
- A luxury perfume brand might choose a soft-touch black paper, while a bakery might prefer a colorful printed wrap.
- The inside is similar but the external look of the box appears totally different.
