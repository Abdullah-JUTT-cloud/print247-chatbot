## Article

- Packaging can do so much more than simply providing protection to the product. It communicates to customers what a brand cares about. For plenty of businesses, this means that opting for the materials while looking good also uses resources wisely. And recycled paperboard is one of the material options.
- Made from recovered paper fibers, recycled paperboard gives brands a practical material for many types of packaging. It can be shaped, printed, and finished in different ways while helping reduce the need for new paper fibers.

## Giving Used Paper a New Purpose

- A recycled paperboard is made by processing recovered paper products into a new board. These fibers are cleaned, prepared, and formed into sheets or boards. The result is a useful packaging material that does not need to start with completely new paper fibers.
- This makes recycled paperboard more than just a material choice. It gives brands another way to think about their packaging.

## Why Do Brands Use Recycled Paperboard?

- One of its biggest advantages is its resource-conscious nature. Using recovered fibers can help reduce the demand for fresh fiber in suitable packaging applications.
- It can also be a good fit for brands that want their packaging to have a simple and eco appearance. Recycled paperboard can also be converted into different packaging shapes. With the right thickness and construction, it can provide enough support for many everyday products.

## A Material That Can Still Look Good

- Some people think recycled packaging must look plain. This is not entirely the truth.
- Recycled paperboard can be printed with every detail a brand requires including logo, name, product copy, graphics, and much more. Coatings and finishing can also be applied to change the final look.
