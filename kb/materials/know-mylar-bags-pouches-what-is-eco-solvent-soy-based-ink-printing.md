## Article

- An eco solvent or a soy based ink printing on mylar bags uses soybean oil based formula as a substitute for the petroleum based inks. It is mixed with resins, pigments, and waxes just like traditional ink. This printing uses renewable oil that makes it environmentally friendly.
- It's a top choice for brands that want to use eco friendly printing inks to print bright and crisp colors while reducing environmental waste. Let's discuss how soy based inks are made and what are its benefits.

## How is Soy Based Ink Made?

- Soy based ink production involves several stages. The process starts with cleaning soybeans and cutting them into flakes. Then it is pressed to extract their natural oils.
- The extracted oil is refined, cookies, and purified to ensure there are no impurities left. Then pigments are added to the oil to create a specific color. The process ends by adding waxes and resins to the mix to help the ink dry and stick to the paper perfectly.

## Advantages of Using Soy Based Ink

- Soy based inks offer several amazing benefits. Let's take a look at them:.
- Vibrant Color Quality: Soy based inks printing helps create sharper details and brighter colors.
- Non Toxic Ink Composition: These printing inks are made from natural ingredients that are safe for retail and consumer packaging.
- Highly Rub Resistant: Once the inks are completely absorbed into the materials, it becomes highly rub resistant. This prevents the design from smudging during handling.
- Soy based ink printing on mylar bag offers remarkable advantages. If you are storing food products in your mylar bags, then soy based ink printing is an ideal solution. It is safe for direct food contact and prevents contamination.

## Final Thoughts

- Soy based ink is the most sensible printing option as it offers a safer solution for the environment. It offers amazing graphic vibrancy and appeals to eco conscious consumers. It requires special additives and a process for mylar bag printing. However, it plays a huge role in reducing factory emissions.
