## Article

- There are a variety of products that require more than a printed paper to make an impact that gets remembered. A luxury watch, designer jewelry, or a special gift should feel premium at first physical interaction. This is where fabric-wrapped rigid board stands out as the best material when packaging needs to look exclusive as the product packed within.
- In this material, instead of covering the rigid board with printed paper, manufacturers and suppliers wrap it with fabric. This results in a box that feels soft in hand, looks elegant, and diverse from ordinary packaging. It immediately gives customers the impression that the product inside is of high value.
- Owing to its unique appearance and texture, fabric-wrapped board is usually used for high end rigid boxes where presentation is just as important as protection of the containment.

## What Is Fabric-Wrapped Rigid Board?

- As the name suggests, the fabric-wrapped grey board is a thick material that is covered with fabric.
- The core rigid material board inside provides the required strength to keep the box in shape and standing upright, while the fabric gives a premium finish to this raw stock. Different types of fabrics can be used depending on the desired look and feel brands want to give. Some fabric types provide a smooth texture to the box while others give a textured feel.
- This combination creates packaging that looks elegant and feels different from standard rigid boxes.

## Why Do Brands Choose Fabric as An Overwrap Instead of Paper?

- With the paper wraps, businesses have access to different design options, but with fabrics the result is totally different as they give a completely diverse experience.
- When customers put their hands on a fabric-covered box, they immediately notice its texture and elegant hand-held feel. This simple detail also makes the packaging look more luxurious and memorable.
- Fabric is most of the times chosen as the core element for products where the packaging needs to be kept for long terms instead of being thrown away. Many customers reuse these boxes to store jewelry, documents, watches, or other valuable items.

## A Premium Look Without Messy Designs

- The major difference between a fabric and paper overwrapped greyboard packaging is that the fabric variant doesn't rely on chic prints or bright printing to stand out.
- The appeal of fabric wrapped greyboard box comes from simple details like texture and clean edges. Even a plain fabric can give the box a sophisticated look. Like in black, gray, navy, or beige.
- For this reason, many luxury brands prefer fabric over printed paper when they want a timeless design.
