## What a Gloss Finish Really Is?

- A gloss finish is a type of coating or a treatment that produces a highly reflective and shiny, glass like appearance. The outer film is designed to have a smooth surface, which makes colors appear more vibrant and sharp. Gloss finish has the power to affect how the bag looks and feels. Here's what gloss finish is mostly used for:.
- Product visibility: Gloss surfaces make printed colors pop. This finishing option is used to make your brand logos and other design elements look clearer and more defined.
- Improve shelf visibility: Gloss lamination makes packaging look shiny, which draws customers'attention even from afar. That's how brands make sure their products get the maximum shelf visibility.
- Protects the surface: Gloss finishes also help keep printed areas safe from light and minor scratches.
- Easy cleaning: Unlike matte surfaces, gloss finish mylar bags can be easily cleaned.

## Advantages of Using Gloss Finishes on Mylar Bags

- There are several amazing benefits of using gloss finish mylar bags. Let's discuss some of the key advantages of using gloss finish:.
- Gloss finish makes printed colors look brighter and sharper.
- It gives packaging a clean and polished appearance with its shiny surface.
- Gloss lamination acts as a strong protective shield against light, moisture, and oxygen.
- Gloss finish is often considered a more budget friendly choice for high volume products.
- It enhances both brand perception and product value on shelves.
