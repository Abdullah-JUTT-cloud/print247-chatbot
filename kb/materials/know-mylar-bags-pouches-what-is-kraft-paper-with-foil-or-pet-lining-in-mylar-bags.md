## Article

- Kraft paper with a foil or PET lining is known as a laminated material and is commonly used in Mylar bags. This material is also used for flexible packaging solutions. These materials offer the natural strength and appearance of kraft paper. These custom pouches are designed to offer protective barrier properties of foil or PET film. This layered structure allows packaging to look natural and attract eco conscious consumers while also keeping products secure inside the packaging.
- The external layer is produced using kraft paper. Underneath it, a thin layer of aluminium foil or PET film is added. The inner side usually has a sealant layer such as polyethylene. This layer allows the bag to be heat sealed. This adds an extra protective layer and makes packaging even more durable, secure, and suitable for a wide range of products.

## The Material Structure and Composition of Kraft Paper Mylar Bags

- Kraft paper on its own is strong but porous. It cannot provide enough security against moisture, oxygen, or light. To further improve its performance, manufacturers like Print247 laminate it with foil or PET film. A typical structure may include:.

## Inner sealant layer for heat sealing

- Many businesses in the USA choose Kraft layers because they offer rigidity and a natural texture, which appeals to eco-conscious consumers. With the growing demand for sustainable product packaging, custom eco friendly pouches have become a top choice in the retail market. The foil or PET layer helps protect the products from external elements like moisture, light, and oxygen. The sealant layer is applied to ensure the pouch can be sealed securely during the packaging process.

## Why Foil or PET Lining Is Used

- The foil or PET lining is the key to improving the functionality of kraft mylar bags. The aluminum foil is used to provide products with a high barrier against oxygen, moisture, and light. If the products need a long shelf life or strong protection from environmental factors, then adding this layer is the best solution.
- PET lining is another option that adds strength and moderate barrier properties. PET is commonly used when the product does not require a full foil barrier but still needs protection from moisture and air.
- Both materials are used to maintain product quality by slowing down the effects of oxidation, humidity, and contamination.

## Exploring the Benefits of Using Kraft Paper in Mylar Bags

- This laminated structure offers several amazing benefits for flexible packaging. The following are the amazing benefits of using kraft paper in Mylar pouches:.
- First, it improves product protection. The barrier layer helps preserve freshness by limiting moisture and oxygen exposure.
- Second, it increases packaging strength. The combined layers make the pouch more resistant to punctures and handling during shipping or storage.
- Third, it supports good print quality. Kraft paper can be printed with branding, labels, or graphics while maintaining its natural texture.
