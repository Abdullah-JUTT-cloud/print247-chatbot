Box materials on the boxes campaign page: kraft paperboard 18-24pt,
eco-friendly retail packaging; corrugated cardboard 3-6mm single/double
wall, shipping heavy or fragile items; SBS (solid bleached sulfate)
12-18pt, cosmetic and food-grade boxes; rigid chipboard 32-80pt, premium
gift and luxury boxes. Thickness varies by material grade and box style;
the team recommends thickness from product weight and shipping
conditions after a manual spec review. Sustainability (FAQ):
biodegradable inks and recycled paper. Context note: kraft and SBS sit
inside the general 12-24pt board range, corrugated is quoted in mm, and
rigid chipboard 32-80pt sits well outside that range — keep all figures
labelled by material, never merged into one range.
