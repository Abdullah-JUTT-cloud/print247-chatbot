## Article

- When opting for the ideal paper for your packaging, you need to look beyond just colors, thickness, strength, and finish. You might also want to consider the source of the paper material. FSC-compliant paper is one option that is used by businesses that want their packaging to follow responsible sourcing standards to play a role in global safety.
- It can be used for many types of printed packaging while still giving brands plenty of room for creative designs.

## What Is FSC-Compliant Paper?

- FSC stands for the Forest Stewardship Council. And the paper stocks made according to standards set by this council are called FSC compliant papers.
- The goal is to support better practices in the supply chain, from forests to paper products. This makes FSC compliance different from simply choosing paper based on how it looks or feels.
- For packaging, the paper still needs to provide the right strength, surface, and print quality for the job.

## Why Do Brands Use This Paper?

- Many brands want their packaging choices to reflect responsible material sourcing. Using FSC compliant paper can support this goal without changing the basic function of the package.
- It can also give businesses another point to consider when planning packaging for products sold in stores or online.
- The important thing is to choose the right stock for the product. FSC compliance does not mean every paper stock has the same strength, thickness, or appearance.

## What Does It Look Like?

- There is no single look for FSC-compliant paper.
- It could either be white, natural, coated, uncoated, smooth, or textured. This gives businesses a fair share of freedom when ordering packaging for their products.
- The paper can also be printed with logos, product information, images, patterns, and other artwork. Finishing options can further change the final appearance of the box.

## Which Box Styles Can Use It?

- As this material is available in different grades, it can be made into many box styles.
- Folding cartons are a common choice because paperboard can be cut, folded, and creased into different shapes. Tuck boxes, sleeve boxes, display boxes, and other folding designs can also use suitable paper stocks.
- For heavier or more premium packaging, a compliant paper may be wrapped around a stronger board core.

## Can It Be Customized?

- Yes. This paper stock does not limit you on the creativity aspect of packaging.
- Brands can choose this paper in different colors, thicknesses, textures, and finishes. You can also custom print brand colors, artwork, product details, and other important information.
- Depending on the stock and design, options such as matte finishes, gloss finishes, foil details, and special coatings are also available.
- This makes it possible to create packaging that matches the brand while using a paper stock that meets its sourcing goals.
