## Article

- Folding Boxboard, also called or known as FBB, is made in separate layers. The middle layer is created from wood pulp, which gives the board thickness without making it too heavy.
- The outer layers, or top and bottom layers, are smoother and lighter in color, which helps with printing and overall seamless appearance.
- Because of this layered structure, FBB feels light in hand but still holds its shape well. It is not as dense as some other boards, but it offers a good balance between weight and strength.

## How It Performs in Packaging

- FBB is known for folding easily without cracking. This makes it ideal for packaging that needs clean edges and smooth creases. Boxes made from FBB open and close neatly, which improves the overall user experience.
- The outer surface is suitable for printing. Designs look clear, especially when simple colors and layouts are used. It may not be as bright as fully bleached boards, but it still provides a clean and presentable finish.
- Because it is lightweight, it also helps reduce shipping weight, which can be useful for brands handling large volumes.
