## Article

- If you're wondering what pearl UV finish really is. It is a special kind of coating that is applied directly on the Mylar pouch surface. This finish helps create a soft pearl like shine that makes the bag look eye catching.
- The pearl like shine and the UV coating make the bags long lasting and eye catching. This finishing technique is used by brands that want to make their products stored in mylar bags look eye catching and appealing.
- This finish is added after printing. This finish creates a glossy effect that reflects light. But it does not make the mylar bag look too bright. This unique finishing technique is used by brands that want their custom Mylar bags to look polished and visually appealing.

## Pearl UV Finish or Regular Gloss Finish: Which One is Better

- It is important to understand the difference between a gloss finish and a pearl UV finish. Gloss finish creates a stronger shine on the mylar packaging. This type of finishing makes the surface more reflective under retail lights.
- The pearl UV finish provides a softer glow with a smoother and more elegant appearance.
- However, the choice depends on the packaging style a brand wants. Gloss finishes work perfectly well for bold, high-shine designs. On the other hand, Pearl UV is better for packaging that needs a subtle premium look. It makes your packaging look eye catching while making it durable.

## How Does Pearl UV Finish Work?

- If you're wondering how pearl UV finish on mylar bags actually works, then you're about to find out how. Pearl UV finish works by applying a layer of UV coating over the surface of mylar pouch.
- The varnish is then passed through the UV light, which is used to dry and harden the coating on mylar surface in just a few seconds.
- The pearl effect comes from the way the coating reflects light. It does not look like a standard glossy finish. Instead, it gives the custom printed pouch a soft shimmer that changes slightly depending on the angle of viewing. This makes colors, patterns, and branding elements appear more noticeable. This helps catch customers'attention even from afar.
