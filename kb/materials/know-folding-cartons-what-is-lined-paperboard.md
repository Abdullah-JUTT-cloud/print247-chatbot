## Article

- S the name suggests, lined paperboard is a type of packaging material that is crafted using more than one layer of paper. Each layer is there for a reason and purpose to fulfil. The outer layer of the lined paperboard is usually smooth and lighter in color which allows it to be printed however required. The inner layers are thicker and help the board to stay firm and hold its shape.
- This multi-layer structure bestows lined paperboard with usefulness as well as efficiency. It doesn't have to totally rely on a single thick sheet of paperboard. Instead, it combines different layers to create a balanced material that is not too heavy and definitely not too weak.

## How It Feels and Performs

- When holding a box that is made from lined paperboard gives the feeling of lightness but still structurally strong. It has the strength to stand upright on shelves while ensuring safety of contained products during normal handling. Although lined paperboard packaging does not support packing of heavy products, it still works great for products that are easy to carry.
- The outer surface allows brands to print names, colors, and simple designs. The print may not be as sharp as premium materials, but it is clear enough for most retail needs.
- Because it folds easily, it is often used in packaging that needs to be assembled quickly. This makes it useful for large production runs.

## Where It Is Commonly Used

- Lined paperboard is used in the packaging of many everyday products. It is often seen in the food industry such as bakery boxes, takeaway cartons, and light snack packaging.
- It is also used in retail packaging for small items like accessories, toys, and personal care products. These products do not need heavy protection, so this material works well.

## Box Styles and Shapes

- This material works best with simple box styles. The options for these styles include:.
- Tuck end boxes.
- Seal end boxes.
- Window boxes.
- Sleeve boxes.
- And more.

## These designs are easy to produce and easy to use

- Shapes are usually square or rectangular because they are easy to stack and store. This helps both brands and retailers handle packaging more efficiently.

## Final Perspective

- Lined paperboard is a practical material that focuses on balance. It gives enough strength for everyday use while still allowing basic printing and design.
