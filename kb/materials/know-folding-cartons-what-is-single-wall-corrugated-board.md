## Article

- Single wall corrugated board is a packaging material that is built for strength and provides protection to the products. It is very different from a flat paperboard because it is not just one solid sheet. Instead, it is made using three layers.
- There are two flat outer sheets, called liners, and one wavy layer in the middle. This wavy layer is called flutes that absorbs shipping pressures and protects the product inside from damages.

## Why It Is Used in Packaging

- It is easy to customize as per the diverse packaging needs of businesses. It can be cut into different sizes, printed with branding, and shaped into various box styles.
- Another plus point of this stock is its recyclability and sustainability. That's why plenty of businesses prefer it.
- It also works well during the stacking process. Boxes made from single wall corrugated board can bear weight without losing their shape.

## Plenty of Use Cases

- Single wall corrugated boards are used in many industries because of their reliability.
- It is commonly used in shipping boxes and mailer boxes for e-commerce and retail products. Whether its packaging for clothing, electronics, or small appliances, this material can help to protect the packed items during delivery.
- It is also used in food packaging for items like pizza boxes and takeaway packaging.

## Box Styles That Work Well With This Material

- This material is used in many practical box styles.
- One of the most common is the regular slotted container, which is the standard shipping box with flaps on top and bottom.
- It is also used in mailer boxes, which are popular for online businesses because they are easy to open and close.
- Die-cut boxes are another option, where the shape is customized for specific products.

## Various Flute Options for Single Wall Corrugated Board

- There are plenty of flute types that can be used to form a single wall corrugated board. The difference in each type is the size. It ultimately affects how the material performs.
- For example, E flute is thinner and smoother, which is good for printing and retail packaging.
- B flute is slightly thicker and offers better strength for shipping.
- C flute is even thicker and provides more cushioning for heavier items.
