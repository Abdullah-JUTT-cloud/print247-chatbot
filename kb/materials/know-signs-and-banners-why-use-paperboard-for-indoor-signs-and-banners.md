## Article

- Paperboard is the most favorite choice for indoor advertising. It is used for signs and banners and is considered the perfect choice for various advertising campaigns. Paperboard is both cost effective and a sustainable choice. It is lightweight, which makes it a perfect choice for large batches of signs. Paperboard signs and banners are a perfect alternative to plastic signs.
- Many brands these days are shifting towards sustainable and eco conscious marketing alternatives. Signs and banners made from paperboard deliver visual impact and meet strict environmental standards. They are perfect for indoor usage, as environmental factors such as UV rays or rain can ruin the paper quickly. Let's discuss why brands use paperboard for indoor signs and banners.

## Why Paperboard is an Ideal Choice for Indoor Advertising

- Paperboard is a perfect choice for indoor advertising. There are plenty of other reasons why brands choose paperboard for indoor signs and banners. It's an affordable choice and is more rigid than traditional plastic signs.
- Paperboard is 100% recyclable, biodegradable, and sourced from responsibly managed forests. This material is FSC certified and complies with strict environmental standards.
- However, paperboard offers a smooth surface for printing. Banners and signs made using paperboard materials deliver high visual impact for retail environments.

## What Are the Benefits of Using Paperboard Signs and Banners

- Paperboard signs and banners offer several amazing benefits. Let's discuss them in detail:.
- They offer a smooth surface that allows you to print sharp colors, detailed graphics, and clear text using different printing methods.
- They are pretty easy to cut, crease, and fold.
- They are budget friendly and are considered an ideal choice for short term promotional campaigns. They help lower shopping costs due to their lightweight.
- These signs and banners eliminate the need to use plastic banners or signs for short term advertising purposes.
- Paperboard signs and banners help reduce the carbon footprint. They easily fit into standard paper recycling streams.
- They are easy to carry, hang, and move around the retail environment.

## Where Paperboard Signage Are Used

- Paperboard signs and banners are used for short term promotional purposes. Let's discuss where they are used:.
- Retail stores use them for short term promotional signs. They are also used as hanging signs in aisles and shelf talkers.
- They are also used at events and trade shows as table signs, hanging ceiling banners, or directional boards.
- They are also used for window displays to advertise graphics and campaigns.

## Final Verdicts

- These paper based rigid signs and banners are a perfect choice for indoor advertising. They also work perfectly well for posters and POP displays.
