## Article

- Not every product can be shipped in a standard corrugated box. Some products are larger, heavier, more fragile, or simply more valuable than average. These products need packaging that can withstand stacking pressure, long shipping routes, warehouse storage, and rough handling without losing its shape and compromising product safety.
- This is where BC Flute corrugated board becomes an ideal solution for shipping boxes.
- BC Flute is a double-wall corrugated structure made by combining two different flute profiles into one board. Instead of relying on a single fluted layer, it uses two layers working together to create a stronger and more durable packaging material.
- Because of this reinforced structure, BC Flute has become one of the most trusted materials for heavy-duty packaging applications.

## Understanding How BC Flute Is Constructed

- BC Flute combines B-Flute and C-Flute within the same board structure.
- The B-Flute layer contributes crush resistance and structural stability.
- The C-Flute layer provides cushioning and shock absorption.
- When these two flute types are combined, the result is a material that offers benefits from both profiles.
- This combination creates a thicker double wall board that is suitable to handle greater loads than standard single-wall corrugated materials.
- The additional layer also increases durability during transportation and storage.

## Why Businesses Prefer BC Flute for Shipping

- The primary reason businesses use BC Flute is the strength and durability it offers.
- Products usually travel through complex supply chains involving warehouses, trucks, shipping centers, and retail distribution facilities.
- Throughout this journey, packaging could be stacked under heavy loads, moved repeatedly, and exposed to pressure from multiple directions.
- A weaker box may bend, crush, or fail under these conditions.
- BC Flute helps reduce these risks by providing enhanced structural support.
- For companies shipping high-volume orders or large products, this added strength can significantly reduce damage rates.

## A Reliable Choice for Warehouse Storage

- Warehouses place unique demands on packaging.
- Products may remain stacked for extended periods before distribution.
- If packaging lacks stacking strength, boxes can collapse and damage inventory.
- BC Flute performs exceptionally well in these environments because its double-wall structure helps maintain shape under sustained pressure.
- This makes it a common choice for businesses managing large inventories or bulk storage operations.
