Finishes include matte lamination, gloss lamination, soft-touch
lamination, spot UV, foil stamping, gold foil, copper foil,
rose-gold foil, holographic foil printing, embossing, drip-off
and AQ coating.

Stand-up pouch add-ons: gloss, matte and soft-touch laminations,
spot UV, foil printing, holographic foil printing.

Printing methods: CMYK plus Pantone/PMS matching, offset, digital
and flexographic for food. Finishes are a separate slot from
material — "matte" alone does not answer the material question.
