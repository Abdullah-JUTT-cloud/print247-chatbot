## Article

- Solid Bleached Sulfate, also known as SBS paperboard, is made from fresh wood fibers that are cleaned and processed until they become bright white. This gives the material a smooth and even surface from the very beginning.
- Because of this, SBS does not look rough or uneven like some other materials. It feels clean, solid, and well-finished. This is why many brands use it when they want their packaging to look neat and professional.
- At the same time, it is not just about looks. SBS is also strong enough to hold its shape and protect products during handling. So it offers a balance between appearance and performance.

## Why Brands Prefer SBS for Printing and Design

- One of the biggest advantages of SBS is how well it handles the printing process. The smooth white surface allows colors to appear bright and accurate. Even small text and fine details remain clear and easy to read.
- This is important for packaging that includes instructions, ingredients, or branding elements. Nothing looks faded or unclear. Everything stays sharp.
- SBS also works well with different finishes. Brands can add coatings like gloss for shine or matte for a softer look. These finishes not only improve appearance but also add a layer of protection.
- Because of this flexibility, the same material can be used for both simple packaging and more premium designs.

## Common Box Styles Made with SBS

- SBS paperboard is easy to cut and fold, which makes it suitable for many packaging styles. Some of the most common ones include:.
- Tuck End Boxes - Used for products like cosmetics, medicines, and small retail items. They are easy to open and close.
- Sleeve Boxes - Often used for premium products where a sliding cover adds a better unboxing experience.
- Window Boxes - A small transparent window can be added so customers can see the product inside.
- Display Boxes - Used in retail stores to present products clearly on shelves or counters.
- These styles work well with SBS because the material holds sharp edges and clean folds, which improves the overall look of the box.

## Where SBS Paperboard Is Commonly Used

- SBS is widely used in industries where packaging needs to look clean and trustworthy.
- In cosmetics and skincare, it helps products look fresh and premium.
- In pharmaceutical packaging, it ensures that text is clear and easy to read, which is very important for safety.
- In food packaging, especially for dry or frozen items, it can be coated to make it safe while still keeping a neat look.
- It is also used in retail packaging, where strong branding and presentation help products stand out on shelves.
