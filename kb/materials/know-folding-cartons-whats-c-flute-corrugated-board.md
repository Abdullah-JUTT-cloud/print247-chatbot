## Article

- If there is one corrugated material that can be called the workhorse of the packaging industry, it is C-Flute corrugated board.
- From e-commerce deliveries and retail shipments to industrial packaging and warehouse storage, C-Flute is used almost everywhere. Many of the corrugated boxes people receive at home every day are made from this flute profile.
- Some flute types are designed primarily for presentation, while others focus heavily on protection. C-Flute sits comfortably in the middle, making the material choice a suitable option for a variety of packaging applications.
- This versatile use case has made it famous and one of the most trusted materials in modern packaging.

## Understanding the Structure of C-Flute Corrugated Board

- To understand why C-Flute performs so well, it helps to look at its structure.
- Like other corrugated materials, C-Flute consists of liner sheets with a fluted medium placed between them.
- However, the flute size is larger than B-Flute and significantly larger than microflute options such as E-Flute and F-Flute.
- At the same time, the board remains strong enough to maintain its shape under stacking pressure.
- This combination of cushioning and structural support is what makes C-Flute such a reliable packaging material.
