## Article

- Roll stock is a simple, flexible packaging material that is delivered in the form of a continuous sheet. It doesn't come in premade bags or pouches. It is basically designed to be used with form-fill-seal machines. These devices shape the packaging into pouches, fill them with product, and close them in one steady procedure.
- This type of packaging is widely used in pharmaceutical, medical, food, and consumer goods industries where speed and consistency matter. Today, we will discuss everything you need to know about what roll stock packaging is and why it's so famous.

## How Roll Stock Packaging Works

- Roll stock comes as a large roll of printed or plain flexible film. The film is loaded onto a form-fill-seal (FFS) machine. This is an automated machine that converts a plain roll of film into a closed pouch. As the machine runs, it pulls the film, forms it into the desired shape, fills it with the product, and seals it. The final result can be custom pouches, sachets, stick packs, or flow wraps, depending on the machine and application.
- The following are three common machine types used with roll stock:.

## Vertical form-fill-seal (VFFS)

- Horizontal form-fill-seal (HFFS).

## Thermoform fill seal (TFFS)

- Each setup determines the final package shape and use.

## Types of Materials Used in Roll Stock Packaging

- Roll stock packaging is made using various packaging materials. They are combined with alumium foil for superior product protection according to the product's requirements. Below are the materials used to make roll stock packaging:.
- Plastic films like polyethylene (PE) and polypropylene (PP).
- Aluminum foil for higher level product protection.
- Polyester (PET).

## Paper-based lamination for a natural look

- This multi-layer structure of roll stock packaging is often used to provide barrier protection against moisture, oxygen, light, and contaminants. This packaging solution is highly effective for high volume production in pharma, food, and other industries.

## Exploring the Benefits of Roll Stock Packaging

- Roll stock packaging offers many amazing benefits, and among them is efficiency. Since packaging is formed during production, it reduces handling time and increases output speed.
- The following are the key benefits you get from roll stock packaging:.
- Since packaging is created on demand, it lowers material waste.
- Rolls take up less storage space and reduce shipping and storage expenses.
- It can be customized according to exact size and shape needs.
- They are designed for high speed and continuous production machines, which lowers labor costs.
- It is a cost effective packaging solution as compared to pre-made bags or pouches.
- Roll stock also allows high quality printing, which is another advantage for brands as they can print clear branding and product information.

## Conclusion

- Roll stock packaging is a flexible, machine ready packaging solution that is used to create pouches during the filling process.
