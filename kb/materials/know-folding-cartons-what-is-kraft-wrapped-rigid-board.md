## Article

- Not every brand wants packaging that looks shiny or highly polished. Many businesses now prefer a more natural style. They want their boxes to feel simple, warm, and closer to the materials used inside them.
- Kraft wrapped rigid board fits this style very well.
- It combines a rigid board core with a kraft paper outer layer. The board gives the box its strength, while the kraft wrap gives it a natural and earthy appearance.
- This makes it different from the glossy and matte finishes used on rigid boxes. Kraft wrapped board is opted by brands that want their packaging to feel honest, simple, and environmentally safe.

## What Is This Material?

- A Kraft wrapped board is a rigid packaging board covered with kraft paper.
- The rigid base in this material keeps the box stand firm and holds its shape. And the kraft paper on the outside gives a regular brown outlook. This can also be dyed into white. Brands can also print their logos, product details, patterns, or other artwork onto the surface.
- So while the structure stays strong, the outside can be designed to match the brand.

## Why Choose a Kraft Finish?

- The biggest reason is appearance.
- Kraft paper has a natural texture to it that can make packaging feel more genuine and less processed. It works well for brands that want minimalist design rather than a decorated box.
- Like, a small handmade soap company may prefer a kraft finish because it fits the natural image of its products. A coffee brand may use it to create a warm and familiar feeling.

## Rigid Box Styles That Can Use Kraft Wrapped Board

- The kraft wrap can be applied to several rigid box styles.

## Magnetic Closure Boxes

- A kraft finish gives magnetic closure boxes a softer and more natural appearance while the rigid core provides the needed structure.

## Drawer Boxes

- The simple kraft exterior of sleeve boxes works well for products where the packaging needs to feel practical yet premium.

## Two-Piece Rigid Boxes

- These rigid boxes in two piece style are useful for gift sets and products that need a clean, classic presentation.

## Setup Boxes

- Kraft wrapping can give these ready-to-use boxes a more natural personality.

## Custom Gift Boxes

- Brands can combine kraft wrapping with printed artwork, labels, or simple branding for a personalized look for gift packaging.
