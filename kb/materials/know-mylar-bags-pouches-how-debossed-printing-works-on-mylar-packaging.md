## Article

- Debossed printing is a printing technique where the design is pressed into the surface of mylar packaging to create a sunken effect. Mylar bag printing method uses pressure and heat to push the material inward, forming a permanent recessed impression. Debossing creates a premium tactile feel on mylar packaging.

## How the Process Actually Works

- Debossing on custom pouches is done using a custom made metal die. This die is engraved with the design. This design appears on the packaging in the recessed form. During the production, the mylar material is placed between the metal die and a counter surface. Heat and pressure are applied simultaneously, forcing teh design into the surface of custom printed mylar bags.
- It is important to know that pressure needs to be controlled carefully. Too much force can damage the structure, while too little force won't create a clear impression. Print247 specializes in debossed mylar bag printing. They carefully adjust temperature and pressure based on the thickness and composition of the pouch.

## Where Debossing Is Applied on Mylar Bags

- Debossed elements are mostly added to specific design areas. It is mostly applied to logos, product titles, design patterns, and brand names. This printing method is mostly used on matte finish pouches. Matte finish pouches make the recessed effect more visible and easier to feel. Many other printing methods are also used. In such cases, the design is printed first, and debossing is applied afterwards.

## Conclusion

- Not all mylar bags are considered suitable for deep debossing. Thin materials will not be able to show the impression more clearly. Thin materials make highly detailed designs to lose clarity when pressed into flexible packaging like Mylar bags.
