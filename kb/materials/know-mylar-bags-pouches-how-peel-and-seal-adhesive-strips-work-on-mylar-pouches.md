## Article

- Peel-and-seal strip is a self adhesive closure solution which is widely used on mylar pouches for a secure and user friendly sealing. This type of closure solution allows bags to be sealed without using any heat sealing device or any other additional tools. The strip is mostly placed near the opening of the pouches. It uses a pressure sensitive adhesive to close the package securely.
- This type of closure solution is used for packaging that needs quick sealing or can be reopened easily without any extra effort. It is mostly used for lightweight product packaging and mailing style pouches.
- The strip is placed along the inside flap of the pouch opening as a narrow adhesive line. It is covered with a protective liner to make sure it stays clean before its first use. Once the liner is removed, the adhesive becomes active, and the pouch will be ready to be sealed.

## How does the Sealing Process Work?

- The sealing process is very simple. Once the product is placed inside the pouch, the user can remove the protective liner that covers the adhesive strip. Once the liner is peeled off, the user must fold down the top flap of the pouch over the adhesive strip.
- Apply a light pressure to make sure the adhesive bonds to the surface of the pouch, forming a secure seal. The seal allows the pouch to be closed securely without needing any heat sealing equipment.

## Where Peel and Seal Closures Are Used

- Peel and seal adhesive strips are mostly used for packaging items that require a quick closing method. This closure solution is often found on flat pouches, sample bags, custom poly mailers, and lightweight product packaging.
- This closure solution is often found in products that need to be packed and closed manually, and where packaging speed matters.
