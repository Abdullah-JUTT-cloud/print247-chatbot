## Article

- Solid Unbleached Sulfate, also called SUS paperboard, is made from wood fibers that are not bleached. Because of this, the material keeps its natural brown color and stronger fiber structure.
- This makes SUS tougher than many other paperboards. It does not focus on a bright or polished look. Instead, it focuses on strength and durability.
- When you hold SUS packaging, it feels firm and solid. This makes it a good choice for products that need extra protection.

## How It Looks and Feels in Packaging

- SUS has a natural, earthy appearance. The surface is not as smooth or bright as white paperboard, but it gives a strong and honest look.
- Brands that want a simple or eco-friendly feel often prefer this material. The natural color can also be printed on, although designs usually work better when they are bold and not too detailed.
- Because the material is strong, it holds its shape well. Boxes made from SUS feel stable and reliable during handling and shipping.

## Where Strength Matters Most

- SUS is often chosen when packaging needs to handle weight or rough use. It performs well in situations where boxes are stacked, moved, or transported over long distances.

## It is commonly used for:

- Food packaging, especially for items like beverages or frozen products that need a sturdy outer layer.
- Beverage carriers, where strength is important for holding multiple items.

## Shapes and Practical Design Use

- SUS is usually used in simple and practical shapes. Straight edges and firm corners help maintain strength and make stacking easier.
- While it can be shaped into custom designs, it is most effective when used in structures that support weight and stability. This makes it ideal for packaging that needs to be both strong and easy to handle.

## Final Perspective

- Solid Unbleached Sulfate paperboard is all about strength, reliability, and simplicity. It may not have the bright and smooth look of bleached materials, but it offers better durability and a natural appearance.
