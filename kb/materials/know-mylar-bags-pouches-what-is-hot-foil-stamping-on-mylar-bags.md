## Article

- Hot foil stamping is a sort of printing process that is mostly used to add a lustrous metallic design to text, logos, or specific design patterns. This printing process gives your retail products an edge over competitors. This printing procedure uses heat and pressure to transfer a thin layer of foil onto the mylar packaging surface.
- The final result is a reflective finish that immediately makes your products stand out. This also gives your packaging a premium look that attracts attention.
- This printing option is commonly used on food pouches, cosmetic bags, coffee packaging, supplement pouches, and luxury retail packaging.

## How the Hot Foil Stamping Process Works

- In hot foil stamping, a metal die is warmed and pressed against the foil and packaging material. The heat helps the foil stick only to the selected design area.
