## Article

- UV printing, as the name suggests, is a modern printing technique that uses ultraviolet light to dry or cure specially formulated inks directly on the printing surface. They work phenomenally well on mylar bags. This printing method creates vibrant and highly durable designs on mylar bags. This printing method relies on heat and air drying to harden the ink on the surface of mylar bags.
- UV printing tends to cure in an instant and prevent smudging, making it a perfect choice for printing on mylar bags. Today, we will discuss how UV printing works on mylar bags and its benefits.

## How UV Printing Works on Mylar Bags?

- UV printing uses a fast process to transfer and cure ink directly onto the surface. Let's see how it works on mylar bags:.
- The process starts by preparing the design. First, you have to upload the design to the UV printer's software and adjust its dimensions according to the product's specific requirements.
- The printer sends UV curable inks onto the mylar surface.
- As the ink is applied on the mylar surface, a UV light is passed over it to instantly cure the ink. This solidifies the ink onto the flexible mylar surface.
- The finished printing results are double checked for color accuracy, durability, and sharpness.

## Exploring the Benefits of UV Printing

- Let's discuss the amazing benefits of using UV printing:.
- Highly Durable: UV printing method hardens the ink on the surface of mylar bags. This makes them water resistant and won't scratch or fade under any condition.
- Improve Product's Shelf Appeal: UV printing creates a glass like shine and a raised texture, which makes logos stand out and adds a touch of luxury to the packaging.
- Reduce production time: The ink dries/cures instantly, which helps reduce production time.
