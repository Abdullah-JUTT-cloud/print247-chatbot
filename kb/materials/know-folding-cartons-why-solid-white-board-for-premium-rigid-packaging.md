## Article

- When buying a premium product, customers expect its packaging to also be of top quality. And a well designed rigid box has the potential to make any containment feel premium and full of quality. The basic material for these boxes is greyboard which is then wrapped with decorative paper, however, some businesses choose solid white board. This selection lets them achieve a cleaner and more refined appearance.
- Solid white board is a rigid paperboard material with a bright white surface. It offers the same sturdy construction expected from luxury packaging while providing a clean base for printing, branding, and decorative finishes. This mixture of strength and visual appeal makes this material a go-to choice option for businesses that want packaging to look modern yet elegant.
