## Article

- A raised coating is also known as 3D UV coating. It's a premium finishing technique that applies a clear and thick layer of high gloss varnish onto the selected areas of custom printed mylar bags. It is cured instantly under ultraviolet light. This helps harden the coating and create a raised texture on the material. It can easily be felt upon touch.
- It's not like regular UV coating. Raised UV is thicker, adds height, and dimension to the specific design elements. It is mostly used for specific design patterns like text, logos, and graphics.
- This finishing technique can be felt slightly elevated upon touch. It also reflects light when it comes in direct contact with it. This helps make products stand out crowded retail shelves. 3D raised UV coating is often applied after the artwork is printed on the mylar surface.
- It is precise and placed only on chosen parts of the design. It adds a premium touch to the packaging and attracts attention even from afar.

## How Raised UV Works on Mylar Bags?

- The process begins once the mylar pouch is fully printed according to your specific requirements. Then, a special clear UV varnish is applied to selected areas like logos, text, or specific design elements. The UV varnish is applied using a digital or screen process.
- Then the bag passes under UV lamps. The UV light instantly cures and hardens the coating.
- The raised pr 3D UV coating is thick which help create a slightly raised texture. This gives a 3D effect without changing the structure of the pouch. The flexibility and barrier properties of the Mylar material stay the same.
