## Article

- A glitter finish on a mylar bag offers a decorative surface that gives the packaging a shimmery, sparkling, and textured look. This lamination makes the products look premium and luxurious. Businesses use this finishing option to enhance the visual appeal of their products by creating a dazzling, high impact sparkle that grabs attention fast. The printed glitter effect is created using high resolution graphics and metallic inks. This finishing is applied to the outside surface only.

## How the Glitter Effect is Applied to Mylar Bags

- The glitter finishes are mostly applied using a specialized printing technique or hot stamping/foiling during the printing or lamination stage of production on Mylar bags. Here's how the glitter finish is applied to Mylar bags:.
- First of all, the base Mylar material is prepared.
- The glitter pattern is printed using metallic or reflective inks.
- Many suppliers also use a pre made glitter film which is laminated onto the outer surface.
- At the end of this process, a protective layer is applied to protect the finish.
- When lamination is applied to the glitter film, it becomes part of the outer layer. In any case, the structure of the Mylar bag stays the same. Glitter finished mylar bags will still be able to offer you the same level of strength and barrier properties, ensuring your products remain safe from light, moisture, and oxygen.
