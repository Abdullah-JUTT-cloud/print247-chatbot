## Article

- CMYK printing is a standard printing method. This printing method is widely used to print full color designs on packaging such as mylar bags and other flexible pouches. It is based on four different color processes that build images using Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, and Key (Black).
- This printing method mixes the inks to create a wide range of colors in different proportions CMYK printing is mostly used in packaging because it helps produce high quality visuals especially in flexible Mylar packaging.

## What Is CMYK Printing?

- CMYK is a color model which is used in printing. CMYK basically stands for Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, and Key (Black). It's a subtractive color model for printing, that uses microscopic layers of these inks coated on the material.
- It helps reproduce unique images and designs in full color. It does not use separate ink for every color instead it mixes all four inks to create different shades.
- CMYK is a printing method used to handle complex designs such as photographic images, and gradients perfectly well. Remember that the colors are created by absorbing light. They do not emit light.

## How CMYK Printing Works on Mylar Bags

- CMYK printing on Mylar works by layering transparent CMYK inks using a digital process. All these inks create a full spectrum of colors. Mylar offers a smooth surface for printing.
- The design is separated into four color channels. Then these channels are printed one by one. These layers create the final full color design on the mylar pouch when these colors are combined.
- Mylar film offers a non porous surface which is why the ink is cured or dried using UV curing or heat to offer durability. This helps ensure that the design sticks perfectly on the mylar surface.
