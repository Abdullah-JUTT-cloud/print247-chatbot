## Article

- A triplex board is similar to a duplex board, but it includes one additional layer that increases its durability. This extra layer also improves overall quality and the handheld feel. Consequently, packaging made from triplex board is more durable and premium than duplex.
- Just like duplex boards, the triplex has a smooth outer surface that is very much suitable for printing. The inner layers are thicker, which helps the box feel more solid.

## What Does the Three-Layer Structure of a Triplex Board Do?

- Because of the added layer, the triplex board can handle more pressure and support the box structure. It does not bend easily and gives better support and protection to the product packed inside.
- The printing surface on the triplex board is slightly better than that of the duplex paperboard. Colors appear to be clearer and more refined, and the overall look feels more chic compared to lower-grade boards. This gives you the opportunity to print your branding details like logos, colors, and product details.
- This versatility and freedom of custom printing make a triplex board an excellent choice for packaging. It enhances the visual appeal of both the packaging and the product at a glance while remaining budget-friendly.

## When Should You Use a Triplex Board?

- Most of the time, a triplex board is used for creating food packaging, cosmetics packaging, and retail boxes that require strength and clean look at the same time.
- It can also work well for products that need a box with a solid structure. The thickness of the triplex board should be selected according to the product's weight, size, handling, and protection needs.

## Box Styles and Shapes

- It works well with styles like auto bottom boxes, tuck end cartons, and structured retail boxes. These styles require extra support to hold moderately weighted products, which a triplex board provides reliably.
- For the box shapes, as a business you have the flexibility to go for simple as well as complex ones for your triplex board boxes.
