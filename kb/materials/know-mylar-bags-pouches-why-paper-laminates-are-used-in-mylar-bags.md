- Paper Laminates are used in Mylar bags to provide a natural appearance of paper with the strong protective properties of flexible Mylar films. They are designed to combine both sustainable and premium aesthetics, improving the product's shelf presence.
- The packaging structure includes bonding a layer of paper with materials such as polyethylene (PE), polyester (PET), or foil, which helps make packaging durable and functional. They offer excellent barrier properties and are used across multiple industries to make products look premium and keep them safe from light, moisture, and oxygen. They are designed to prevent external contamination. Today, we will discuss what paper laminates really are and why they are used in Mylar bags.

## What Are Paper Laminates?

- Paper laminates are packaging materials made by attaching paper to one or more layers of plastic film or foil. The paper layer offers a natural texture and printable surface, while the additional layers improve the overall functionality of the packaging.
- This combination creates packaging that is more durable than plain paper and more visually appealing than standard plastic pouches.

## Why Are Paper Laminates Used in Mylar Bags?

- A paper on its own does not provide enough protection for products that need a longer shelf life. Paper alone can absorb moisture, tear easily, and lose its shape during storage and transportation.
