## Article

- When you pick up two packaging boxes that have the same look, you might notice that one of them is lighter in weight and has a flexible structure while the second one has a solid and more durable feel to it. Even before you open them to check the product, you will naturally and psychologically make a perception that the heavy box must contain a quality product.
- That feeling is not created by printing or decoration alone. It comes from the material hidden inside the box.
- One of the materials responsible for that solid feel is hardboard.
- Not externally but hardboard works as the internal structure that gives rigid boxes their strength and stable built. It allows luxury packaging to protect products while creating a premium experience for the customer.

## Why Does Packaging Need Hardboard?

- Some products deserve more than a standard folding carton.
- A perfume bottle, luxury watch, premium skincare set, or corporate gift need a packaging that doesn't bend or lose its shape as it can reduce the value of the product inside.
- Hardboard solves this problem by creating a box that stays firm from production to delivery.

## Hardboard Offers More than Protection

- Apart from security, this material allows designers to create packaging with features that would be difficult to achieve using thinner materials.
- Hardboard is commonly used to make:.
- Magnetic closure boxes.
- Drawer or slide boxes.
- Two-piece rigid boxes.
