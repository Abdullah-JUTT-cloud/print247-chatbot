## Article

- Screen printing is a type of printing procedure that pushes ink through a mesh screen onto a surface to produce a unique design. For mylar bags and pouches, this printing process is used to add logos, text, patterns, and simple artwork directly onto the packaging surface.
- It's nothing like digital printing. Digital printing applies ink through a digital process. Screen printing is a famous and versatile printing process that employs a set screen for each design. It performs extremely well for bold graphics, solid colors, and designs that require strong visibility on flexible packaging.

## How Does Screen Printing Actually Work on Flexible Pouches?

- First of all, a template of the design is created. Then the screen printing process starts by making a stencil of the chosen design on a mesh screen. The areas that need printing remain open, while the rest of the screen stops the ink.
- It is important to prepare the mylar surface properly to ensure the inks stick well. During the printing process, the screen is placed over the mylar bag or pouch. Ink is applied on top of the screen and distributed using a device called a squeegee. The pressure pushes the ink via the open areas of the screen and transfers the design onto the bag.
- The final steps involve drying the ink and curing it on the mylar surface to ensure it sticks. That way, the design binds with the surface. Screen printing offers a clean and durable print that can handle regular handling and packaging use.

## Exploring the Benefits of Screen Printing on Mylar Pouches

- There are many amazing benefits of using screen printing on Mylar pouches.
- One of the primary benefits of screen printing is its capability to make robust and visually appealing designs. The ink layer is usually thicker compared to the other printing methods which colors to stand out more on the mylar packaging.
- Screen printing also works well for designs with fewer colors. This allows businesses to print specific colors accurately without needing complex setups.
- Durability is another advantage screen printing method offers. A properly cured, screen-printed design can resist everyday handling. This makes them eligible for packaging that needs to keep its appearance during storage and transportation.
