## Article

- When you pick up a high-end product box, do you notice how smooth and velvety it feels? It is this premium feel that makes soft touch lamination so popular among high-end businesses. Unlike standard glossy boxes, this special packaging finish gives the product a rich, matte appearance and a soft feel. With this unique coating, retail brands and cosmetic companies can turn standard packaging into a high-value physical asset. The retail shelf is easily a target for customer attention.

## What is Soft Touch Lamination?

- A soft touch lamination is a special finishing applied to paper-based packaging products. There is a slight layer of plastic film that bonds securely to the printed paper or cardboard. The texture is a silky, velvety texture that feels like velvet or fine leather. The product removes harsh shine and adds serious durability.

## 5 Reasons Why Luxury Brands Choose Soft Touch Lamination

- High-end retailers and businesses want their products to stand out. Here are five reasons why premium brands choose soft touch lamination for their custom packaging.

## Creating a Multisensory Brand Experience

- A customer uses more than just their eyes when they hold your product. They judge the item's quality with their hands. Regular packaging only gives them something to look at. Soft touch lamination changes the game by adding a highly physical element. As soon as buyers touch the smooth box, they feel the luxury right away. This physical interaction makes your custom packaging memorable. When multiple senses work together, your business leaves a much stronger mark on the buyer.

## Enhancing Perceived Value and Premium Positioning

- Businesses strive to sell goods at a higher price point. It is important that the outside of the box matches the quality of the contents inside. The soft touch lamination makes standard cardboard look extremely expensive. Due to the premium packaging, shoppers automatically assume the product inside is more expensive. This simple upgrade allows your company to stand out from the competition. You have perfectly justified your premium pricing strategy.

## Minimizing Visual Glare for Sophisticated Aesthetics

- The brightness of store bulbs reflects too much light off shiny boxes. The glare makes it difficult for shoppers to read details about your product or brand name. Soft Touch Lamination eliminates this problem by providing a deep matte finish. The material absorbs harsh light instead of reflecting it. It will look richer and more professional on a retail shelf when your custom printed colors are used. It is easy to read your text from any angle because it is crisp and clear. As a result, your brand appears very clean and modern.

## Providing Superior Resistance to Fingerprints and Scuffs

- When fingerprints or small scratches accumulate on dark packaging boxes, they look great, but they don't last long. The packages you ship and stock undergo a lot of physical handling during shipping and storage. The soft touch finishing provides an excellent shield of protection over your printed designs. This product resists oily fingerprints and scuff marks naturally. You want the box to look brand new after your customer takes it off the store shelf. It keeps your brand image looking sharp and professional at all times.

## Establishing an Immediate Emotional Connection with Consumers

- The sense of touch is extremely powerful in the human body. When people feel comforting textures, their brains respond quickly to them. There is something warm and inviting about Soft Touch Lamination's velvety surface. A longer hold makes the buyer want to keep the box. Your product is more likely to sell to someone who holds your package for a longer period of time. Brand loyalty is quickly built with this positive emotional reaction. Shopping becomes a memorable and highly premium experience when it is done this way.
