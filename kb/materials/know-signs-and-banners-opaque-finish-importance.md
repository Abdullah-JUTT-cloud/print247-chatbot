- An opaque finish means no light can pass through your sign. This dark block of light keeps your message easy to read. Sun glare will not ruin the bright colors on your banner. It hides any shadows that might show up from the back.

## How Do Opaque Banners Help With Double Sided Printed Sign Displays?

- People often ask how to print on both sides of banners. You need a dark center layer to stop the ink bleeding. Without it, the back design will show through to the front. That makes the whole sign look messy and hard to read.
- An opaque finish is intrinsic for banners for the purpose of maximum visibility yet making it more presentable. The durability to prevent light from passing through the material. It eliminates the show-through where shadows support poles or the background lights making text and images unreadable.
- An opaque finish stops this problem before it can even start. Your front graphics stay clean and your back graphics stay clear. This is why businesses love this choice for their outdoor signs.

- Yes, these heavy materials hold up very well in the rain. They do not tear easily when the wind blows really hard. The thick vinyl stops the hot sun from fading the ink. This means your sign will last a long time right outside.
- You will save money because you replace your signs less often. The smooth feel of the banner also helps keep dirt away. You just wipe it clean with a simple wet soft cloth.

A matte opaque banner is the top pick for outdoor use. Opaque finish remains a sought-after choice in signs and banners for the best outlook.
