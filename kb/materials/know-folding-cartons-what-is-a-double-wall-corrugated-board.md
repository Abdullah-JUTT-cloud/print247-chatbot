## Article

- A standard packaging is not suitable to pack heavy and fragile products. Because a thin or single layered box does not retain its structure at all times during lifting and stacking. It needs something more durable to ensure the shipment remains secure throughout the shipping process. There is one choice to make the shipping boxes perfect for this kind of fragile and heavy products. And that is a double wall corrugated board.
- Double wall corrugated board is made with five layers instead of regular three layers of a single corrugated sheet. Meaning, it has two single corrugated sheets attached with an additional inner lining sheet. This double fluted structure creates a much stronger material compared to a single one.
- As a result, the packaging made from this double sheet stock can handle more pressure during shipping and handling while bearing more weight without losing shape.

## How the Double Layer Structure Improves Strength

- In a regular corrugated box, there is only one wavy layer that absorbs shock. Whereas in a double wall construction, there are two of these layers working together.
- This creates extra cushioning and improves the overall durability of the box.
- This matters a lot during transportation. Boxes are often moved through warehouses, trucks, and delivery centers. They may be stacked under other heavy boxes or handled many times before reaching the customer.
- Double wall corrugated board helps reduce the risk of damage during all of these stages.

## Why Businesses Choose Double Wall Corrugated Board

- The main reason businesses use this material is certainty of protection.
- Products that are large, heavy, or delicate need packaging that can bear pressure and frantic movements. A weak box can lead to damaged products and unhappy customers.
- Double wall corrugated board gives businesses more confidence during shipping because the structure is built to handle tougher conditions.
- It is also useful for products that stay in storage for long periods. The stronger walls help the box keep its shape even when stacked in warehouses.
- Another reason is cost efficiency in the long run. While the material costs more than single wall board, it can reduce product damage and replacement costs.

## Where It Is Commonly Used

- Double wall corrugated board is widely used in industries where products need stronger protection.
- It is commonly used for electronics packaging, especially for larger devices that need extra cushioning.
- It is also used for industrial equipment, where products may be heavy or have sharp edges that put pressure on the box.
- In e-commerce and export shipping, it helps products survive long shipping routes and repeated handling.
- Furniture parts, automotive components, and bulk retail goods also often use this material because of its durability.

## Box Styles That Work Best With This Material

- Double wall corrugated board is mostly used in practical shipping styles rather than decorative packaging.
- The most common style is the regular slotted container, which is the standard shipping box with top and bottom flaps.
- It is also used in heavy-duty mailer boxes and die-cut corrugated boxes designed for specific products.
- For larger products, custom inserts made from corrugated material can also be added inside the box for extra protection.
- These styles focus on keeping the product secure during movement and storage.

## Understanding the Flute Combination

- Double wall corrugated board uses two flute layers, and different flute combinations can change the performance of the box.
- For example, a BC flute combination is very common because it provides both cushioning and stacking strength.
- An EB flute combination creates a slightly thinner structure while still maintaining durability and a smoother surface for printing.
- The flute choice depends on how much protection the product needs and how the box will be used.

## Final Perspective

- Double wall corrugated boards are built for situations where regular packaging is not enough. It provides stronger support, better stacking ability, and improved protection during shipping and storage.
- It is not meant for a luxury presentation. Its main job is to keep products safe under pressure, and it does that very well.
