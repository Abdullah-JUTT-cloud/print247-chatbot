## Article

- Anti scratch coating is an extra protective finish that is carefully applied on the outer surface of mylar bags and flexible pouches. Their primary goal is to reduce scratches and scuffs that are easily observable. Mylar packaging uses laminated films that provide a strong barrier and keep products safe from light, moisture, and oxygen. Adding anti scratch coatings is a good choice since it adds an extra layer of protection that helps keep the original appearance of the custom printed pouches.
- This coating is mostly applied during the finishing stage. It creates a durable surface that resists friction and surface wear. This coating helps brands ensure the printed colors, graphics, and design elements remain intact for a long time.

## How Anti-Scratch Coating Works

- Anti scratch coating helps form a thin protective layer on top of the printed film. This layer reduces friction between surfaces when bags rub against each other or against other materials during storage and transportation. Instead of the printed layer absorbing the impact directly, the coating absorbs most of the friction.
- The coating also enhances the surface hardness of the packaging material. This prevents custom Mylar bags from having light scratches that can occur when packages slide across shelves or packing equipment. It doesn't completely make the packaging scratch proof, but it helps reduce visible marks that can badly impact the product's presentation.

## Exploring the Benefits of Anti Scratch Coating

- Anti scratch coating on mylar bags offers several amazing benefits. The following are the perks os using anti scratch coating:.
- Anti scratch coating helps enhance durability. The coating helps keep the packaging safe from tears, punctures, and scratches. This lowers the risk of damage during shipping and handling.
- It also helps improve the product's shelf presentation. When there are no visible scratches on the packaging, it looks more polished and cleaner.
- Anti scratch coating makes the packaging appear new and premium upon arrival. This increases customer satisfaction and builds trust.

## Final Thoughts

- Brands that look for packaging to make sure they stay visually consistent throughout distribution, anti-scratch coating is a practical finishing option. It adds that extra layer of protection without affecting the flexibility, sealing performance, or barrier properties of Mylar packaging.
- At Print247, anti-scratch coating can be applied as an extra finish for custom Mylar bags and pouches. This helps shield printed surfaces while preserving the neat and professional look that modern packaging requires.
