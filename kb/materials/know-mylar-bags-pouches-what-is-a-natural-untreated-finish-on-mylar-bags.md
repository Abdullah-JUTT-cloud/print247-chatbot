## Article

- If you are wondering what a natural or untreated finish on mylar bags is, it actually refers to the surface that is left without additional coatings, special finishing layers, or laminations. This finishing technique is used to retain the original texture and appearance of the Mylar material. It does not add effects like gloss, matte, or metallic coatings.
- This finish is mostly chosen when the focus is on a simple, practical packaging solution rather than a decorative surface appearance. It offers a natural look and feel of the material while still offering the basic protective properties that are expected from Mylar packaging. That includes keeping products safe from external factors like light, moisture, and oxygen.

## How Does a Natural Finish Mylar Bag Look and Feel?

- Natural finish mylar bags are known to have a plain and clean surface. It's nothing like the coated finishes that create a specific texture or shine. Untreated surfaces maintain the original film characteristics.
- The appearance can vary depending on the mylar structure and printing method. Some natural or untreated mylar bags may have a slightly reflective appearance. On the other hand, some can appear more neutral. The surface feels smooth but does not have an added texture created by finishing treatments.

## Which Industries Use Natural / Untreated Finish Mylar Bags?

- Natural finish mylar bags are used for different packaging needs where functionality matters most. They are commonly used for:.
- Industrial and everyday packaging applications.
- Food and beverage packaging where a simple material finish is preferred.
- Retail packaging with minimal branding.
- Storage pouches also use a natural finish.
- Products that do not require a premium surface effect.
