## Article

- Aluminum foil is a thin sheet that is widely employed in the packaging industry. Aluminum foil is known for its power to block oxygen, moisture, and light. These three external factors contaminate and affect product quality. Because of its superior barrier protection, aluminum foil helps improve a product's shelf life.
- They are widely used across various industries such as food packaging, electronics, cosmetics, and pharmaceutical packaging. Aluminum foil on custom mylar bags helps preserve the product's freshness, extend their shelf life, and keep sensitive products safe during storage and shipping.

## What Is Aluminum Foil Made Of?

- Aluminum foil is produced by rolling aluminum into extremely thin sheets. Aluminum foil is often used alone or combined with other materials such as polyethylene (PE), polyester (PET), nylon, or Mylar film.
- In flexible packaging, aluminum foil is laminated with plastic films, which help improve strength, durability, sealability, and resist punctures. The combination makes the packaging safe for a wide variety of product storage needs. When combined with custom printed mylar bags, it offers a superior level of protection and keeps products safe for a long time.

## Why Is Aluminum Foil Used in Packaging?

- The main reason aluminum foil is used in packaging is its outstanding barrier performance. It blocks oxygen, moisture, UV light, and odors from penetrating or leaving the package.
- This level of security helps maintain the quality of products that are sensitive to environmental conditions. It also lowers the risk of spoilage, flavor loss, oxidation, and contamination.
- Because aluminum foil is lightweight and flexible, it can be easily shaped into pouches, wraps, sachets, lids, and other packaging formats.
