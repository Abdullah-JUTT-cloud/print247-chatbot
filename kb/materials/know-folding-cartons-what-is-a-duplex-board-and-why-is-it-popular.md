## Article

- The first visual representation that comes to mind when we hear "duplex board" is a two colored paperboard sheet. One side of this material is white and perfectly smooth surface on which the printing process is done to give catchy visuals. And the other side of this material board is a grey and rough to the touch surface which shows that it is made from recycled materials.
- This material helps to reduce the overall packaging making costs while giving brands the opportunity for clean branding. The white side of the duplex board faces outward so customers see a neat and presentable box. While the surface that is rough, stays on the inside and is well hidden.

## How It Works in Packaging

- The duplex board has a strong enough build to be used in the packaging of thousands of retail products. It is known to hold its structure and shape while protecting items during storage and display. Although strong, it is not suitable to accommodate too heavy or delicate products. On the other hand, for lightweight goods, it works like a charm.
- The smooth white side of the duplex board allows printing of logos, product names, and simple graphics. While it might not support very detailed artwork and inch perfect graphics, it still provides clear and readable results.
- As duplex boards are easy to fold and cut, they are widely used in large production runs that ultimately helps brands to keep costs under limit.

## Where It Is Used

- Duplex board is commonly used in food packaging such as cereal boxes and dry food cartons due to its adaptable nature and sturdy build. In some cases, it can also be used in packaging for small household items and retail goods.
- It is a popular material choice when the custom product packaging needs to look chic but cannot be too expensive.

## Box Style and Shape Options

- Duplex boards can be shaped into all the basic structures that any retail product needs for its packaging. Yes you heard that right. The most common packaging styles made from duplex board include:.
- Straight tuck end boxes.
- Reverse tuck end boxes.
- Seal end boxes.
- Display boxes.
- Sleeve boxes.
- Gable boxes.

## And more

- These box styles are simple and match the strength of the material.
- As far as shapes of the boxes are concerned, duplex board packaging is usually made in the basic structures like cube and rectangular to keep the production fast and the costs low.

## Final Thoughts

- Duplex board is a splendid material choice for packaging preferred by brands that want to give a clean outer look to their products without much spending.
