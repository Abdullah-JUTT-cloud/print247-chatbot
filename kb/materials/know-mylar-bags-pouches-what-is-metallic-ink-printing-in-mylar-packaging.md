## Article

- The metallic ink printing method is used to create shiny, reflective designs on mylar bags or printed pouches. This printing technique helps make mylar bags look eye catching. Metallic inks contain tiny metal particles or pigments that give the packaging a glossy or metallic effect. These inks are commonly available in silver, gold, bronze, copper, and other metallic shades.
- This printing technique is used to highlight branding elements like product names, logos, or specific design elements. This helps businesses instantly make their products stand out on the retail shelves. This printing option makes mylar bags look premium and eye catching.

## How Metallic Ink Printing Works

- In metallic ink printing, special metallic inks are applied directly onto the surface of the custom stand up pouches during the printing process. The reflective particles inside the ink catch light and produce a shiny finish.

## Things to Consider Before Choosing Metallic Ink

- Before using metallic ink printing, it is necessary to understand that metallic colors may appear different under different lighting conditions. The final effect can also vary depending on the pouch material and finish.
