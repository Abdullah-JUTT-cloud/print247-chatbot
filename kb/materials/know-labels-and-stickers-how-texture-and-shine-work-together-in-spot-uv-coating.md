## Article

- Do you remember the days when you walked down the shelves at the store and picked up a specific product box ? Most likely you grabbed it because it looked expensive and felt interesting in your hands. This feeling of touch is what can be achieved through spot UV coating of the product packaging. It is a form of a creative finishing technique that combines gloss and a raised texture. Using this combination will help you stand out on crowded retail shelves.

## What Is Spot UV?

- Spot UV Coating isn't a gloss that covers your entire box. Instead, you apply a clear shiny layer only to the specific parts you want people to notice. The factory does this through spot UV printing. An ultraviolet light dries the liquid instantly when it is put down by a machine. There will be a raised glossy patch on top of the plain paper due to this. It is an innovative technique to make your logos and text stand out without spending a fortune.

## How a Glossy Finish Catches the Customer's Eye?

- People are drawn to shiny surfaces overwhelmingly. Glossy surfaces next to matte backgrounds create an imposing contrast. Spot UV Coating makes your company logo stand out under store lights. Think about seeing a shiny black logo sitting on a flat black box. This highlights the reflective nature of the design due to the glints of light coming from the surface. Creating an impression through branding yourself professionally works very well.

## Adding a Physical Touch to Your Custom Boxes

- Customers judge the product's quality with their hands and eyes. It is natural for human beings to want to touch things before making a purchase. You can encourage customers to run their fingers over your box because spot UV coating has a slight raised feel. They create a fast sensory connection when they feel the smooth texture. How people perceive quality largely depends on their sense of touch. The consumer assumes that the item within the box is equal to the price paid for it if the box feels robust and of high quality.

## Why Putting Shine and Texture Together Works So Well?

- The combination of a cool look and a nice feel is a huge win for any business. Using spot UV coating and spot UV printing together gives you better results than just using a standard finish.
- Here is why this setup works so perfectly for brands:.
- Unbeatable shelf presence: A box that shines and has texture gets noticed much faster than a flat cardboard box.
- Memorable unboxing moments: Consumers remember the first time they opened an item.
- Stronger brand identity: Showing off premium details tells buyers your company actually cares about quality.
