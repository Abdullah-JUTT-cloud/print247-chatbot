## Article

- Satin finish is used to make the mylar surface feel both glossy and matte. The soft touch lamination creates a smooth surface and makes the packaging feel premium. This finishing option adds a soft and low shine appearance to mylar bags. Unlike gloss lamination, it does not reflect light.
- Satin lamination makes the packaging look and feel luxurious and easy to read. Brands use this finishing option to make the packaging look sophisticated. It makes the texture of the packaging feel more refined while balancing visual appeal.
- This finish is created by adding a protective laminated layer over the printed surface of a mylar bag or pouch. The lamination helps protect the design while making sure the packaging has its signature satin like feel.

## How Does Satin-Like Lamination Work?

- Satin finishes by applying a specialized pressure sensitive plastic film on the printed mylar bag. It feels smooth and does not reflect light. It makes the packaging look cleaner under different light conditions. It feels softer on touch.
- This finishing also works as a protective barrier that keeps the mylar bags safe under different circumstances. It keeps the printed design safe from minor scratches and moisture exposure.
