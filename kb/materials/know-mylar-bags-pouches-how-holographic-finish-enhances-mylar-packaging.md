## Article

- Holographic finish is a type of finishing technique that is applied to Mylar bags and pouches. This finishing technique creates a reflective rainbow effect on the pouch. When the light hits the surface, it reflects in multiple colors and patterns. This effect is created using a holographic film that is laminated onto the Mylar material. Holographic finishes are one of the most popular finishes in the retail market these days. This type of mylar packaging commands attention in a way matte surfaces simply cannot.

## What Really is a Holographic Finish?

- A holographic finish is produced by embossing microscopic patterns onto a film layer. These patterns reflect light when they bend, creating the shifting color effect. The holographic film is then laminated to the mylar bag during manufacturing.
- The bags are made using polyester, which means they are strong enough to keep the product safe. It keeps its strength and barrier protection, keeping products safe from light, moisture, and oxygen, no matter the finishes. The holographic layers simply make the packaging look eye catching and add an extra protective surface on the top.

## How Holographic Finish Improves Product Visibility

- One of the main reasons why businesses use holographic finishes in mylar bags is visibility. This finishing technique enhances product visibility on crowded retail shelves. The holographic surface naturally catches light, which instantly makes the products stand out. This finishing option can turn standard packaging into a visually striking display that attracts customers even in crowded retail settings.
- Holographic finish makes the products appear high end and changes customers'perceptions about your products. Businesses can use this finishing option to highlight specific design elements, such as custom logos, product names, and artwork.

## Advantages of Using Holographic Finish on Mylar Packaging
