## Understanding the Litho-Lamination Process

- The term litho refers to lithographic printing. It is a printing method that is used to get high end yet detailed printing results.
- In litho-laminated packaging, artwork is first printed onto a separate sheet.
- This printed sheet is then laminated onto a corrugated board.
- Unlike direct printing on corrugated surfaces, this process gives good image quality, sharp details, and vibrant color outlook.
- This results in a packaging that combines chic printing with corrugated strength.
- The first image that comes to mind of customers upon seeing litho-laminated packaging is that it's made from premium paperboard due to its smoothness and polished appearance.
