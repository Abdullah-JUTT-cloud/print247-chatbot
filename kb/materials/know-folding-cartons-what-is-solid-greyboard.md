## Article

- We all know that luxury packaging is associated with leaving a lasting impression. The weight, quality, and handheld feel of packaging builds a positive perception before the product has even been revealed.
- One material plays a significant role in creating that experience: solid greyboard.
- Although it is hidden under decorative overwrap, but still, this material forms the backbone of many premium packaging solutions. Its thick build gives rigid boxes their signature strength. It is widely used to make luxury packaging for various industries.
- Packaging boxes crafted from this material gives off a premium feel to the inside products whether they are cosmetics, jewelry, or corporate gift items.

## What Is Solid Greyboard?

- Solid greyboard is a thick material made by compressing multiple layers of recycled paper. Unlike folding carton materials that are designed to bend and fold, greyboard is manufactured to remain firm and maintain its shape.
- Its natural gray color comes from the recycled fiberboard used during production. The material is then wrapped with printed paper or textured overwrap. This makes the initial gray surface rarely visible.

## Why Luxury Brands Rely on Solid Greyboard

- A flimsy box can reduce the perceived quality, while a rigid box instantly creates a sense of significance. Solid greyboard can help brands to achieve that premium look.
- It also holds its shape over time. This allows the packaging box to remain attractive even after repeated use.
- For luxury brands, this reuse becomes part of the overall product experience.
