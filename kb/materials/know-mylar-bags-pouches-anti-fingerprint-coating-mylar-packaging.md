## Article

- Anti-fingerprint coating is a protective finish that is applied to the outer layer of mylar bags. This type of finishing helps reduce visible fingerprints, smudges, and other marks on the mylar packaging that could appear when people handle the packaging frequently. This finish is chosen to keep the pouch looking clean and professional even after repeated handling.
- In retail environments, packaging is often touched by so many customers every day. Without these protective layers, oils and fingerprints can leave visible marks on the printed surface. Therefore, brands choose anti-fingerprint coating to prevent these marks from becoming visible and ruining the product's reputation.
- It's a thin protective layer that is applied on top of the printed surface of the mylar pouch. The finish is mostly used on matte or soft touch packaging surfaces where there's a great chance of fingerprints becoming easily noticeable.

## How the Coating is Applied to Mylar Bags

- Anti fingerprint coating is applied during the finishing stage of production. Once the design is printed on the surface, a special coating layer called anti fingerprint coating is applied to the outer surface of the custom pouches.
- This layer is then cured or dried so it forms a smooth protective film. Once applied, the coating becomes a part of the packaging surface. These finishes works throught the entire life cycles of the pouch.
- The coating does not affect the structure of the Mylar bag. It only helps improve surface performance and make sure the packaging looks professional and eye catching.

## Uses of Anti Fingerprint Finish

- If your products needs o maintain a clean and professional appearance, then anti fingerprint finish is your best choice. These finishes are mostly used on custom stand-up pouches, flat pouches, and many other flexible packaging formats.
- Brands use this finishing option for products that rely on strong shelf presentation, such as cosmetics, specialty foods, supplements, cannabis packaging, and luxurious retail products. Visual condition of the packaging plays an essential role in shaping customers'perceptions about your products. Packaging that looks good on retail shelves helps enhance the product's perceived value.

## The Perks of Using Anti-Fingerprint Coating on Mylar Bags

- Anti-fingerprint coating offers several amazing benefits. The following are the benefits that this finishing option offers:.
- Smudge Free Visual Appeal: The coating helps hide away oil from fingertips, preventing any visible mark that can ruin the look of a polished mylar packaging. This finishing option helps keep bags looking brand new, clean, and professional on retail shelves.
- Easy to Clean: These coatings make it easy to remove dirt or oils without needing any specialized cleansers.
- Enhance Durability: The coatings work as an extra protective layer against scratches, signaling a premium quality product.
