## Article

- For as long as it can be remembered, companies often had to choose between two core material options for their product packaging. They could opt to use paperboard. This material options looked attractive but offered limited protection. Or they could skip paperboard and use the traditional corrugated board that provided better strength and durability but appeared bulky.
- So, to combine the good of both the materials in one, Microflute corrugated board was developed. This material combines the protective qualities of corrugated packaging with the cleaner appearance of folding cartons. It gives brands a way to create packaging that feels strong without looking oversized.
- As products become more design-focused and customers pay more attention to packaging, microflute board has become increasingly popular across many industries like retail, e-commerce, electronics, cosmetics, and food, to name a few.

## Structure of Microflute Board at a Glance

- In a nutshell, the microflute corrugated board might look like any ordinary paperboard we see every day. But upon keen notice, the difference becomes clear when you look at its inner structure.
- Like other corrugated materials with different corrugation levels, it also contains a fluted layer placed between liner sheets. However, the flutes are much smaller and closer together than those found in traditional shipping boxes.
- Because the waves are smaller, the board remains thinner. But is still maintains the overall strength to a certain degree.
- This creates a material that offers cushioning and support of a thick corrugated board without making the packaging look heavy-duty or industrial.
- The result is a packaging box that feels more refined while still protecting the product inside.

## Why Brands Are Moving Towards Microflute Packaging

- Consumer expectations have changed significantly over the last decade.
- Customers no longer view packaging as just a container. They often judge the quality of a product based on how it is presented.
- A strong shipping box protects products, but it may not create the best first impression. A folding carton may look attractive, but it may not provide enough protection for fragile products.
- Microflute board solves this challenge by offering both benefits in one structure.
- Brands can create packaging that looks sleek on retail shelves while also providing additional protection during transportation and handling.
- This balance is one of the biggest reasons why microflute packaging continues to grow in popularity.

## How It Improves Product Protection

- The small fluted layer inside the board acts as a shock absorber.
- When the package experiences pressure, vibration, or minor impacts, the flutes help distribute that force across the structure.
- This added cushioning can reduce the risk of product damage.
- For products such as cosmetics, electronics, skincare items, candles, supplements, and specialty foods, this extra protection can make a significant difference.
- The packaging remains lightweight while still offering greater structural integrity than standard paperboard.

## Printing and Branding Advantages

- One reason many brands prefer microflute board is its excellent print surface.
- Traditional corrugated packaging sometimes produces uneven printing because of larger flute patterns underneath the surface.
- Microflute packaging provides a smoother foundation, allowing graphics, logos, colors, and product information to appear cleaner and sharper.
- This makes it easier for brands to maintain a premium appearance without sacrificing protection.
- For businesses investing in custom packaging, this combination of visual appeal and durability is extremely valuable.
