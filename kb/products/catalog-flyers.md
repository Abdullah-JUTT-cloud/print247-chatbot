## FAQ
- Q: How Print247 keeps production local and reliable? A: Local manufacturing matters for speed and quality control. Print247 runs presses in the U.S. with nationwide shipping, which reduces transit time and simplifies problem-solving. Live support checks files, suggests paper options, and confirms proofs so you won't get surprises when the shipment arrives. Sustainability is available across many runs: choose 100% recycled stock or soy-based inks to lower footprint without losing print quality. For quick campaigns, rush production can be arranged to meet tight windows. Shipping, packaging, and minimum order details.
- Q: How to order custom business flyers and what to send? A: If you have print-ready AI, PDF, or EPS files, please send them or send a ZIP file with the images and fonts included. Printed247's free and practical design support will create the layout and prepare final files if only the logo and copy are provided. Please provide details about the flyer paper weight, finish, as well as any extras you want, such as variable coupons and numbering. After careful review, our team will send you a digital proof and production schedule once we've checked your files. Upon approval, presses run and a tracking number is sent to your address. Feature.
- Q: Can Print247 help plan the distribution mix for a flyer campaign? A: Yes. We consult on channel fit (direct mail, door drops, counter handouts, or event inserts) and recommend sizes and stocks for each channel. That planning helps control cost per response and keeps packages compact for easy hand distribution or mailing.
- Q: How does variable data printing work for segmented lists? A: Variable data printing links a spreadsheet to your design so every piece gets unique text or codes. It's ideal for personal offers, sequential numbering, or tracking distribution. We proof the mapping, sample the first batch, and then complete the full run to ensure accuracy.
- Q: Can you combine flyers with other point-of-sale items for one campaign? A: Yes. The artwork and timing of flyers are synchronized with items such as shelf tags, counter cards, or seasonal items. It simplifies the packing and distribution process for teams that require collateral bundled together.
- Q: How do I measure whether a flyer campaign worked? A: You can use a coupon code, a numbered offer, or a landing page tied to the QR code on your flyer. Keep track of redemptions, web visits, and foot traffic during the campaign period. To improve your next campaign, you can track which list segments responded best with variable data.
