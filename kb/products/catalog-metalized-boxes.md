## FAQ
- Q: Why do our custom metallized boxes work for modern brands? A: Your product has only a few seconds to win attention. The cost of paid media is high, retail aisles are crowded, and online thumbnails battle for clicks. Print247's custom metallized boxes turn light into an ally. When your item is highlighted, reflected, and has crisp edges, it is more likely to be picked up and added to cart sooner. We offer a shiny panel around a logo or a full-panel holographic effect that raises the perceived value without changing the product itself. That higher perceived value matters in every channel. On the shelf, the reflective face panel looks like a higher price point. In eCommerce, the same surface reads well on camera and keeps color alive under studio lights. It immediately feels upgraded when you open the lid of the box and see the metallic interior print. It is referred to as premium by customers. It's a packaging win that pays for itself, according to your team.
- Q: How do you control readability on reflective surfaces with small types and barcodes? A: We place a precise white underlay behind text, regulatory notes, and barcodes, then print CMYK or Pantone on top for clean contrast. Line weights, trapping, and overprint settings are adjusted in prepress, and we test scannability during proofing so retail systems read codes without fuss.
- Q: Are metallized boxes recyclable and what eco options do you offer? A: We implement the latest techniques that use recycled paperboard, soy inks, and eco-conscious metallization methods. Many municipalities accept metallized cartons in the paper stream when designed properly. If sustainability targets are strict, we'll tune metallic coverage and adhesives to keep recycling simple while keeping the premium look.
- Q: What products suit custom metallized boxes best? A: Beauty, fragrance, skincare, wellness, confections, small electronics, vape accessories where legal, and premium beverage gift sets benefit the most. These categories gain from higher perceived value and strong shelf pop. Inserts keep fragile items secure, while windows and interior print add merchandising power without new tooling.
- Q: Can you combine metallic effects with tactile features without overdoing it? A: Yes. We recommend a balance of surfaces. For example, a soft touch body with a spot gloss logo, or a matte panel with raised UV lines over a silver field. Emboss or deep emboss for tactile interest, and use foil stamping as a colored accent rather than flooding the panel.
- Q: What file setup helps you hit color targets on metallic stock? A: Please send vector logos and type, high-resolution images, and separate layers for metallic knockouts, white ink, CMYK, and any spot Pantone. If embossing, debossing, or raised UV are to be applied, keep small text as vector paths, avoid hairline rules, and provide a clear keyline. We accept AI, PDF, and EPS packaged in a ZIP.
