Box style cards on the boxes campaign page: custom product boxes,
gift boxes, mailer boxes, kraft boxes, display boxes, square boxes,
cosmetic boxes, apparel boxes. The main quote form dropdown has only:
product box, display box, mailer box, gift box, kraft box, other —
square, cosmetic and apparel boxes have cards but no dropdown value and
map to Other in the main form; the style-card modal preserves the card
title as the product type. Uses named on the page: retail packaging,
branding, product protection, eco-friendly retail (kraft), shipping
heavy/fragile items (corrugated), cosmetic and food-grade (SBS), premium
gift/luxury (rigid chipboard). No tuck-end, auto-lock, pillow, sleeve or
tray style is named here, and rigid appears as a material, not a style.
