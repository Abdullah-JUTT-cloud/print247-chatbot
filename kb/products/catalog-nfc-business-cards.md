## FAQ
- Q: What phones can read these custom NFC business cards without extra apps? A: Most modern iPhones and Android phones read the chip natively with a light tap near the antenna area. The link opens in about 1 to 2 seconds. For older phones or users with NFC off, we can print a small QR code as a backup so no one is left out.
- Q: Can I update the link after NFC cards are printed for a new campaign or product launch? A: Yes. The NFC chip is easily rewritable and supports URL, vCard, or a custom landing page. You can change links anytime, run promotions, or A/B test CTAs. If you need to freeze the information, the chip can be locked after final programming based on your IT or compliance policy.
- Q: What is the best finish and material for field sales or outdoor use? A: Plastic with matte or soft touch lamination deals with weather, frequent taps, and pocket wear. Many clients add spot gloss to logos for a subtle pop. If you need a premium board feel for in-office meetings, heavyweight cardstock with soft touch and foil is a strong choice.
- Q: What are common names for NFC business cards and do they all mean the same thing? A: You might hear NFC cards, NFC chip business cards, NFC visiting cards, NFC name cards, business cards with NFC, or smart business cards. All describe an NFC-enabled business card with a chip that opens a link or contact file. The only difference is the wording, not the function. Custom Finishes For Premium Feel.
