## FAQ
- Q: Can I add ingredient and label information to my foundation boxes? A: Yes. We design your foundation boxes with space for ingredients, net quantity, batch details, and any other product information you need to include, so everything reads clean and clear on the box.
- Q: What's the best material for foundation boxes? A: It depends on your bottle and your goals. Kraft and Cardboard work well for standard bottles with solid everyday protection. Corrugated adds extra shipping protection for glass bottles or long-distance orders. Rigid is best if you want a premium, high-end look and feel.
- Q: Can I get a window cutout to show the shade of the foundation? A: Yes. We offer window cutouts options in any shape you require to show the shade of your foundations to customers and retail buyers to select the right one at a glance.
- Q: Do you charge extra for multi-color designs? A: No. Whether your design uses one color or several, the price stays the same.
- Q: Can you help me choose the right size for my foundation boxes? A: Absolutely. Send us your bottle dimensions and our team will help you find a snug, secure fit that avoids extra movement during shipping.
