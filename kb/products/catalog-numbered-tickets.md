## FAQ
- Q: What To Prepare Before Ordering Your Numbered Tickets at Print247? A: Before you are going to order numbered tickets, it's essential to take some things into account. The most important thing is information. Providing accurate information saves time and revisions later. Confirm the number of series you require, the total quantity, and whether they are divided among VIPs, general admissions, or staff. You also need to decide whether you want a single ticket, ticket plus stub, or multi-part ticket with claim sections. You will also need to decide on the numbering format you want to keep on tickets. You can keep the straight sequential numbers, prefixed codes like EVT-0001, or multiple series for different doors. Creating an Excel sheet containing variable data, such as seat, section, or tier labels, will speed up the process. Feel free to talk to our live support team if you're unsure of the best format for your numbered tickets.
- Q: Can I supply my own numbering data for complex formats on numbered tickets? A: Yes. Many organizers send an Excel file with ticket numbers, prefixes, seat details and even attendee names. Our production team loads this into the variable number field so each numbered ticket lines up with your seating chart or access list instead of a generic auto sequence.
- Q: Do you offer custom numbered tickets wholesale pricing for resellers or large organizers? A: Yes. Resellers, agencies and organizers who order numbered tickets in bulk for multiple events or locations can access wholesale pricing. Print247 can group different series, designs, and ship-to addresses under one project. Regardless of location, your clients still receive location-specific tickets from the unified sourcing plan.
- Q: Can you match my existing ticket layout or house style? A: If you already have a tried and tested design, you can send the file or even a photo of past tickets. Our free design support recreates the layout within our print specs, keeping your branding and structure while aligning it with our numbering and perforation settings for accurate repeat runs.
- Q: What if I am unsure which size or format will work for my event? A: Talk to our support team about the type of event, the number of attendees, and the staffing process. Therefore, we recommend a size, layout, and numbering approach that is compatible with the way your team checks tickets in the real world, not just on a screen.
