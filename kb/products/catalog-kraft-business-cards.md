## FAQ
- Q: Can I write on these Kraft business cards with a regular pen? A: Yes. You can write on these cards. Kraft paper has a natural, uncoated surface that makes it very easy to write on. Whether you use a pen or a permanent marker, the ink stays put and does not smudge.
- Q: Will my colorful logo look the same on kraft paper as it does on white paper? A: Not exactly. Kraft paper, since it's brown, naturally warms your colors. It's like a filter. Darker colors like navy, forest green, and black look fantastic, while very light colors blend into the background. High-contrast designs work best on this stock.
- Q: Are these cards actually made from recycled materials? A: We use environmentally friendly inks and kraft cardstock made from 100% recycled material. Any brand looking to demonstrate their commitment to the environment will benefit from their biodegradable and compostable construction.
- Q: Is the 18PT (450 GSM) thickness too bulky for a standard wallet? A: No, it's much thicker and heavier than a standard 12PT card, but it still fits perfectly into any standard wallet or business card holder. The additional thickness actually is helpful in finding it in large stacks of business cards.
