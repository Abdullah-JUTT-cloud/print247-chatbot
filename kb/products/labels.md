Labels and stickers include custom labels, special stickers, product
labels, business labels, custom stickers, specialty labels, product
stickers, barcode labels, die-cut labels, roll labels, kiss-cut
labels, oval labels, paper labels and round labels.

Label materials: white paper for standard shelf and product work,
silver film for asset and equipment IDs, clear film for transparent
labels, vinyl for abrasion resistance and outdoor use (highly
resistant to water and sun), holographic film for brand security.

For products that face water or oils, BOPP or vinyl with a gloss or
matte laminate is usually recommended. Roll labels are matched to
core size, roll direction and maximum roll diameter. Sheet labels
come in A4 or letter-size sheets. Kiss cut means cutting through
the label layer but leaving the liner.

Barcode labels go through an in-line inspection process and are
graded to standard. Labels use PP white vinyl only in the old sales
script for label stock — state that as a script rule, not a site
claim, if asked.
