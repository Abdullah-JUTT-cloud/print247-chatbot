## FAQ
- Q: Can we set up pull up banners easily without tools? A: Yes. We manufacture pull up banners that are easy to set up and require no tools. Just open the base, connect the pole, pull the graphic up, and attach it to the top rail. The process is designed for staff who are busy at events, receptions, or retail floors. The instructions card and QR video guide help teams get it right the first time.
- Q: Will my QR code scan reliably on a printed pull up banner? A: Yes, when it is designed and printed correctly. We recommend strong contrast, enough quiet space around the code, and a size that matches real viewing distance. Print quality checkpoints focus on sharp edges and clean contrast so scanners can read the code under venue lighting, even when people are moving.
- Q: Can I reuse the same banner hardware and only change the graphic later? A: Yes. You can reuse the same banner and in reality, many brands do. It is practical to reuse graphics and cassettes across seasons and locations if you plan campaigns across seasons or locations. If you change your message, order replacement prints when the hardware is stored. The result is a consistent display system that reduces long-term costs.
- Q: What is the best way to place pull up signs so they get noticed? A: Put them where decisions happen. For lead-gen, place one at the booth corner facing aisle traffic and another near your signup point. For wayfinding, place them at entrances, intersections, and outside rooms before people get lost. For price promos, place them near product tables and checkout lines where the offer can trigger action.
