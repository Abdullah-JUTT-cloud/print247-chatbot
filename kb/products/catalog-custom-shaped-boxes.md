## FAQ
- Q: Why Custom Shaped Boxes Matter More Than You Think? A: Think about how many products your customer sees in a day. Dozens, maybe hundreds. It comes mostly in square or rectangular boxes, plain, flat, predictable, in square or rectangular shapes. Consider having your product sit next to them in a star-shaped, diamond-shaped, or custom die-cut box that literally resembles the product inside. That's what grabs attention. That's how sales start. Custom-shaped packaging isn't just about being "different"; it's about building an identity. If your product is a premium candle, why box it like cereal? If you're selling a luxury fragrance, why send it in a brown carton? Your product shape should lead your box design. We help make that happen.
- Q: What types of shapes can I get for custom shaped boxes? A: You can request nearly any shape you can imagine, circles, triangles, stars, hearts, and fully custom outlines. Our die-cutting equipment is capable of producing complex shapes as long as the design stays within the structural limits of cardboard. Each shape is engineered for function and presentation.
- Q: Are your custom shaped boxes suitable for shipping? A: Yes, we design every custom shaped box with durability in mind. Using materials like rigid and thick white cardboard, combined with weatherproof finishes, we ensure your boxes hold up during transit.
- Q: Do you offer samples before I place an order? A: Absolutely. We offer physical samples within 3 to 4 working days. These allow you to check the structure, print quality, and overall look before committing to a bulk order. Sampling is highly recommended for custom shape boxes.
- Q: What's the minimum order quantity, and how fast can I get my boxes? A: We have a minimum order quantity of 500 boxes per shape/design. Once your design is approved and the sample is signed off, production takes around 12 to 15 working days. We also offer free design support to streamline the pre-production process.
