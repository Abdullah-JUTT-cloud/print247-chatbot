## FAQ
- Q: Why Do Our Business Labels Work For Real Operations? A: We print and manufacture labels like a production manager thinks. Before we do anything, we collect where the label will sit, what it touches, how it is applied and how long it must last. Paper suits short life or low cost jobs, while vinyl and BOPP suit moisture, oils and regular cleaning. Polyester steps in when industrial strength is needed, such as machinery tags or outdoor stock labels. Adhesive choice is just as important as print. Permanent adhesive is great for shipping boxes and branding labels, but removable grades matter for temporary promos, reusable containers or rented equipment. For cold chains, we shift to freezer grade adhesive that bonds even on slightly frosted surfaces.
