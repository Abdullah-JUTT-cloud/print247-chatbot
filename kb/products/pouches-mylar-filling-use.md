Let hot food cool before sealing a mylar pouch: barrier film handles
warmth, but extreme heat can melt the inner layer; fill at room
temperature for long-term storage. Heat-seal with a standard commercial
impulse sealer, steady pressure for about two seconds above the zipper
line, creating an airtight, tamper-evident seal until the consumer tears
the notch. Oxygen absorbers work with high-barrier film: size the
absorber to the fill weight and drop it in just before sealing.
Hang holes for pegboard display: standard round punches and sombrero
style cuts at the top of the pouch.
