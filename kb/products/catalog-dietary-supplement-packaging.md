## FAQ
- Q: Can you help with the layout for Supplement Facts and regulatory text? A: Yes. We regularly design supplement label layouts with space for a clear Supplement Facts panel, allergens, warnings, storage guidance, pregnancy or age notes, barcodes and required symbols. Your regulatory team always has final say on wording, but we handle the space planning and hierarchy so everything fits and reads well.
- Q: How do you support anti counterfeit and traceability needs? A: We plan spaces for QR code traceability, batch code, lot number, expiry date printing, serialization and unique codes on dietary boxes. Hologram areas and 3D raised UV elements can be added as visual security cues. This gives you a practical platform for anti counterfeit packaging, without cluttering the design or slowing your line.
- Q: Is your supplement packaging suitable for e commerce brands that ship nationwide? A: Yes. We design e commerce ready packaging with strong structures, scuff resistant finishes and drop test focused thinking. Boxes ship flat to save freight, then are built into solid cartons that protect nutritional supplement containers in parcel networks. Weather-proof inks and coatings help your packs look good after long shipping legs.
- Q: How does your packaging handle herbal and odor heavy supplements? A: For herbal supplement packaging and oil based products, we focus on barrier packaging against odor and migration. We pair suitable containers with correct liners and seals, then add cartons that control light and support freshness protection. Label information can explain aroma or sediment, which reduces customer complaints and returns.
