Mylar pouch sizes are Width x Height, with gusset as the third
dimension. Units: inches, cm, or mm. On the main campaign form the
gusset is optional (blank = 0); the jerky form has no dimension fields.
Main popular sizes (guide, not a guarantee): 3x4 inches — single-serve
powders, herb samples — 2-5g; 4x6 inches — gummies, hard candy — 14-28g;
5x8 inches — ground coffee, bath salts — 56-113g; 6x9 inches — protein
powders, pet treats — 113-226g; 8x12 inches — bulk food, rice, beans —
453-907g. Jerky sizes (W x H + gusset): 4x6+2 — sample/single-serve —
1-1.5 oz; 5x8+3 — snack retail — 2-3 oz; 6x9+3.5 — standard retail —
3-5 oz; 7x11+4 — large retail — 6-8 oz; 8x12+4 — bulk/value — 12-16 oz.
Context note: the same nominal size has different capacities across
the two tables because capacity depends on gusset and fill — never
present a capacity as a guarantee. Custom cutting is offered.
