- Q: What size of folded business cards works best for most brands? A: Most brands keep their business cards close to standard business card sizes so they fit into any wallet, but their open sizes are doubled for 4-panel layouts. With this design, your folded business card feels familiar to hold while providing enough room for services, a short brand story, and a call to action.
- Q: Can I really use folded business cards as appointment or loyalty cards? A: Yes. Many clinics, salons and repair shops use the inner panel as an appointment card with time and date fields. Cafes, gyms and retail stores often use the same writable interior as a loyalty card grid with boxes ready for stamps. One printed piece handles both contact details and repeat-visit tracking.
- Q: Which cardstock thickness should I choose for my folded business card? A: If your business cards live mainly in wallets or pockets, 14pt or 16pt cardstock balances durability with easy folding.
