## FAQ
- Q: How should I store my kraft paper cards to keep them in perfect condition? A: If you store them in their original packaging sleeves until you're ready to use them, they will keep well and last a long time. Since natural kraft paper absorbs moisture easily, it can warp slightly if exposed to high humidity.
- Q: Will the painted borders of these business cards chip or flake off after sitting in my wallet? A: No, the color stays fully intact on our painted edge business cards. We apply the pigment deep into the thick paper edges using specialized equipment. It bonds securely with the paper fibers, so it survives the daily friction of being pulled in and out of a leather wallet.
- Q: Can I use a regular ballpoint pen on these cards without the ink bleeding? A: Ink works exceptionally well on these recycled kraft paper cards. The uncoated surface grabs ballpoint pen ink instantly without smudging like glossy paper does. You can even use standard pencils and markers in them for quick notes.
- Q: Do QR codes scan properly on the textured brown surface? A: The QR codes work perfectly if you print them in a dark, solid color, such as black or navy. Avoid using yellow or light gray as phone cameras are unable to distinguish light colors from brown. Custom Finishes For Premium Feel.
