## FAQ
- Q: How do you determine the right insert material for heavy medical devices? A: We analyze the exact weight, dimensions, and center of gravity of your device. We rely on dense EVA foam or thick corrugated inserts for heavy equipment. These materials absorb heavy impacts and stop the device from shifting. They also prevent stress fractures during rough courier transit.
- Q: Can we use molded pulp inserts for sterile surgical kit packaging? A: Yes, molded pulp is an excellent choice. It securely holds sharp surgical tools in place without scratching them. It meets high sustainability standards. We design specific product cavities so every scalpel and clamp stays perfectly organized, aiding rapid access for busy healthcare staff.
- Q: Do custom foam inserts pass transit drop tests? A: Yes. We engineer the shock absorption capabilities to meet strict courier requirements. This foam dissipates kinetic energy by strengthening the high-impact zones around fragile glass items. Your products remain completely safe even if the box takes a hard fall off a sorting belt.
- Q: How do medical boxes with inserts improve workflow for healthcare staff? A: Time is highly critical in a medical setting. Each component of a multicompartment insert appears in a logical order. It is not necessary for a nurse to dig for a specific test swab or vial. The process is highly efficient because everything fits perfectly into a dedicated die-cut cavity.
