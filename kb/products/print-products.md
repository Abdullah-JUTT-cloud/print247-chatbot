Print products include postcards, flyers, brochure printing,
bookmarks, presentation folders, booklets, magnets, greeting cards
and invitations.

Brochure fold styles include Bi-Fold, Tri-Fold, Z-Fold, Gate-Fold,
Accordion-Fold, Roll-Fold, French Fold, Cross-Fold, Double Parallel
Fold, Half-Fold, Step-Down, Envelope, Insert and Folder,
Poster-Style and Die-Cut.

Flyers: letter size 8.5 x 11 inches, QR codes can link to landing
pages, numbering and variable data can track responses, die-cut
flyers are available, weather-proof stocks and eco-friendly inks
are offered.

Variable data printing can automatically change names, numbers and
barcodes on a run. Print products FAQ says small minimum order
quantities are offered on many premium custom items — this is a
per-item exception, not a replacement for the general 1000 MOQ.
Confirm the item with the team before quoting a small quantity.
