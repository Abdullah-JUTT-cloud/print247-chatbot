## FAQ
- Q: Still in doubt? A: We are here to assist you with your custom car window decals questions. Feel free to call our support team. Category.
- Q: How long do car window decals usually last on a vehicle? A: Car window decals'lifespan depends on sun exposure, climate and how often the car is washed. However, most outdoor-rated sticker sets hold up for several years on window glass under normal use. The use of UV-focused inks and lamination helps slow fading, especially on rear windows that spend hours in direct sunlight each week.
- Q: Can these decals be used on both glass and body panels? A: Yes, many clients apply one kit across different surfaces. We can use films and adhesives that perform well on glass, painted metal and selected plastic trim, with guidance on where each piece should go. For heavily textured plastics, we may suggest smaller sizes or alternate placements for longer life.
- Q: Are your car window decals safe for factory tint and defroster lines? A: Yes but when applied correctly. There is a thin layer of film sitting on top of the tint, not across the defroster lines. Our proofs indicate heater grid areas, and we suggest layouts that avoid cutting directly over them. To preserve the defroster grids and tint film, low heat and gentle tools are used during removal.
- Q: How do you remove decals from car windows? A: You can easily remove decals from the car window without scratching the glass or harming tint. Start by gently warming the decal with a hair dryer or low-heat gun to soften the adhesive. When the vinyl feels flexible, lift a corner with a fingernail or plastic scraper and slowly pull back at a low angle. In most cases, the adhesive comes off with the film with this heat-peel method.
