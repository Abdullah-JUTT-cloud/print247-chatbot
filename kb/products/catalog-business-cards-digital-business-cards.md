## FAQ
- Q: How Tap-to-Share Information Actually Works? A: Tape-to-share technology moves data between devices instantly using Near Field Communication (NFC). Here is the simple version. Someone picks up your card, either taps it against their phone or scans the QR code, and your full digital profile opens in their browser. No app. No setup on their end. Just their phone and your card.
- Q: Who Uses Digital Business Cards and Why Does It Work? A: A custom digital business card or a smart business card is not just for businesses in the tech industry. These business cards are used by sales teams at every client meeting, which is when they get real results. The card enables freelancers and creatives to share their entire portfolio without fumbling with paper or emailing later. With one tap, photographers, designers, and consultants can provide prospective clients with all the information they need to know. Executives and C-suite professionals use metal NFC cards because they are matched to the meetings they are entering. Realtors share property listings, testimonials, and contact information on one card, then track how many people actually engage with it. During trade shows and conferences, event marketers distribute branded digital business cards instead of traditional paper cards.
