- Q: Why do multilayer hemp Mylar bags win the durability race? Those numbers aren't marketing hype; they stem from the same lamination schedule used for specialty mylar coffee bags that guard single-origin beans for six months or more. By replacing the beans with CBD-rich flower, you get the same low-oxygen environment without the roasting-destroying grease. As brands upgraded, grubby jars were replaced with crisp, glossy printed pouches that maintained trichome purity until the point of sale.

Even coffee roasters repurpose the same barrier film for roasted beans, proving the pouch's crossover appeal.
