## FAQ
- Q: Which box style works best for 30 ml hemp oil droppers? A: Most brands choose straight tuck or reverse tuck for speed and clean front panels. If the bottle is heavy or has a dropper with a wide bulb, a seal-end style adds strength. We add a custom insert that locks the shoulder, which reduces leaks and keeps the cap from taking the load.
- Q: Do you offer child-resistant options for hemp oil packaging? A: Yes. We produce child-resistant sleeves and lock-tab cartons designed to fulfill necessary protocols when matched with approved closures. These designs include push-and-slide actions or tear-strip gates. We can guide testing with your lab and keep the structure user friendly for adults while providing clear open-close instructions.
- Q: Can you add batch codes, best-by dates, and QR-to-COA on the box? A: Yes. We can print static or variable data areas for lot, best-by, and barcodes, plus a QR to your COA. If you prefer to print codes in-house, we leave a coated patch for thermal or inkjet. Placement follows retailer scanner rules so your line flows smoothly.
- Q: What insert options keep glass droppers safe in boxes while in transit? A: Paperboard U-locks fit most droppers and fold flat for shipping. For premium sets, rigid EVA or molded pulp inserts are available. The goal is to grip the bottle at the shoulder or base, not the cap, so torque on the closure stays low. We size inserts to your exact bottle spec.
