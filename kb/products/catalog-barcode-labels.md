- Q: What barcode formats can you produce, and will they scan on my hardware? A: We produce UPC, EAN, Code 128, Code 39, ITF 14, Data Matrix, and QR. We size modules to your scanner distance and verify each code to ISO and ANSI grading. Share your scanner models or POS, and we will align specs so first time reads are consistent.
- Q: Will these labels survive in a freezer or outdoors? A: Yes, but you must select the right material for barcode labels. For freezers, we use a specific freezer-grade adhesive and a synthetic stock like vinyl or polypropylene. For outdoor use, we add a UV-resistant laminate to prevent sun fading. Let us know your specific environment, and we will recommend the correct stock.
- Q: Which adhesive should I choose for my surface and environment? A: When it comes to cartons and smooth plastics, permanent is an excellent choice. When it comes to glass or short-term promotions, removable avoids leaving behind residue. If you have cold rooms or textured bins, use a freezer-grade or high-tack adhesive. Let us know the surface, temperature, and dwell time of your surface, and we will propose the best solution.
