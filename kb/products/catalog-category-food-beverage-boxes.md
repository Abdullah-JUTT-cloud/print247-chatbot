## FAQ
- Q: What is the best material for eco-friendly food packaging that maintains heat retention? A: For hot food delivery, E-flute corrugated cardboard or high-density Kraft paperboard; these act as a natural thermal anchor, are 100% recyclable, and maintain structural integrity under high-heat or steam-heavy conditions.
- Q: Can I order custom printed food boxes with low MOQs for product testing? A: Yes; high-fidelity digital printing allows low minimum order quantities without the cost of expensive metal plates.
- Q: How does smart packaging integration help in scaling a food brand? A: Embedding dynamic QR codes or NFC tags turns static packaging into a digital gateway, useful for real-time sourcing transparency, nutritional data, and disposal instructions.
- Q: Are custom food sleeves more cost-effective than full enclosure boxes? A: Sleeves use 40% less material than full boxes while providing ample space for branding, and suit "ready-to-eat" trays.
- Q: How do I ensure my food packaging is optimized for the "unboxing ritual" in e-commerce? A: Sensory unboxing elements such as soft-touch matte laminations, spot UV highlights, and precision-scored "petal-style" closures.
