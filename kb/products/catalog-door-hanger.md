## FAQ
- Q: How do our custom-printed door hangers impact your brand? A: A door hanger is more than just a rectangle with a hole. It delivers a quick, effective pitch. The front shows your main message and call to action, while the back addresses any concerns or questions. For hotels, a clean do not disturb door hanger sign with bilingual copy and brand-consistent icons helps staff move faster and guests feel cared for. For campaigns, lead with a single hero message and a scannable offer. For multi-tenant buildings, folded door hangers carry a second panel for apartment-specific info or emergency numbers. Facility managers lean on custom hanging door signs for maintenance messages, safety checks, and shifts, with a writable surface for dates and initials.
- Q: What size should I pick for residential routes versus commercial properties? A: For residential and commercial neighborhoods, 4.25 x 11 inches balances presence and stackability. It handles door movement without curling. For commercial door hardware and tall glass doors, the 5.5 x 17 inches stays visible from a distance. If teams carry very large stacks, 3.5 x 8.5 inches saves weight and still fits a headline, offer, and QR.
- Q: Can you add names, addresses, or unique QR codes to each door hanger? A: Yes. Print247 offers variable printing that personalizes each unit with names, store addresses, sequential numbers, or QR codes tied to a location page. We set safe zones so codes scan on the first try and test on your chosen stock. Send your data file, and we'll validate fields before proofing.
- Q: Are these hangers suitable for facility door signs and housekeeping needs too? A: Yes. Custom hanging door signs and custom door hanger signs work well for maintenance, safety, and shift notices. Hotels rely on custom hotel door hangers for guest communication. We offer erasable surfaces for initials and dates. For long-term cleaning zones, ask about rigid synthetic options or lamination for daily wipe-downs.
- Q: What if we need a different shape or a special hanging cut? A: We can run custom-shaped dies, hooks for lever handles, and perforated catches that hold tight on windy entries. Send a photo of your typical hardware and we will match the hole size and slit geometry. If you want something original, our team builds a die line for your approval.
