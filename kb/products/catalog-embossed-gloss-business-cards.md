## FAQ
- Q: Why should I pick an embossed gloss card instead of a standard gloss business card? A: A standard gloss card shines but feels flat. Our embossed gloss version adds height that guides fingers and eyes to key details, boosting recall. Raised surfaces also extend card life; people keep unusual textures longer than smooth prints, so your contact details stay visible during decision time.
- Q: What stock and thickness do you use for embossed gloss business cards? A: Paper cards start at a hefty 16-point coated board, while embossed plastic business cards use 20-mil PVC or PETG for water and tear resistance. Both options accept full-color printing, gloss UV, and spot foils without warping, giving you a premium feel that survives heavy daily handling.
- Q: How long does production and shipping take? A: Samples ship in 3 to 4 working days. Full runs of 1,000 or more units each to your doorstep in 12 to 15 working days, depending on extras like foil. Live tracking lets you monitor every leg of the journey.
- Q: Can I combine embossing with foil, spot gloss, or raised UV on the same card? A: Yes. The die-strike comes first, followed by foil stamping or raised UV. Spot gloss can land either over or next to the emboss. We set registration marks to ensure layers align exactly, giving you complex multi-texture cards without mishits or registration shadows.
- Q: Are the inks and coatings used on your products eco-friendly? A: The inks we use are eco-friendly, CMYK, and contain very low volatile compounds. UV varnish cures under light, not heat, leaving no solvent fumes. Soft touch laminate is available in a biodegradable variant, and leftover paper stock is recycled. You get premium results plus peace of mind for your sustainability goals. Custom Finishes For Premium Feel.
