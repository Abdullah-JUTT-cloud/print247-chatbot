## FAQ
- Q: Can custom static clings be used on textured walls or uneven surfaces? A: No. Smooth and non-porous surfaces like glass, mirrors, and flat-painted walls are a perfect match for custom static clings. If you use them on textured or porous walls, the electrostatic grip will be reduced that can cause edge lifting. If you want to place clings on a textured wall, then adhesive-backed vinyl is a better option.
- Q: Are custom window clings safe for car glass and will they block visibility? A: The static clings for car windows are designed to stick to the rear and side glass. For safety reasons, they should not interfere with the driver's direct line of sight. Our team at Print247 can advise you on appropriate placement zones based on your vehicle type, intended use, and regional guidelines.
- Q: How should I store custom clings between uses to get the most reuse out of them? A: It is recommended to store custom static clings flat, away from direct sunlight and dust. The cling surface is significantly extended if they are kept face-to-face with the original release liner. In most cases, after a campaign, a single print run can last for multiple seasons, which reduces the per-use cost significantly over time.
- Q: Do I need print-ready files, or can Print247 help with the design? A: You can send any file in AI, PDF, EPS, or ZIP format. Print247 has a dedicated team of expert designers. If your files are not print-ready, then our team can help you. They review them before production at no additional charge. Our free design assistance is available and can work with your existing brand assets to make everything production-ready.
