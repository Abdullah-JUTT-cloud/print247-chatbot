## FAQ
- Q: What is the best time to make use of labeling with holographic? A: Labels with holographic designs are highly successful when it comes to attracting the attention of consumers. A visually appealing product is particularly essential in a congested market. There are many household brands available in bath, cosmetics, healthy supplements, food and beverages, and also the expanding CBD market, space on the shelves is scarce.
- Q: How Do Holographic Labels Add Value to Products? A: An exclusively designed holographic tag can be applied to nearly any item and gives the appearance of a high-end product, from a delicate shimmer to an imposing impact. The entire label does not have to be holographic. However, we recommend that you design your label using a clear area to let the holographic material be prominently displayed.
- Q: Do holographic labels require a lot of effort to print? A: Holographic labels are great for the use of inking, but they are too simple and require expertise for pristine printing. Printing designers check out the locations where they want to block out the effect of holograms in specific regions by adding a white ink layer. Flexible label materials allow for customization of designs and surfaces that are curved. High gloss laminates are the most appropriate finish that will maximize shine and reflectivity.
