## FAQ
- Q: Do I need child-resistant features for my patch packaging? A: It depends on the active ingredients. Child resistant mechanisms are rarely required for over-the-counter pain patches. However, strong prescription treatments and nicotine patches must come with child-resistant locking tabs. The locking mechanisms we design meet pharmacy compliance standards while also being accessible to adults.
- Q: Can you print barcodes directly on the box, or do I need stickers? A: We print easily scannable barcodes directly onto your patch packaging surface during the main production run. This prevents the extra labor cost of manually applying barcode stickers later. If you provide us with your exact barcode files in high resolution, we will place them perfectly on the bottom panels.
- Q: What happens if my primary sachets are slightly thicker than planned? A: Even a millimeter of unexpected thickness of sachets causes bulging when packing multiple units. We always ask for an exact thickness of your filled sachet before finalizing the dieline. We can then calculate the exact internal clearance necessary for the patch boxes to stack perfectly flat.
- Q: Do regulations dictate the font sizes on the outer packaging? A: Yes, regulatory bodies maintain strict rules regarding the minimum font size for active ingredients, warnings, and usage instructions. We review your typography layout to verify all medical data is legible and strictly compliant with legal requirements without disrupting the overall design hierarchy.
- Q: Is it better to load patches from the top or the side? A: When packing machines are automated, side-loading cartons are much faster and more efficient. When packing boxes manually by hand, top-loading reverse tuck cartons are the best choice. We build the flap orientation of printed pharmaceutical boxes based exactly on your specific fulfillment process.
