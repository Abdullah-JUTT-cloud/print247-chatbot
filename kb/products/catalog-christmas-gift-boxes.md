## FAQ
- Q: Can Christmas gift boxes be fitted with LED lights or battery packs? A: Yes, as "Christmas light up gift boxes"; boxes can be modified with recessed cavities or die-cut tabs to house LED modules and battery holders, with placement coordinated with the electrical component supplier.
- Q: Are these gift boxes suitable for automated filling and assembly lines? A: Many styles work on automatic packing lines when designed with consistent flap sizes, tuck tolerances and reinforced edges; pre-glued crash-lock or auto-bottom styles are named for fast machine set-up.
- Q: How should printed Christmas boxes be stored before filling? A: Flat, in a cool dry place away from sunlight and humidity, stacked to recommended heights, rotated FIFO, on pallets off the floor.
- Q: What secondary packaging protects stacked gift boxes in transit? A: Layered cardboard slip sheets, corrugated trays or partitioned cartons, plus edge protectors on pallet corners and stretch wrap.
- Q: What types of Christmas gift boxes with lids are offered? A: Rigid two-piece boxes (lid and base) are named as the most popular option, with other unboxing styles available on request.
