## FAQ
- Q: Will raised UV or raised foil make fine text unreadable? A: When applied correctly, raised UV or raised foil doesn't make fine text unreadable on foil accent business cards. Our technical team reviews every file and may recommend thickening the stroke weight of tiny serif fonts to preserve clarity under embossing pressure.
- Q: How quickly can I receive cards for an unexpected trade show? A: The priority scheduling reduces print time to 6 to 7 working days, and we can provide express courier delivery to most US cities so that you receive your business cards as quickly as possible. It is always best to contact our support team before placing an order for fast deliveries.
- Q: Can I order fewer than 1,000 cards for market testing? A: For foil stamping, runs under 1,000 are inefficient because of the required machinery. Print 1,000 instead, then split the batch across two or three design versions, logo A and logo B, as well as limited-edition holiday artwork, so you can test response rates without wasting stock.
- Q: Which file format yields the cleanest foil registration? A: A vector art file in Adobe Illustrator (.ai or.eps) provides the sharpest edges and the most predictable plate alignment. The resolution of high-resolution PDFs is not as important as the ability to edit layers. Avoid using flattened bitmaps for foil masks as they may result in jagged edges.
