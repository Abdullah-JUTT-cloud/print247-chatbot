## FAQ
- Q: Why Print247's Custom Matte Business Cards Win Attention in Busy Spaces? A: Every handshake lives or dies by the card you leave behind. Print247 offers custom matte business cards due to their various advantages. Under harsh office lights or café down‑lighting, a matte surface absorbs glare and keeps your design readable from every angle. The softened sheen reduces fingerprints, allowing photography, gradients, and small types to stand out cleanly. You'll instantly feel confident when prospects run a thumb across your card, notice the soft texture, and notice the branding standard you adhere to. It's not uncommon for clients to touch fresh press sheets when reviewing them on our production floor. The tactile appeal of matte business cards makes them more effective than glossy ones for generating follow-up calls.
- Q: What is the difference between matte and glossy surfaces in terms of readability? A: Due to a matte surface, light diffuses, which reduces glare that can occur in bright environments with glossy cards. Fonts and color areas stay clear because less shine competes for the eye's attention. The flatter surface also absorbs tiny oil marks from fingertips, so text remains crisp long after the initial hand‑off.
- Q: Can I mix matte with Spot UV without creating cracking or peeling at the border? A: Yes. Spot UV is laid after the matte coating cures and is flash‑dried under UV lamps. The two layers bond at a molecular level, so you get glass‑like highlights sitting flush on a velvet base. Proper file separation and registration keep edges clean, avoiding the flake lines seen in cheaper prints.
- Q: Why is the minimum order set at one thousand units? A: Offset presses require plates, ink calibration, and make‑ready sheets before hitting steady‑state quality. That setup cost spread over fewer than one thousand cards would push unit pricing too high. A thousand‑card starting point delivers premium stock, advanced finishes, and full quality control while keeping per‑unit cost attractive.
- Q: What timeline should I expect from proof approval to delivery? A: Once digital proofs or a 3D mock‑up earns your go‑ahead, physical samples leave within three to four working days. Full production usually wraps up seven to nine days later, followed by two to three days in transit. Plan on a total of twelve to fifteen working days for most mainland addresses.
- Q: Will QR codes print cleanly on a matte surface? A: Absolutely. The matte coating provides a smooth, low‑sheen canvas that keeps black modules tight and white spaces open. Our pre‑press team checks code contrast ratios and size against the stock to guarantee quick scans with standard smartphone cameras.
