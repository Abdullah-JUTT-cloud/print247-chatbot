Banners and signs include vinyl banners, pull-up banners, banner
stands, flag printing, angled flags, rectangle flags, outdoor and
rigid signs, office signs, decals, table coverings, and event and
tradeshow displays.

Vinyl banner weights: 9 oz, 10 oz, 13 oz, 15 oz, 18 oz, 20 oz, 22 oz.
Finishing: heat-welded hems and grommets, reinforced corners, pole
pockets available. Printed in CMYK.

Pull-up banner types: Standard, Premium, Double-sided, Wide-format,
Tabletop. Pull-up sizes include 33.5 x 79 inches, 23.5 x 79 inches
and 47 x 79 inches.

Banners have no minimum in the old sales script. Vinyl banner spec
says "Minimum Quantity — 1000 Banners (Short run orders accepted)".
See facts/moq.md for the full context note.
