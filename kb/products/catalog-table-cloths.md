## FAQ
- Q: What brand information should be printed on custom table cloths? A: We print your brand logo in the center on the front drop so it is clearly visible. You can write your business name with a marketing tagline that instantly shows who you are and what you are offering. Add your business contact information and address. With these custom details, your tablecloth directly communicates your brand and makes it stand out in a crowded business setup.
- Q: What type of tablecloth should businesses use for trade shows and exhibitions? A: It depends on your storage needs and event theme. You can use a fitted tablecloth for a professional and clean appearance. If you use stretch spandex, it gives a wrinkle-free appearance and withstands wind, making it best for outdoor exhibitions. When you need to cover the whole table, use standard throws. They come in 3 and 4 sides options to completely hide the table or keep one side open for easy access to stock.
- Q: How do custom tablecloths promote your business? A: Our printed tablecloths are customized with brand details and a logo for a clear and bold display. These stunning visuals draw attention from a distance. They establish an immediate professional image and attract potential partners and buyers.
- Q: Are tablecloths washable and reusable? A: Yes, tablecloths for businesses are made for repeated use in different commercial trade shows and exhibitions. These display tablecloths can be washed and reused. How you can wash these printed items depends on their material type. Some fabrics can be machine washed, and some need steaming.
- Q: What table cloth material should I choose for my next business exhibition? A: It depends on your event venue. Choose a polyester table cloth if you are setting up an indoor display. This material is wrinkle free and moisture resistant. It gives a smooth and sleek visual appearance. If you are hosting an outdoor event, select stretch spandex; it resists tearing and withstands weather changes. These table cloths give your stall a modern look.
