Custom boxes include display boxes, rigid boxes, metalized boxes,
cosmetic boxes, CBD boxes, pharmaceutical boxes, apparel boxes, food
and beverage boxes, mailer boxes and folding cartons.

Tuck box styles: Straight Tuck End, Reverse Tuck End, Crash Lock,
Snap Lock Bottom, Gable, Dispenser, Perforated, Double Wall, Window
Cutouts, Hang Tabs.

Box board is generally 12 to 24pt. Pharmaceutical boxes primarily use
14pt to 24pt SBS (Solid Bleached Sulfate) paperboard. Food boxes:
14pt to 16pt lightweight, 18pt to 20pt standard, 24pt heavy duty.

Boxes are shipped flat. Pharmaceutical boxes can include braille
embossing, custom die-cut foam or cardboard inserts, tamper-evident
glue patterns and perforated tear strips.

Minimum order quantity: 1000 units per size (see facts/moq.md for
context notes).
