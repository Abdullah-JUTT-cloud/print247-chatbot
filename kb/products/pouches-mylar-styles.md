Form-selectable mylar pouch styles on the campaign page: stand-up pouch,
flat pouch (3-side seal), pinch bottom pouch, side gusset pouch,
flat bottom pouch, and custom. The beef-jerky form adds clear pouch.
Gallery styles: stand-up pouches (bottom gusset, stand on shelves),
3-side seal bags (single-serve), flat bottom pouches (five printable
sides), and spout pouches (liquid, nozzle cap).
Context note: spout pouch appears in the gallery but is NOT a form
dropdown option (gallery prefill bug) — do not offer it as a
form-selectable style until dev fixes it. This campaign list is
narrower than the general pouch list: no vape, CBD, chocolate,
pre-roll, or poly-mailer styles are named here.
