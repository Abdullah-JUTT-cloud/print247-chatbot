## FAQ
- Q: Can I add serialization or QR codes for limited edition runs? A: Yes. Every gold foil packaging box can be customized with serial numbers, barcodes, QR codes, and NFC tags. With this solution, you can run limited editions, automate registration, and add anti-counterfeit checks without having to assemble anything additional.
- Q: Can inserts be produced in the same material as the box or do they need separate sourcing? A: If you need protection, we can manufacture inserts from matching board, die-cut foam, or corrugated partitions. Adding inserts to the first run reduces handling and simplifies kitting, so this option can be included in the initial quote.
- Q: How does variable printing affect lead time and cost? A: Printing variable data adds setup to the artwork and may affect production flow, but it is handled within the same print run. Generally, moderate quantities do not have a significant impact on lead time, but serialization on large runs can cause delays. Depending on how you prepare data and print it, the cost will vary.
- Q: Are the gold foils and laminations durable in humid or coastal climates? A: Yes, with the right coatings and inks. Weather-resistant finishes and test protocols are applied for shipments to humid or coastal regions. We perform adhesion and rub tests to confirm long-term durability before shipping.
