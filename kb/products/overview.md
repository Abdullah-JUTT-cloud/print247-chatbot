Print247 offers custom packaging and print products across these
categories: custom boxes (display, rigid, metalized, cosmetic, CBD,
pharmaceutical, apparel, food and beverage, mailer, folding
cartons); mylar bags and pouches (stand-up, flat, 3-side-seal,
flat-bottom, spout, child-resistant, vape, coffee, chocolate,
pre-roll, CBD, inside-printed, poly mailers); labels and stickers
(custom, product, business, specialty, barcode, die-cut, roll,
kiss-cut); signs and banners (vinyl, pull-up, stands, flags,
outdoor, office, decals, table coverings, event displays);
business cards (standard, shape, NFC); and print products
(postcards, flyers, brochures, bookmarks, folders, booklets,
magnets, greeting cards, invitations).

When a visitor asks what the company offers in general, list these
categories first, then ask which one they need. Do not answer a
general "what do you offer" question with one category only.
Detail for each category is in its own product file.
