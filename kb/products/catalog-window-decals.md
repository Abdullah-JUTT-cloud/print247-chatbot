## FAQ
- Q: Are window decals suitable for storefronts? A: Yes, window decals are primarily designed for storefronts. Our clear or perforated decals turn plain glass into a branding wall without blocking the view. On the other hand, opaque and frosted decals add privacy. You can display your brand logo and promotional sales to grab the attention of passing customers.
- Q: Can I reuse window decals for different spaces? A: Yes, if you are using static cling decals, you can simply remove them and rearrange them, as they stay with static electricity and not glue. If you are utilizing sticky vinyl decals, they are meant for long term application. These decals are for one time use only, but can be removed neatly.
- Q: How do window decals withstand rain and snowfall? A: Commercial grade window decals are specifically built for outdoor settings. Print247 manufactures these decals with durable material that withstands harsh weather conditions. We apply UV protective gloss or matte laminates that shield decals from moisture and sun. Our strong protective laminations work as a shield against water, moisture, and UV rays and keep decals intact.
- Q: Do window decals ruin the window after removal? A: No, our window decals are made of durable and high quality material. Our static cling and removable vinyl both peel off cleanly. Although permanent vinyl may leave light residue that comes off with a glass cleaner or adhesive remover. Our decals can be easily removed and repositioned.
- Q: How do I choose the right finishing material for window decals? A: It will depend on where you want your window decals and the amount of light exposure they will get. High gloss finishing will give your storefront a beautiful and bright look. If you have to place signage in direct sunlight, select a matte finish. It reduces harsh reflections and keeps your decal crisp and clear. UV protective laminations secure decals from fading and cleaning scratches.
