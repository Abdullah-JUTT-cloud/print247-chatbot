- Q: What is the main difference between standard paper stock and BOPP material? A: Paper stock works perfectly for dry environments and offers a highly inexpensive solution. BOPP is a durable plastic polymer that naturally resists water and oils. You should use it for any items stored in bathrooms or areas exposed to high moisture and friction.
- Q: How should I format my artwork before submitting it for production? A: You must submit your designs in high resolution vector formats like AI, PDF, or EPS. Make sure to outline all your text fonts and embed any linked images. Your design should remain inside the safety line to avoid being clipped during the cutting process.
- Q: Will the adhesive backing leave a sticky residue on my glass jars? A: We use highly resistant adhesives formulated for strong grip without permanent messy bonding. For custom printed product labels, we offer standard adhesives that stick firmly during regular use. They leave no heavy residue when removed from glass and smooth plastic. It makes recycling containers much easier for your customers.
- Q: How do these custom labels hold up on flexible packaging like pouches or bags? A: They perform exceptionally well on flexible packaging. The BOPP material is highly flexible, which makes it superior to stiff paper. With these edges you won't have to worry about lifting or cracking when you stretch, flex, and fold the pouch.
