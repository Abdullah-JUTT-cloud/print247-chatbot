## FAQ
- Q: Can I print different details on both sides of a single flag banner? A: Yes, our double-sided rectangular flags are made of two separate fabric layers. Both sides can be printed with different details. We insert a blockout layer between two pieces of fabric. It prevents light from showing through. That's how we keep individual artwork and information clear to read without bleeding through.
- Q: Do these flag banners withstand extreme heat and weather changes? A: Our custom rectangle flags are made of durable material that withstands harsh weather changes and temperatures. These strong fiber materials do not tear or scratch easily. We use UV-resistant ink to prevent fading and keep your flag noticeable.
- Q: Are rectangular flags permanent or removable? A: Rectangle flags are fully removable and portable. They can be easily set up and removed. These flag banners are made of lightweight materials and attached to poles that can be easily broken down. You can move them across various locations and commercial trade shows.
- Q: What printing method do you offer for rectangle flags? A: Print247 uses advanced dye-sublimation technology for crisp color printing. This method creates high-resolution graphics and sharp contrast. We customize each flag, matching it with your brand theme and exact colors. Using durable printing and finishes, we ensure our flags do not easily peel or fade.
- Q: What makes rectangle flags better than feather and teardrop flags? A: Rectangle flags are better than feather and teardrop flags because they offer up to 40% more printing space. The flag stays absolutely flat and tight with its horizontal pole arm, so your brand and words are fully visible even with no breeze. Our custom flags with a rectangular shape make it easier to read long text and complex logos from a distance. They give your display a clean, professional look.
