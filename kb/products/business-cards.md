Business cards include standard cards, shape cards and NFC cards.

Card stocks: Cardstock, Kraft, PVC, PET, Acrylic, in 13 PT, 14 PT,
16 PT and 18 PT. Printing: Offset (CMYK), single-sided,
double-sided, digital, any Pantone colour.

Minimum order quantity for business cards: 1000 cards, per the
shapes business cards FAQ.

On approval, a 3D mockup is generated showing the card from every
angle. NFC cards work by tap: the prospect taps their phone and
the profile opens.

Artwork for business cards: AI, PDF and PSD files are accepted
(see facts/artwork.md — PSD is business-card specific).
