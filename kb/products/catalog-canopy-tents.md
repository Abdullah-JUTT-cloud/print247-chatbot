- Q: How should I clean a personalized canopy tent without damaging the print? A: Personalized canopy tents can last longer if you clean them properly. Wash the surface with lukewarm water and mild soap. Use a soft cloth. You should avoid using harsh cleaners and stiff brushes since they will dull the print and roughen up the coating. Let it air dry fully before packing. If it's stored damp, mildew becomes the real enemy, not dirt.
- Q: What is the weight requirement for holding a canopy pop-up tent safely? A: For most setups, plan 25 to 50 lb per leg. Use the higher end for open areas, windy locations, or larger canopy tents. Maintain tight footholds by adding guy lines when the wind picks up. Anchors and weight are the safest setups, not one or the other.
