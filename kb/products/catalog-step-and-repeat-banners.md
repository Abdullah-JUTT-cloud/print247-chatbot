## FAQ
- Q: How to Get Started For Step and Repeat Banners With Print247? A: Here's how you can get started with us. Drop our support team a message with dimensions, venue type, and quantity.
- Q: What materials do you offer for step-and-repeat banners? A: We print on matte stretch polyester and heavy-duty 18-oz vinyl. The durability of polyester makes it ideal for flash photography indoors, while the durability of vinyl makes it ideal for outdoor photography. Both substrates use low-VOC inks and include reinforced edges for repeated installations.
- Q: How large can my custom step and repeat banner be? A: Our equipment handles seamless prints up to ten feet wide; larger walls are tiled in precision-matched panels. Length is virtually unlimited. Just send venue dimensions, and we'll advise the most economical layout, including allowances for stands or truss systems.
- Q: Will colors match my brand guidelines? A: Yes. We calibrate presses daily and accept Pantone, CMYK, or RGB references. Test swatches are shipped on request, and every order includes a digital proof showing repeat alignment and exact color targets.
- Q: How do I set up the banner on-site? A: Each unit ships with labelled top and side edges plus an easy reference sheet. If you have your own frame, slide the pole pockets over the crossbars. For first-time users, we can supply a collapsible aluminium kit; setup typically takes one person five minutes without tools.
- Q: Are fabric step and repeat banners washable? A: Absolutely. We use polyester backdrops that are long-lasting enough for machine washing and tumble drying, so makeup dust and scuff marks will be gone by the time you book the next event. Since dye-sublimated prints embed themselves into the fibres, they won't peel or crack after laundering.
