## FAQ
- Q: What materials can I choose for custom Eco‑friendly Pouches? A: You can specify plain white PET, clear PET, metalized PET, aluminium foil laminations, natural kraft laminations, or a hybrid clear‑plus‑metallised film. Compostable and recyclable grades are offered across most constructions, with gauges from 80 µm to 150 µm to match your barrier needs.
- Q: How low is the minimum order quantity, and can I mix sizes? A: MOQ is 1,000 pieces per SKU. Within that count, you may split runs across different heights, widths, or gusset depths of the same artwork, letting you launch sample, retail, and club packs together while paying the same tooling cost.
- Q: Are the inks and finishes food‑safe? A: Yes. All inks are water‑based or vegetable‑derived, free from BPA, phthalates, and heavy metals. They meet FDA and EU regulations for direct and indirect food contact. Gloss, matte, soft‑touch, and spot UV coatings cure under controlled conditions that lock out residual solvent.
- Q: What lead time should I expect for production and shipping? A: Once artwork is approved, samples ship within 3 to 4 working days. Full production leaves our plant in 12 to 15 working days. Domestic delivery averages three days, while most international lanes arrive within ten, subject to carrier schedules and customs clearance.
- Q: How do these pouches cut our environmental footprint? A: Lightweight films reduce freight emissions versus glass or tin, compostable options return organic matter to soil, recyclable grades stay in material loops, and low‑VOC inks curb factory emissions. Local production slashes transport miles further, delivering a lower cradle‑to‑gate carbon tally.
