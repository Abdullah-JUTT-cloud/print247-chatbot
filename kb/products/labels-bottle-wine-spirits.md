Yes — we print beverage and bottle labels, including wine labels.
Whiskey bottle labels are made the same way: a custom bottle label
in your size, material and finish, so yes, we can print whiskey
bottle labels like wine labels. Tell us your bottle size and the
look you want, and we will quote it like any other bottle label.

Beverage labels on the site include foil options (gold, silver, rose)
with hot-stamp colour locking and no peeling, and are fully
customizable. Clear labels are made from durable BOPP film, are
water-resistant and scratch-resistant, and suit glass and plastic
jars and bottles. Oval labels suit jars, bottles, tins and cosmetic
containers, sized to fit a flat or slightly curved surface without
wrinkling. For products that face water or oils, BOPP or vinyl with a
gloss or matte laminate is usually recommended. Vinyl is weatherproof
and flexible, handles oils and moisture, and comes in gloss or matte.

Context note: whiskey is not listed as a separate product page of its
own — it is covered here because it is a spirit bottle label, made
with the same bottle-label production as wine and other beverage
labels. Do not claim a separate whiskey-only product line exists.
Minimum order follows the general rule: 1000 units. Labels are custom
sized to fit the bottle; roll labels are matched to core size, roll
direction and maximum roll diameter, and sheet labels come in A4 or
letter-size sheets.
