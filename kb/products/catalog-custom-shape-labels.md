## FAQ
- Q: How do I choose between permanent and removable adhesive types for my packaging labels? A: The adhesive choice for custom shape labels depends entirely on the end user experience. Permanent adhesives create a long lasting bond on glass and plastic. They are perfect for retail goods. Removable adhesive allows buyers to peel off the private label stickers easily without scraping off sticky residue.
