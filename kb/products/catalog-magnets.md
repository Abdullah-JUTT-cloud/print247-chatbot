## FAQ
- Q: What business details can be added to custom magnets? A: You can add your brand logo, contact information, business name, and marketing tagline, etc. Our custom magnets are built to increase your brand visibility. Other important elements that you can print include your email address, brand location, and promotional discount text, etc. You can also add social handles and QR codes to directly connect with customers.
- Q: What artwork format should I provide for custom magnets? A: Submit your high resolution vector designs via AI, PDF, EPS, or ZIP files. These formats allow you to customize your magnet according to image size and quality without losing sharpness. Print247 aims to give you consistent branding tools in the form of custom printed magnets.
- Q: Are custom printed magnets weather resistant? A: Yes, our personalized magnets are designed for outdoor use. They are made of durable material with UV resistant and vinyl finishes, making them water-resistant and able to withstand harsh weather conditions. These protective layers prevent the magnet's top layer from fading and scratching. Our custom printed magnets perform reliably on both interior and exterior decorations.
- Q: Do custom magnets ruin the surface when removed? A: No, we use smooth magnetic backing instead of chemical adhesives. Our custom printed magnets do not damage metal surfaces. They are very easy to peel and apply and leave zero sticky residue when removed.
- Q: How long do custom magnets last? A: It depends on the material choices and the surface where the magnets are applied. Our custom magnets generally last between 3 and 5 years when used in outdoor places such as cars or storefronts. They can last up to 10 years when used for indoor displays such as refrigerators, display boards, or cabinet walls.
