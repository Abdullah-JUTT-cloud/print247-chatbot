## FAQ
- Q: Do you offer a high end clothing subscription box? A: Yes, we manufacture a wide range of clothing subscription boxes. Whether you need a custom rigid box or a magnetic closure box, we personalize them according to your brand personality. With Print247, you can fully customize every aspect, including box material, finishes, dimensions, or adds on etc.
- Q: Can I combine multiple finishes on the same packaging? A: We offer custom finishes with multiple combinations to create a high visual appearance. You can opt for a soft touch lamination with a spot gloss for a rich effect or a matte lamination with a hot stamp foil. We ensure your contrast makes your logo and branding elements attention grabbing.
- Q: Is it possible to print my logo outside and a custom design inside? A: Print247 offers both inside and outside printing options. Whether you need a minimal logo on the exterior or a bold design inside the box, we use advanced printing methods to keep your custom visuals crisp. We manufacture custom apparel boxes with personalized graphics to make your business stand out.
- Q: How do foil stamping and embossing work together on one box? A: They work very well together in a process called foil embossing. We transfer metallic foil onto the paperboard using heat and pressure. We utilize a customized metal die to pop that specific foiled area up off the surface. It produces a raised 3D metallic effect that immediately gives a touch of luxury to your brand.
- Q: Do you offer window cut outs to show the garment inside? A: Yes, we cut custom windows on your package to showcase the texture or color of your garment. Using these cutouts gives product visibility, and customers can see it without opening the box. Adding a transparent window enhances the usability of custom packaging.
