- Q: Which Industries Get the Most Value From Linen Business Cards? A: Professionals across various industries use linen texture business cards as they provide the highest ROI. Some industries see a more direct return on investment than others by using these cards. Understanding where your business sits helps you configure the right card for the right audience. A clean layout on heavy linen stock with minimal embellishment is typically preferred by corporate professionals, consultants, and law firms. It communicates the same message as a corner office when the firm name is embossed on 32PT linen with Soft Touch Lamination. For creative professionals such as photographers, brand strategists, architects, and interior designers, the card is an essential portfolio piece. Using a linen card with Spot Foil on the logo gives your clients an insight into working with you.
- Q: Can I order linen business cards in small quantities to test before a large run? A: There is a minimum order quantity of 1,000 linen business cards. The threshold exists since linen cards require specialty stock setup, precise calibration for textured printing, and lamination processes that become cost-effective at volume. Brands are typically able to test 1,000 linen cards across multiple team members and gather feedback before scaling up.
- Q: How does the linen texture affect how readable my text and logo will be? A: The linen texture of cardstock can reduce the sharpness of very fine lines and extremely small text, typically anything below 6pt in size. You should use typefaces with sufficient stroke weight to read clearly on a woven surface. Bold sans-serifs and strong serif fonts perform well. The design support team at Print247 will review your artwork before printing and flag any elements that might not translate well.
- Q: Are linen business cards durable enough for daily use in a wallet or card holder? A: Linen does have this ability, and it is one of its underrated advantages. Unlike uncoated cards, linen paper cards with lamination resist bending, creasing, and surface scuffing better than uncoated cards. In wallets, Soft Touch or Matte Lamination protects the print from moisture. Additionally, painted and gilded edges provide a premium look and protect the card's perimeter.
