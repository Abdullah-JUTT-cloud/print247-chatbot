## FAQ
- Q: Can I remove and reuse these inside-glass decals later? A: In most cases, vinyl options come with a permanent or semi permanent adhesive that is designed for a one time application. They lose their stickiness once removed. We highly recommend choosing static cling material for reusable signage. It can be easily removed and reapplied.
- Q: Are these decals feasible to stick with surfaces other than glass? A: We carefully engineer these decals for glass. However, they will also attach to other surfaces like clear acrylic and smooth polycarbonate. Having a clean, flat, and highly transparent surface is essential for the adhesive to work effectively. Don't apply them on textured surfaces and porous materials.
- Q: How can I prevent air bubbles while applying these decals? A: First clean your glass before applying these front stickers. After cleaning, spray a light mist of soapy water on the window. You can slide the graphic into the exact right position with this liquid barrier. Gently remove the water and air bubbles with a squeegee.
- Q: How long do front adhesive stickers last? A: There are multiple factors that affect the longevity of front side stickers. These factors include sunlight, temperature, and materials. Generally, these stickers can last two to five years with proper UV resistant inks and anti fade coatings.
