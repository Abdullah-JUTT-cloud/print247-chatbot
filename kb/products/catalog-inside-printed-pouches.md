## FAQ
- Q: How is inside printing different from placing a second label inside the pouch? A: A loose label can shift, crease, or absorb oil from the product, ruining legibility inside print bonds graphics directly to the inner laminate during manufacturing. Artwork sits behind a food-safe layer, so colors stay vibrant and text stays readable even against greasy or powdered products.
- Q: Will the inside print affect barrier performance or food safety? A: No. We embed the link between film layers, then apply a heat-sealed lining that still meets FDA food contact rules. Oxygen and moisture pass rates remain identical to standard pouches in the same material and thickness class, so shelf life stays intact.
- Q: Can I mix gloss outside with matte inside on one pouch? A: Yes. Our press lines apply multiple coatings in a single pass. You can pair matte plus spot UV outside with a full-color gloss image inside, without slowing turnaround. Just supply separate art files for each surface, and we handle registration.
- Q: When will I receive the first shipment after artwork approval? A: Digital proofs arrive within 3 to 4 days. Once you sign off, we book the job and ship finished pouches in 12 to 15 days. Local manufacturing cuts ocean transit time, meaning you can have printed packaging pouches on shelves less than a month after final design.
