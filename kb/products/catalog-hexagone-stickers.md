## FAQ
- Q: Can I print multiple designs in one custom hexagon sticker order? A: Usually, you can split an order into several designs within one overall order, such as flavors, SKUs, or messages. We set reasonable minimums per version to keep color and packing efficient. It's a popular approach for subscription boxes, seasonal collections, and event kits.
- Q: Are hexagon stickers suitable for jars and bottles that may get wet or refrigerated? A: We recommend laminated vinyl or coated label material with an adhesive rated for chill or freezer conditions for jars, bottles, and food packs. This helps hexagon labels stay put in fridges, coolers, or damp environments. Send us the storage and washing conditions for the right material.
- Q: Can I write on these hexagon labels with a pen or marker? A: We have matte or uncoated stocks that hold ink from standard pens and markers for handwriting dates, batch codes, or names. We recommend gloss or laminated surfaces for wipe resistance, but permanent markers work better than normal pens.
- Q: Will hexagon wall stickers damage paint when I remove them later? A: We usually suggest a removable adhesive grade, applied only to fully cured, good-quality paint on smooth surfaces for painted walls. When removed slowly at a low angle, most walls hold up well. Poor quality paint can lift in spots, so testing a sample in a discreet area is always wise.
- Q: Do you keep my artwork and shapes on file for future reorders? A: If you order custom hexagon stickers, the artwork, hexagon die line, and job specs are saved on our system. Therefore, your next order can be sent directly to proof and production without much setup time. It is possible to reorder the same job or adjust quantities, materials, or finishes as your needs change.
