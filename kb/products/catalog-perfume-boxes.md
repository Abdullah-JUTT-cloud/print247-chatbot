## FAQ
- Q: Why are Print247's custom perfume boxes the practical luxury choice? A: Print247 creates a unique box for perfume that sells stories, not just scents. We combine production knowledge with brand thinking so that wholesale perfume boxes protect delicate bottles, meet labeling expectations, and create repeatable unboxing experiences that increase perceived value. With white, silver, or holographic rigid board, you can achieve the exact look and feel you want without compromising durability. We take a practical approach to our work. To increase retail appearance and minimize returns, we test structural and insert designs against real bottle geometry and shipping scenarios.
- Q: How should I handle allergen and ingredient labeling on perfume boxes? A: Depending on the market, certain labels will include cosmetic information and allergen disclosures. List ingredients, warnings, and batch data where they can be seen but don't disrupt the brand image, usually on the inside flap or bottom panel. It is easier to avoid last-minute redesigns when planning early.
- Q: Which insert type combines protection with recyclability? A: In terms of sustainability, molded pulp provides strong protection and full recyclability. It is best to use precision foam inserts for uneven or ornate bottle shapes, but they may affect the recyclability of the bottle. To achieve protection and end-of-life goals, we help you find the right compromise.
- Q: Can I serialize limited edition perfume boxes? A: Yes. Serial numbers and authenticity marks are applied via hot-stamp, variable-data printing or labels. During serialized runs, numbering strategy and proofing are important to ensure each unit prints correctly before full production begins.
- Q: What advantages do soy-based inks offer for perfume packaging? A: The use of soy-based inks reduces volatile organic emissions and promotes recycling, compared to petroleum-based inks. Combining them with recycled stocks and appropriate certifications can deliver vivid prints with a lower environmental impact.
- Q: How do you ensure color consistency across large production runs? A: For printing, we use Pantone matches, calibrated plates, and pre-press proofs. We review digital prototypes and physical prototypes before printing. The consistency of brand color across batches is ensured through color checks and sampling during production.
