## FAQ
- Q: Which finish is best for a premium retail look? A: Use gloss or spot finishes to give a reflective look to your product. Add 3D raised UV finishes to highlight the brand logo or other branding elements. If you want to give a premium velvety touch to your packaging, go for soft touch laminations. Custom packaging alone can't capture buyers'attention on a busy shelf. Your product has to grab the client from a distance.
- Q: Why are metallic labels better than foil labels? A: Metallic labels are more durable against scratches and peeling than foil labels. These labels use specific inks to print complex and full colored designs over a shiny surface. On foil labels, you can print only selected areas. Custom printed metallic labels are water resistant and can better handle heavy duty shipping.
- Q: Can I order metallic labels in different sizes and shapes? A: Print247 offers custom sizes and shapes according to your product needs. You can order different product sizes and shapes, including circle labels, oval labels, rectangular and square labels, or custom cut structures.
- Q: Are metallic labels shipment-friendly? A: Yes, these labels are best for long distance shipping. The gloss or matte laminations on these labels create a protective layer against moisture and temperature changes. The strong adhesive material keeps them sticky during rough handling and outdoor placements.
- Q: Can metallic labels solve the problem of limited space for branding? A: Metallic labels are surface friendly for all kinds of spaces. You can paste them on any container. These labels are glossy and make your brand details visible and instantly catch buyers'eyes. Custom metallic labels are an affordable and simple method to help your business stand out.
