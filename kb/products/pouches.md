Mylar bags and pouches include stand-up pouches, flat pouches,
3-side-seal pouches, flat-bottom pouches, spout pouches,
child-resistant pouches, vape pouches, coffee pouches, chocolate
pouches, pre-roll pouches, CBD pouches, inside-printed pouches and
poly mailers.

Pouch films are 80 to 150 microns on pouch pages. Stand-up pouch
stock weights: 1 oz, 2 oz, 4 oz, 8 oz, 12 oz, 16 oz, 32 oz.
Printing: flexographic, gravure, digital. Rotogravure is copperplate
printing and suits large runs; digital printing suits small runs
with zero or very low setup.

Child-resistant zippers use a two-step motion: squeeze both sides,
then slide. Certified child-resistant zippers require two-handed
strength. High-barrier metalized foil gives maximum protection
against oxygen and light for long-term storage.

Food rule from the mylar category: do not store items in mylar
pouches that contain more than 10 percent moisture (stated as a
category rule, not a guarantee for a specific product — confirm
with the team for food use).

Only FDA-approved food-grade materials are used on demand for food
packaging. Pouches are made at the local Rosenberg plant, which has
automated pouch lines.
