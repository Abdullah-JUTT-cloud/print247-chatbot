## Our Story

- Print247 started in 2018 with a straightforward goal: to give businesses of every size access to high-quality custom packaging without the barriers that usually come with it. In the past, small and mid-sized brands struggled to get the same packaging standards as large corporations. We stepped in to change that. As a US-based packaging company, we are committed to quality, consistency, and building packaging solutions that address real business needs.
- Our operations are headquartered in Rosenberg, Texas, where we manage our packaging production and order fulfillment. We ship across the United States through UPS. Every order comes with free shipping nationwide. We keep our production timeline clear and honest. Most custom orders are completed and delivered within 12 to 15 business days. There are no surprises or hidden delays. Our customer service team will let you know exactly what to expect and when to expect it after you place an order at Print247.
