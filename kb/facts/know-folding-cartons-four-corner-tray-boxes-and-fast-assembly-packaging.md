## Article

- A four corner tray box is designed to ship flat and form into shape when opened. The corners are pre-glued during production, so the tray locks automatically when pressure is applied. This removes the need for manual folding or tab locking during packing.
- Once assembled, the base stays flat and the side walls remain upright. The structure holds its square or rectangular shape without additional support. This makes the tray ready for immediate product placement as well as display on POP or retail shelves.

## Why Pre-Glued Corners Matter

- The glued corners provide consistent alignment every time the tray is opened. They prevent the walls from shifting during packing. This helps maintain a clean and stable structure.
- Because the base is already connected, weight is distributed evenly. Products sit flat instead of pushing against the walls. This reduces the risk of bending or collapse.
- The result is a tray that performs the same way across high-volume runs.

## Impact on Packing Speed

- Four corner tray boxes are used when assembly time needs to be minimized. Workers can assemble the tray in one sweep motion without handling or bending multiple panels. This improves packing speed and also reduces errors.
- The open-top design also improves visibility. Products can be placed, adjusted, and aligned quickly. This supports consistent presentation without slowing workflow.
- Flat shipping format saves storage space. Large quantities can be stored before use without taking up volume.

## Common Applications

- Four corner trays are widely used in food packaging and bakery items. They are also used for retail kits and multi-product sets. Any product that needs a stable open base fits well in this structure.
- The tray is often paired with sleeves, lids, or overwraps. This allows protection to be added without changing the base format.

## Conclusion

- Four corner tray boxes focus on speed and structural consistency. Pre-glued corners allow fast setup and reliable shape. The flat base supports stable packing and organized presentation.
