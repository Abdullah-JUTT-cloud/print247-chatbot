## Article

- We see tent cards and standing business cards on tables, counters, and reception desks almost everywhere. These small, self-standing pieces quietly work as powerful mini billboards at the point of sale. These standing business cards give your business a portrayal of uniqueness.
- They deliver your message right where customers are already looking and deciding. In retail, hospitality, events, and offices, tent cards have become an essential marketing tool.

## What Exactly Is a Tent Card or Standing Business Card?

- We define a tent card as a small folded card that stands upright on its own like a miniature tent. It is usually made from thick cardstock, printed on both sides, and scored down the middle so it folds into an A-frame shape.
- Also called standing business cards, table tents, or tabletop signs, they typically measure between 3.5 × 5 inches and 4 × 6 inches when folded. The design allows the card to stand freely on flat surfaces without any additional holder or stand.

## How Tent Cards Work and Why They Are Effective

- We place tent cards exactly where the customer's eyes naturally fall, on restaurant tables, hotel nightstands, checkout counters, conference tables, and reception desks. Mostly standard business card sizes vary but in our guide, we have all important sizes.
- Because they sit at eye level and are impossible to ignore, they deliver your message at the perfect moment.
- They function as silent salespeople that promote specials, upsells, Wi-Fi passwords, QR codes, services, or brand stories while the customer is already seated or waiting.

## Key Features That Make Tent Cards Powerful

- We rely on these characteristics that turn simple cards into highly effective marketing tools:.
- Double-sided printing - both front and back can carry different messages or the same branding.
- Self-standing design - the folded A-frame structure needs no extra hardware to stay upright.
- Premium feel - thick cardstock with soft-touch, spot UV, foil stamping, or embossing creates luxury perception.
- Quick and easy updates - perfect for daily specials, seasonal offers, or limited-time promotions.
- Versatile applications - restaurants, hotels, salons, retail counters, events, weddings, and corporate offices.
- Cost-effective - low production cost with high visibility and long usage time indoors.

## Common Materials and Finishes Used

- We typically print tent cards on 12-24 point cardstock for the best balance of rigidity and elegance.
- Popular finishes include matte, gloss, soft-touch lamination, spot UV, and metallic foil stamping.

## Some versions use recycled or kraft paper for eco-conscious branding

- The scoring and folding must be precise so the card stands straight without sagging over time.

## Strategic Benefits for Retail and Business Success

- We see tent cards increase average order value by promoting desserts, add-ons, or upsells at the exact moment of decision. They reinforce brand identity even when staff are busy.
- QR codes on tent cards drive customers to menus, booking pages, loyalty programs, or websites effortlessly.
- In hotels and restaurants they reduce staff questions by clearly displaying Wi-Fi passwords, hours, or policies.
