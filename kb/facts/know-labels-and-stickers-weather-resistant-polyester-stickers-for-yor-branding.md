## What Are Weather Resistant Polyester Stickers?

- Weather resistant polyester stickers are product stickers made from a strong plastic called Polyethylene Terephthalate. In the business, these stickers are commonly referred to as PET stickers. This material handles extreme stress that would ruin regular paper tags in seconds.
- Paper labels rips easily and turns to mush when wet. But PET labels bring intense tensile strength and dimensional stability to the table. They maintain their shape perfectly and do not tear. In truth, they are even tougher than vinyl stickers.

## Here's how?

- They block water and greasy oils on contact.
- Under pressure, they never shrink, stretch, or wrinkle.
- They can withstand rough shipping routes and heavy handling.

## How Weather Resistant Polyester Stickers Save Your Brand Identity?

- Your packaging is your best silent salesperson. In the event a customer sees a damaged product on a shelf, they are likely to buy it from your competitor instead. Investing in durable polyester labels directly benefits your business.

## Protects Brand Reputation

- Professional sticker design conveys buyers that you take care of the details. A perfect combination of stickers with packaging builds customer loyalty and keeps them coming back for more.

## Keeps Safety Details Legible

- Lots of products need exact warning text or ingredient lists to stay legal. Weather resistant polyester stickers guarantee these tiny words never smudge. This keeps you safe from costly legal issues down the road.

## Reduces Packaging Waste

- Throwing out products just because the packaging looks awful is a massive drain on your wallet. Tough PET stickers stay stuck and look fresh for years. You save serious cash because you do not have to constantly buy replacements or fix ruined inventory. This puts money right back into your business.
