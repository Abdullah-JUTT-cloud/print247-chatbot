Boxes campaign FAQs: minimum order is 1000 customized box units, which
enables wholesale pricing and quality standards. A free digital mockup
is always generated for approval before final printing (the hero also
promises free 3D mockups — keep both wordings separate). Production
typically takes 12-15 working days to complete and ship standard orders;
rush production is offered for wholesale orders with tight deadlines,
with no rush duration stated. Material is chosen by the team from
product weight and shipping conditions, with board thickness reviewed so
nothing arrives damaged. Sustainable options include biodegradable inks
and recycled paper. Boxes ship flat, which massively reduces freight
costs, prevents transit damage, and saves warehouse space until boxes
are folded for fulfilment.
