Site-published payment methods (footer on every page): Visa,
Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal.

Cash on delivery (COD) is not one of the listed payment methods: Visa,
Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, ACH, Wire and Zelle.

Refund page payment methods: ACH, Zelle, Wire Transfer, Zoho
Payments (credit and debit cards).

Internal sales-script facts (state as script rules, not site
claims): payments ACH, Wire and Zelle, plus card links with a 3
percent fee. No payment fee or surcharge is stated anywhere on
the site itself, so never quote the 3 percent fee as a site fact.

Payment is due upon ordering (Terms page); late payment may incur
interest. After initial payment, the packaging design is prepared
free of cost (homepage).
