## Article

- A 1-2-3 bottom box, aka snap lock bottom box, is a folding carton with a manual three step locking base. The bottom is closed by folding flaps in an orderly manner. Each step is necessary to build the final structure.
- The name of this box comes from the 3-step folding process. In the first step, one flap is folded. For the second step, the corresponding side flaps are folded on top of the first one. Then for the final step, the remaining flap comes in interlocking placement to lock firm and hold the structure. This assembling sequence creates a secure and stable base of the box.
- Unlike auto lock bottom boxes, this style relies on guided assembly. The process is quite simple and easy to learn.

## Why This Folding Method Matters

- The step-by-step bottom gives packers more control. Each flap is placed with intention. This helps ensure the box is closed properly every time.
- Because the bottom is folded by hand, adjustments are easy. Packers can feel when the base is secure. This adds confidence during product filling and handling.
- The structure supports consistent shape and balance. Products sit evenly inside the box. This helps prevent tipping or uneven weight distribution.

## Practical Benefits for Brands

- 1-2-3 bottom boxes have always been a reliable and cost-effective packaging choice. They do not require pre-glued bases. This keeps production simple and flexible.
- The design works well for medium-weight products. The locked bottom provides steady support once assembled. It also holds up well during storage and transport.
- These boxes also provide clean surfaces for printing. Branding and product details remain clear and readable. The structure supports neat presentation on shelves.

## Where 1-2-3 Bottom Boxes Are Commonly Used

- 1-2-3 bottom boxes are used across many industries. They are common in retail goods, food items, pharmaceuticals, and personal care products. Stationery and small household items also use this style.
- Brands often choose them when control matters more than speed. They work well in lower to medium-volume packing environments. Both retail and ecommerce businesses use this box type.
