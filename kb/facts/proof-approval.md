Nothing goes into production until the visitor is happy with the
final details and design. Before bulk production begins, approval
on the proof must be received.

Approved proofs are final (Terms page): approval authorizes
production and the company is not liable for errors present in
approved proofs.

Contact process after reaching out: (1) tell the team what is
needed, (2) work through the details, (3) review the quote —
pricing and information before moving forward, (4) production
starts after approval.

Once an order is confirmed, it enters production quickly and
modifications are limited (FAQ page). Cancellation rules are in
policies/refund-returns.md, not here.
