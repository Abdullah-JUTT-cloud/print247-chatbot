## What Are Floor Signs?

- Floor signs are decals or displays for the floor. Some of these signs stick to the surface. Others stand on it, like an A-frame sign. Both types give people a clear message as they walk. These signs are made of durable material that withstands weather changes. You can use them for business promotions within an organization and outdoors as well.

## Types of Floor Signs

- There are different types of floor signs depending on their structure and purpose. Here are the most common kinds.

## Safety and Warning Floor Signs

- These signs flag dangers to keep your staff and visitors safe. Place them in high risk areas to prevent accidents and injury.

## Directional and Wayfinding Floor Signs

- Arrows and aisle markers guide people through your space. They help customers find exits and queues. They also point shoppers to the right department.

## Promotional Floor Graphics

- You can place your logo on the floor. A sales message works well too. Shoppers see it as they walk. You can also lead them to a product display.

## Informational Floor Signs

- "Stand here" markers keep people in order. Waiting line markers do the same job. Your team answers fewer questions at the counter.

## Choose the Right Materials and Finishes

- Before choosing the right material, decide where you will place the sign. Outdoor-rated signs use material that withstands weather and UV exposure. Vinyl is a common choice for adhesive floor decals. It prints well. It also applies easily. A laminate finish protects the print.
- Glossy finishes reduce traction. Use a textured, anti-slip laminate in any area people walk on, and ask for the slip-resistance rating. Freestanding signs use rigid plastic bases. You can move them any time.
