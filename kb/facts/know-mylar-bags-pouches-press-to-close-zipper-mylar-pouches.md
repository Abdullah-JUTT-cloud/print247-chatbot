## Article

- Sealing Mylar bags properly is essential to preserve and protect the products inside. Mylar bags are generally used for long term food storage. When sealed properly, mylar bags offer an airtight closure that keeps products safe from light, moisture, and oxygen. Therefore, businesses use these bags for long term product storage. When you close them accurately, you can guarantee your products remain safe from contamination.
- A press-to-close zipper is a built-in resealable strip that is added to a Mylar pouch. It allows the user to open and close the package numerous times after the first use. In this guide, we will explore how the press to close zipper functions on Mylar bags.

## How the Press to Close Zipper Works on Mylar Bags

- This type of seal is added to the top part of the pouch. It uses two interlocking plastic tracks that are sealed into the inner layer of the Mylar material. When you press along the zipper line, the tracks snap together, creating an airtight seal. To open it, you will have to pull the two sides apart. The interlocking tracks open without damaging the pouch. That way, customers can reseal the packaging for later use.
- In most Mylar pouches, the zipper sits just below a heat-sealed top. This means the pouch is fully sealed during production. In the end, customers tear the top open by using a tear notch, and from that point on, the press-to-close zipper handles reseal.

## Product Freshness

- When the mylar bag is pressed properly, the zipper helps limit exposure to air and moisture between uses. We don't call it a replacement for the original heat seal barrier, but it helps maintain freshness after opening.

## Cost-Effective Closure
