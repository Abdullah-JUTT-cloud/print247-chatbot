## Article

- A pillow pouch is a type of flexible packaging that gets its title from its pillow type shape. When it's filled and sealed with the products, it looks slightly puffy like a pillow. These pouches are usually made from 3 sealed sides, which include top, bottom, and back seals.
- These pouches are made using a single sheet of flexible materials like foil, plastic, or paper, ensuring durability while keeping products safe. These pouches are used for everyday products like coffee, spices, personal care products, and snacks. They have a flat and are lightweight. These pouches cannot sit upright on their own like stand up pouches.

## Benefits of Using a Pillow Pouch

- Pillow pouches are a famous packaging option because they're practical. They offer several advantages, unlike standard packaging solutions. First of all, they use less material compared to rigid packaging, which helps reduce packaging weight and storage space. This makes them more manageable and affordable to transport. They keep products safe from contamination by blocking light, air, and moisture. This way, they help you extend the shelf life of your products.
- They're also flexible in size. Brands can easily adjust the pouch dimensions for single-use, trial packs, or regular retail sizes. They can also customize the shape of their pillow pouches according to their product requirements.
- From a production point of view, pillow pouches are fast to manufacture. High-speed machines can fill and seal thousands of pouches in a short time. For customers, they're easy to handle, lightweight, and simple to open. Overall, pillow pouches are more affordable and reduce transportation costs.

## How a Pillow Pouch Is Made

- A pillow pouch is made from a roll of multilayered packaging film. The film is then wrapped around the product and sealed vertically from the back. It is then filled with the product, and then sealing the top and bottom horizontally.
- The materials used can vary according to business requirements. Businesses can choose among plastic films, foil or paper depending on their product and brand goals.

## Conclusion

- A pillow pouch is a simple and practical packaging format. It's designed to protect products while using minimal material and space. Because it's lightweight, easy to produce, and cost-effective, it works well for many everyday items, especially food and single-use products.
- If a product doesn't need to stand upright or be resealed, a pillow pouch is often the right choice.
