Accepted artwork formats: AI, PDF, EPS or ZIP on most product
pages. Business cards also accept PSD — PSD is business-card
specific, not general.

Cosmetic boxes: artwork includes 3mm bleed and live text should
remain within the safe area.

Child-resistant pouches: the production crew preflights files and
fixes unnoticed errors.

No artwork submission deadline was found anywhere on the site —
never invent one; if asked, hand off to the team.

Printing: CMYK plus Pantone/PMS matching, offset, digital and
flexographic for food.
