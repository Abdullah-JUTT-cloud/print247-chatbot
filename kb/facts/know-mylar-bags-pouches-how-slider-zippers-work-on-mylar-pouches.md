## Article

- Slider zippers are the most common closure system that is used mostly on Mylar bags. They are used to make opening and resealing easier, which helps enhance customer experience. They are known as a plastic slider device that is mechanically used to lock and unlock through one interlocking track. The track itself is attached to the inside top area of the pouch.
- This mechanism allows customers to use the pouch multiple times after it has been opened. This type of locking mechanism is used where the products need to be accessed more than once while making sure they remain safe from contamination.

## How Does the Opening Process Work?

- Many companies add a sealed top section above the zipper in a mylar bag. The heat sealed section helps keep the product safe from external factors like light, moisture, and oxygen before the first use. Customers are required to tear the top along a tear notch or perforation.
- Once the sealed top strip is removed, customers can easily access the slider zipper. The user can move the slider across the tacks to separate the zipper layer. This makes it easier for them to access the products in an easy way.

## How to Reseal the Pouch

- You must be wondering about how to reseal the pouch once it's opened. Well, it's no rocket science. All you have to do is use the slider to reseal the mylar bag just like you did for opening. The user can slide the tab back across the zipper track.
- When the slider moves, it presses the two zipper tracks together again, creating a tight seal that keeps products safe inside the pouch for multiple uses.
- The resealing process is quick and does not require pressing along the entire zipper by hand.

## Where Slider Zippers Are Commonly Used

- If you are a brand that prioritizes customer convenience more than anything else, then slider zipper closures are your best choice. Slider zippers are mostly found in stand up pouches and many other flexible packaging formats.
- Products that use slider zipper pouches include snacks, coffee, dried food, powder, and household items. These products cannot be consumed at once; therefore slider allows the pouch to be opened and closed without ruining the product's quality or damaging the packaging.

## Perks of Using Slider Zipper on Mylar Bags

- Using a slider zipper on Mylar bags offers several amazing benefits. The following are the benefits of adding a slider zipper to a Customized Mylar bag:.
- Convenience: Slider bags offer convenience. They allow users to quickly open and close the bags without needing any additional tools.
- Superior Airtight Barrier: It offers an airtight barrier against light and moisture, avoiding spills after opening.
- Durability: Slider zipper mylar bags are typically made using high quality and durable packaging materials that are puncture resistant and are designed to withstand repeated use.
