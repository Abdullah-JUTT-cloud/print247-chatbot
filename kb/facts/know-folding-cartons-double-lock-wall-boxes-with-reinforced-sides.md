## Article

- A double lock wall box is built for strength and weight support. Its side walls are folded twice, creating two layers of corrugated board on each side. Plus, the top lid has dust flaps in their structure that closes in these double layer corrugated walls to give double the structural durability. Moreover, these layers are secured using locking tabs that hold the panels in position.
- The locking points connect the inner and outer walls. This prevents the sides from spreading under pressure. Once assembled, the box forms a rigid frame around the product.
- Because the walls are doubled, the structure resists bending during handling. The top and bottom closures remain aligned even when the box is lifted.

## Load Capacity and Stability

- This box style is used when products are heavier than standard retail items. The reinforced walls distribute weight evenly across the structure. This reduces stress on the corners and base.
- The bottom panel stays flat and stable during packing. Products can be placed inside without the box collapsing inward. This improves safety and reduces the risk of product damage.
- Stacking performance is another key advantage. The double walls help the box maintain shape under vertical load. This makes it suitable for storage and transport environments.

## Assembly Process and Workflow

- Double lock wall boxes ship flat to save space. During assembly, the side panels are folded inward and locked into place. The locking sequence creates the layered wall structure.
- Although the setup has more steps than simple cartons, it remains predictable. Once formed, the box does not require additional reinforcement.
- Packers benefit from the stable cavity created after assembly. Products stay centered and do not press against the outer walls.

## Typical Applications

- These boxes are commonly used for tools, glass containers, hardware, and bulk retail goods. They are also suitable for shipping multiple items in one package.
- In e-commerce, they protect products that need extra side support. In industrial settings, they help contain heavier components safely.
- The reinforced design makes them reliable for long-distance transport.

## Branding and Surface Use

- The outer panels provide large, uninterrupted areas for printing. Because the walls stay rigid, graphics remain aligned and visible. This supports a professional appearance even in demanding conditions.
- Interior printing can be added for organized product placement. The structure keeps items separated and properly positioned.
