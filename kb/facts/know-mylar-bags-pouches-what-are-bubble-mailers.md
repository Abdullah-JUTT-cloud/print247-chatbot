## Article

- Bubble mailers are padded envelopes commonly used for shipping items. Many of you've probably received one before. They are lightweight envelopes and have bubbles inside that help keep the products secure during shipping and transit. They are uncomplicated, useful, and widely used for various purposes. In this guide, we will discuss what bubble mailers are, how they are utilized, and why businesses depend heavily on them.

## Exploring What Bubble Mailers Are?

- A bubble mailer is a padded envelope that is mostly made from plastic and paper, with a layer of air filled bubble on the inside. The outer layer provides structure. On the other hand, the inner layer provides the mailer with its protective qualities. The bubble lining acts as a cushion and protects the products inside. Along with these we also make custom poly mailers of the pristine materials.
- Bubble Mailers come with a self sealing adhesive strip. That means you don't need tape to close the bag. The sizes of the bags can vary. You can order small envelopes for jewelry or larger bubble mailer envelopes for books or electronics accessories. There are two main types of bubble mailers:.
- Poly Bubble Mailers: They have plastic on the outside and bubble padding on the inside.
- Paper Bubble Mailers: They have kraft paper on the exterior and a plastic bubble on the inside.
- They are both employed to serve the same goal. You have to select carefully based on how much product protection, flexibility, or moisture resistance you need.

## Why Businesses Rely on Bubble Mailers

- The biggest reason is practicality. Bubble mailers protect products without adding bulk or weight.

## They are:

## Lightweight, which helps reduce shipping costs

## Easy to store, since they don't take up much space

## Quick to pack, saving time during order fulfillment

- For eCommerce sellers shipping dozens or thousands of orders, these small advantages matter.

## How Bubble Mailers Keep Items Safe

- Bubble Mailers are widely used for their amazing protective qualities. As we have said before, they have a bubble lining that can absorb shock. If the package is dropped or pressed, the air locks help reduce impact. The outer layer also works to add a powerful barrier against dust, dirt, and moisture exposure.
- This makes them an excellent choice to ship products safely to their destination. It is important to note that bubble mailers can't protect fragile items like glass or heavy electronic items. They can be used for everyday small products.

## Are Bubble Mailers Recyclable

- Bubble mailers are normally not recyclable in curbside bins. That's because they are made of mixed materials that jam the recycling machinery. Poly bubble mailers are difficult to recycle. On the other hand, bubble mailers made using only paper can be easily recycled.
