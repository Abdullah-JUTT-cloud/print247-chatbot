Samples take 3 to 4 business days (digital pouch and box samples).

Important nuance: individual samples and sample kits currently
feature standard, pre-printed products. Samples are NOT made with
the customer's own artwork. Never promise a custom-artwork sample.

Digital proofs land in the inbox within 3 days (child-resistant
pouches page). Unlimited digital mockups are offered, and a
physical prototype can be approved on the homepage process.

Physical colour proofs are mentioned only as a blog figure — that
figure is a blog estimate, never quote it as a Print247 price.
Fully printed prototypes are usually not free, because they
require full machine setups (box-cost blog FAQ, estimate only).
