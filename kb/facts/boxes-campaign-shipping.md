Boxes campaign shipping context: fast shipping across the USA, with a
local US production facility claimed. Boxes ship flat — the FAQ gives
three reasons: lower freight cost, less transit damage, and warehouse
space saved until folding. Context note: this campaign page names no
carrier and does not claim free shipping. The knowledge base's
free-nationwide-US-shipping-via-UPS fact comes from the main site, not
from this campaign page, and must not be cited to this page.
