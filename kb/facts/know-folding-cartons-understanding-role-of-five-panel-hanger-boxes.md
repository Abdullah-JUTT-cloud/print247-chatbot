## Article

- Five panel hanger boxes are specially designed folding cartons used for hanging display of products. It includes an extended panel on top having a die-cut hang hole. This allows the box to be placed on peg hooks or retail hanging systems.
- The structure uses five main panels instead of the standard four. That extra panel adds strength and supports the hanger area. The result is a box that stays upright while suspended.
- These boxes are made to make best use of limited space on shelves. They move products off flat surfaces and into the customer's direct view. This makes them ideal for fast-scanning retail environments.

## Why Brands Choose Five Panel Hanger Boxes

- Five panel hanger boxes are designed to increase product outreach in retail setting. Their hanging feature keeps items at eye level of onlookers. Customers can easily spot products without bending or searching.
- The added panel also improves durability. The hanger area stays reinforced under product weight. This prevents tearing during long retail display periods.
- Another advantage is controlled presentation. Products hang evenly and face forward. This keeps branding visible and consistent across displays.
- These boxes also help retailers stay organized. Products remain separated and easy to restock. The hanging format reduces shelf clutter.

## Common Products That Use Hanger Boxes

- Electronics businesses mostly use five panel hanger boxes. Products like cables, earbuds, and small accessories fit well inside them. The hanging hole keeps packaging compact and visible.
- Hardware and tools also rely on this box style. Fasteners, small tools, and repair items stay accessible. Customers can compare options quickly.
- In personal care, hanger boxes hold grooming products and refills. The upright position keeps information readable. This helps buyers make faster decisions.
- Automotive accessories often use this packaging. Small car care items stay organized in busy aisles. The hanger design prevents items from being misplaced.

## Design and Printing Considerations

- The extended top panel must stay strong and balanced. Material choice is important for weight support. The ideal cardstock thickness is selected based on product size.
- Hang holes can be round, euro-style, or custom cut. Each option supports different retail systems. The design remains flexible without changing the box structure.
- Printing focuses on front-facing clarity. Branding and key details are placed where customers look first. Side panels are used for instructions or specifications.
- Clean layout works best in hanging displays. Customers usually view the box quickly. Clear information helps them decide faster.
