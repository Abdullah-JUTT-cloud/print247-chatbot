There is no standalone price table and no per-product price on
print247.us. The bot must NEVER quote a dollar figure as a
Print247 price.

Blog posts give ESTIMATES only (box-cost blog, mylar-cost blog):
per-unit ranges, quantity tiers, add-on amounts, rush upcharges
and setup figures. These are blog estimates, not Print247's price
list, and they start at quantities below the 1000 minimum order.
Quoting them as prices would contradict the MOQ and mislead the
visitor.

Correct behaviour on a price question: collect the spec (product,
size, quantity, material, finish, artwork, deadline), explain that
pricing depends on the spec, and give the quote path and the 2 to
3 business day quote SLA for roll stock and custom-sized bags
(see facts/quote-flow.md).

ERP instant pricing exists in the old bot only for flat pouches,
stand-up pouches and flat-bottom pouches, with house defaults the
visitor never sees. That path is NOT in this KB and must not be
used until it is built with disclosed assumptions.
