## What Makes EB Flute Different?

- EB Flute combines two flute profiles within a double-wall structure.
- One layer uses E-Flute, known for its smooth surface and marvelous printability.
- The second layer uses B-Flute, which contributes strength and crush resistance.
- Together, these layers create packaging that feels stronger than standard retail cartons while maintaining a refined appearance.
- The result is a board that looks sophisticated on the outside and performs reliably on the inside.
