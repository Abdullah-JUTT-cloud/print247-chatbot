## Article

- The front side is smooth and suitable for printing, while the rear side keeps the natural strength of kraft fibers. This creates a material that is both functional and presentable.
- It is often chosen when brands need something stronger than recycled boards but more affordable than fully bleached paperboard.

## How It Performs in Real Use

- CCKB offers better strength than lighter materials, which means it can handle moderate weight and rough handling conditions. It does not feel as heavy as some premium boards, but it provides enough support for many types of products.
- The coated front allows for clear printing. Designs appear visible and clean, making it suitable for retail packaging.
- At the same time, the kraft back adds durability. This helps the box stay firm during storage and transport.
- Because of this combination, CCKB works well in situations where both strength and appearance are needed, but neither needs to be extreme.

## Where CCKB Is Commonly Used

- CCKB is widely used in food packaging, especially for frozen or packaged goods. These products need a material that can handle storage conditions while still looking presentable.
- It is also used in beverage packaging, where moderate strength is required to hold items securely.
- In retail packaging, CCKB is chosen for products that need a good shelf appearance without increasing costs too much.
- This makes it a flexible option across different industries.

## Box Styles and Shapes

- CCKB works well with structured packaging styles that need both support and display value.
- Common styles include seal end cartons, which provide secure closure for food and retail products.
- It is also used in tuck end boxes and multi-pack cartons, where both strength and presentation are important.
- Shapes are usually practical and easy to stack. This helps in shipping, retail display, and storage.
- While it can support some design variation, it is mainly used for efficient and functional packaging structures.

## Final Perspective

- CCKB offers a balanced solution for brands that need both durability and a presentable surface. It does not focus only on strength or only on appearance, but instead provides a mix of both.
- This makes it a reliable choice for many everyday packaging needs.
