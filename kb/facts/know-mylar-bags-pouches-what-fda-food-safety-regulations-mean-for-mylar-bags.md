## Article

- Mylar bags are a popular packaging choice and are widely used to pack a variety of products, especially food items. They help keep food products safe from air, moisture, and light, preventing contamination and spoilage. However, since these bags are used to store food items, they must follow specific food safety standards. That's where the FDA food contact regulation becomes important.
- The FDA (Food and Drug Administration) set rules that all packaging materials that come into direct contact with food items do not release any harmful chemicals into the food during storage. They must not contaminate the quality of food or make it harmful for consumption.

## What Does FDA Compliance Mean for Mylar Bags?

- When it comes to Mylar bags, FDA compliance means materials used to make these flexible pouches are safe for direct food contact. This includes plastic films, printing inks, adhesives, coatings, and inner layers. It is important that a food safe mylar bag should not:.

## Are Printing Inks and Coatings Also Important?

- Yes. FDA food contact regulations do not only apply to the inner film of the pouch. Manufacturers use food-safe inks to print on mylar bags. Printing inks, laminations, adhesives, and coatings must also be eligible for food packaging applications. Businesses use food safe inks to prevent migration to ensure mylar bags are not toxic for food.
- For example, low-migration inks are often used in custom printed mylar bags to reduce the chance of chemicals transferring through the packaging layers.
