## How X-Stand Banners Differ from L-Stand Banners

- We note X-Stands use cross frames for better stability in breezy spots.
- X versions allow faster graphic swaps since corners hook on easily.
- X-Stands often cost less, making them budget picks for frequent users.
- X frames provide even tension across all sides, reducing wrinkles better.
- These differences help choose based on space stability and setup needs.
