## Article

- Metalized PET is a flexible packaging material widely used in mylar bags, pouches, and laminated films. It is often produced by coating the PET film with a very thin layer of aluminum. This metal layer is applied using the process called vacuum metallization. This method involves vaporizing aluminum and depositing it onto the surface of the PET film. That's how this lightweight material is formed. It offers strong barrier protection and flexibility. Metallized PET is primarily used in packaging applications that require supreme protection against oxygen, light, and moisture.

## How Metallized PET is Made

- You must be wondering how metallized PET film is produced. Well, it is produced by coating a thin layer of aluminum onto a PET film, which is the base material. This base material is famous for its strength, clarity, and stability. During metallization, the PET film is placed in a vacuum chamber where aluminum is warmed until it vaporizes. The vapor then settles on the film's surface, creating a thin reflective metal coating.
- This metal layer is incredibly thin but significantly improves the barrier performance of the film. After metallization, the film is often laminated with other materials such as polyethylene (PE) or polypropylene (PP). Lamination helps enhance sealing power and overall durability.
