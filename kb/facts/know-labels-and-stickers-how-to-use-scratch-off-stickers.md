## Article

- Scratch off stickers have become a versatile tool that can be used in a broad range of applications. They are not just ordinary stickers but a feeling of surprise used for promotions, games, business rewards, and much more. The stickers will add a fresh layer of marketing to your product-based brand, retail store, or e-commerce business without breaking your budget. In this blog, we are going to discuss where you can use these scratch off stickers as a business owner or brand manager.
- Let's start.

## 5 Smart Ways To Use Scratch Off Stickers For Your Business

- Business owners are always looking for the ways that promote their business and take sales to the next level. Among them, labels and stickers are a reliable way that engages customers and prompts them for purchase. There are many creative ways brands add scratch-off stickers to their marketing strategy.
- Here are 5 practical approaches in this matter. Let's review one by one.

## 1) Mystery Discount Codes For Repeat Purchases

- Like every business owner, you also want your customers to return to make repeat transactions. For that, you can use custom scratch off stickers to hide discount codes inside your packaging or mailers. Customers who scratch to find "15% off your next order" are three times more likely to come back. Regardless of whether you are a subscription box provider, beauty brand, or a retail store, this option works well. This creates an incentive for a second purchase without feeling like a promotion. In addition, it feels fun and personal.

## 2) Interactive "Scratch-to-win" Giveaways

- Giveaways are undoubtedly crucial marketing tools to increase brand loyalty and get attention. Scratch-to-win setups take giveaways to the next level. You can attach scratch-off stickers to cards or packaging and let customers scratch to see if they'll win a free product, upgrade, or gift. It's a great idea businesses can use for trade shows, retail locations, and even e-commerce packages. This creates excitement around your brand and gets people talking about it. There is no need to spend more on advertising when people talk about your brand.

## 3) Hidden QR Codes for Exclusive Digital Content

- Hidden QR codes are a smart move for brands that connect physical products. These codes create a sense of mystery and boost engagement. Place a QR code under the scratch panel on your package or product inserts. Scratching and scanning leads to an exclusive content page, loyalty portal, or special offer. Thus, you can connect your physical product with your digital experience.

## 4) Product Variation Announcements

- Are you launching a new flavor, color, or size? You can build suspense before your big reveal by using scratch off stickers. You can conceal the new variation name or color by placing heart scratch off stickers on the packaging. You never know what new thing you're going to discover when customers scratch. Food and beverage manufacturers, cosmetics manufacturers, and apparel manufacturers industries can benefit from this. It makes launching a product a mini event that people celebrate and share online.

## 5) Limited-Edition Collectible Campaigns

- The use of collectible campaigns is a great way to keep customers interested. Using scratch off stickers or fully printed scratch off designs, make a series of images, characters, or rewards that are revealed when you scratch them off. It's like trading cards branded with your brand. Customers who want to collect all the designs will keep buying. It is ideal for seasonal promotions, brand anniversaries, and products aimed at younger audiences or gift buyers.
