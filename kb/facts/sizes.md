Sizes are dimensions with units, for example 4x6x2 inches or
100x150mm, written as width x height x depth.

A pack count is NOT a size and NOT a quantity: "2 count", "10 ct",
"5-pack" and "30 capsules per bag" say how many items go inside
each bag, not how big the bag is or how many bags to order. Leave
sizes and quantity unanswered for a pack count alone.

Pouch stock weights: 1 oz, 2 oz, 4 oz, 8 oz, 12 oz, 16 oz, 32 oz.
Large-format mylar: 5 gallon 20-30 inches, 5.0 to 7.0 mil; 55 gallon
38-54 inches, 10 to 12 mil.

Business cards: 13 PT, 14 PT, 16 PT, 18 PT (thickness, not size).
Box board: 12 to 24pt generally.

If a visitor gives a size without a unit, ask for the unit instead
of assuming inches or mm.
