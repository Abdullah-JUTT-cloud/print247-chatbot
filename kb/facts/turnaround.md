Standard turnaround is 12 to 15 business days, including production
and delivery to the doorstep.

Breakdown from the Shipping Policy: after design approval, production
moves forward within 48 hours, manufacturing takes 4 to 5 business
days, UPS transit takes 6 to 7 business days across the USA.

Production starts within 24 hours of final artwork approval in the
old sales script. The 24 hours is the START, not the total — never
promise delivery in 24 hours.

Expedited orders: the Shipping Policy says expedited orders can be
completed in 5 to 7 business days, with timelines varying by size,
materials and complexity. The old sales script says rush orders are
10 to 12 business days and the production slot must be checked first.
Context: the policy figure is the published expedited range; the
script figure is the old rush-production figure. A rush date is
therefore never a commitment in chat; it goes to the team to confirm.
A blog post mentions a 20 to 30 percent rush upcharge — that is a
blog estimate only, never quote it as a Print247 fee.
