Standard turnaround is 12 to 15 business days, including production and
delivery to the doorstep.

Production starts within 24 hours of final artwork approval. The 24 hours
is the START, not the total — never promise delivery in 24 hours.

Rush orders are 10 to 12 business days in the old sales script, but the
script itself says the production slot must be checked first. A rush date
is therefore never a commitment in chat; it goes to the team to confirm.
