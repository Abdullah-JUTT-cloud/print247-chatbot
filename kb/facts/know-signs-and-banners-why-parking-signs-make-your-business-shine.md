## The Art of Sublimation: Printing Signs That Brave the Harsh Outdoor Elements

- Sublimation uses heat to turn solid ink into a deep, penetrating gaseous state.
- This process bonds the vibrant pigments directly into the specialized polyester coating layer.
- You first print your unique design onto a high-quality heat transfer paper sheet.
- The aluminum blank is then pressed firmly against the paper at high temperatures.
- Resulting signs are incredibly smooth and resistant to fading from the bright sun.
- This method ensures that your graphics will never peel or crack over time.

## Mastering the Impeccable Heat Transfer Process for Durable Metal Signage
