## Article

- A child resistant closure on mylar bags is a special type of opening designed specifically to be difficult for young kids to open. It can still be accessible for adults. This type of packaging adds an additional layer of protection by requiring a specific opening method. These closures are mostly used in Mylar bags and hold products that should not be consumed by children. The goal is simple: to reduce the chance of accidental poisoning while keeping the packaging functional for everyday use. In this guide, we'll discuss how child resistant packaging works and the benefits of using it.

## How a Child Resistant Closure Works?

- Child resistant closure uses two different opening mechanisms, typically known as push and slide, or pinch and pull zipper systems. These locking mechanisms are not like regular ziplock closures. These closure systems require two actions at the same time. For a pinch and pull, child resistant lock, you need to pinch specific points on the zipper before sliding it open.
- This step is normally used to stop children who lack hand strength and coordination. Children lack the ability to figure out how to open a package. Adults can easily figure out how to open and close the bag after a brief try.
- Once sealed, the closure clicks or locks back into place, helping ensure the bag stays securely closed.
