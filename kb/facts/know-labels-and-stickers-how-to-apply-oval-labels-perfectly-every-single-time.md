## Article

When applying oval stickers, placement is more important than most brands realize. In this blog, we are going to provide you with step-by-step instructions on how to apply oval labels perfectly, every time, without bubbles or guesswork.

## What Are Oval Labels?

- An oval label is an adhesive label that has been die-cut into an oval shape. Brands use them for packaging, such as bottles, jars, candles, food boxes, and cosmetics. Labels with oval shapes give products a polished, premium appearance. Whether it's a paper label, a vinyl label, or a clear label, they have a style for you.

## Step By Step Guide to Apply Oval Labels Perfectly

- The process of applying labels might seem straightforward, but it's not easy to make them perfectly straight each time. Using these five steps, packaging teams apply oval printable labels cleanly, without bubbles or misalignments.

## 1. Prepare the Packaging Surface

- This step is often skipped, which is the cause of most label problems. You should make sure the surface of your packaging is free of dust, dry, and free of dust and oils before you peel and stick anything. When cleaning glass, plastic, or metal, use isopropyl alcohol. Be sure to let it dry completely. The shape of the labels matters a lot when applied to plastic containers or clear oval labels on glass. The bond between two surfaces is stronger and the finished look is much nicer with a clean surface.

## 2. Identify the Vertical Center Line

- It is crucial to place labels accurately when applying them. You need to locate the vertical center line of the packaging before you stick the label on. If you are measuring bottles and jars, you can use a center-line tool or simply a ruler. If necessary, mark a faint pencil line. By doing this, you can align oval stickers straight down the middle without having to guess.

## 3. Use the Center-Out Application Method

- In this method, air bubbles are significantly reduced when printing oval labels. Working from the center outward, start at the center of the label. The first step is to peel back a small section of the liner. The center of the label should be pressed firmly onto the surface. Once the rest of the liner has been peeled, slowly press down from the center toward the edges as you peel the remaining liner. With this method, you can place the label exactly where you want it and prevent it from folding or wrinkled during application.

## 4. Smooth Toward the Rounded Edges

- It is especially important to pay attention to the curved ends of oval-shaped labels. When the center of the label has been pressed down, use a squeegee or your fingernail to smooth it out toward the rounded ends. Throughout the process, work slowly and evenly. In this way, any remaining air bubbles are removed, and the edges of the label are kept flat without lifting. When printing oval printable labels on curved containers, such as bottles, press firmly as you go to avoid edge lift.

## 5. Inspect for Sincerity and Seal

- After the label has been fully applied, inspect it carefully. Consider the placement from multiple angles. Be sure it is centered, flat, and fully sealed on all sides. To fix any loose spots, run your finger along the entire border. With this quick quality check, you can prevent product returns and protect your brand presentation during high-volume labeling runs. You demonstrate your brand's commitment to quality by applying custom oval labels with care.
