## Article

- Side gusset pouches are a kind of flexible packaging that is expandable on the sides. These expandable folds are known as gusset pouches. When the pouch is filled with the products, these gussets open outward. This allows bags to hold more volume while still looking slim from the front and back. These pouches have a flat bottom, which allows them to sit upright.
- Side gusset pouches are mostly used to pack tea, coffee, dry food, pet treats, and industrial products.

## How Side Gusset Pouches Work

- A side gusset pouch works by adding a gusset to the sides that allows the pouch to expand when loaded with products. It is flat when empty. Once the product is filled inside, the sides of these pouches expand, creating a box like shape. This design helps the pouch stand more upright when packed tightly in cartons or placed on shelves. Their self standing bottom makes them an excellent option to boost product visibility.
- They can be heat sealed at the top and bottom to make an airtight closure. Brands can also include features like resealable zippers, tear notches, or degassing valves to enhance customer experience.

## Exploring the Advantages of Using Side Gusset Pouches

- Side gusset pouches offer so many amazing benefits. One of the best benefits is capacity. The gussets allow more product to fit into the pouch without increasing its front-facing size. Let's explore more amazing benefits of using gusset pouches:.
- These pouches expand on filling, which allows them to hold more product than a standard flat pouch.
- They use fewer materials, which lowers the production costs.
- They are lightweight and easy to transport, which decreases shipping expenses.
- They offer excellent barrier protection when made with laminated films.
- They are fully customizable when it comes to size, material, and printing.
- Side gusset pouches are a versatile packaging alternative and can be used to fill a wide variety of products. They can be easily customized with resealable features for user experience.
