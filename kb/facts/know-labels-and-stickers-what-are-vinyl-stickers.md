## What Are Vinyl Stickers?

- Vinyl stickers are adhesive labels that are built from PVC (polyvinyl chloride). PVC is an economical synthetic thermoplastic polymer derived from salt. These stickers are printed with the finest ink and designed to stick to nearly any surface. Companies use custom vinyl stickers to label products, package them, promote brands, and give them away as giveaways.
- Average vinyl label lifespan is 3-7 years, which is higher than you might expect. Based on your preferences, you can choose from a variety of shapes, sizes, and finishes. Moreover, they are tough enough to withstand heat, wear, and outdoor conditions without its appearance altering. It makes them an ideal investment for any company looking to brand itself.

## How To Apply Vinyl Stickers To Any Surface?

- You don't have to be an expert to apply stickers to vinyl surfaces or any other surface. Making sure it's done right the first time will keep them looking sharp and clean for a long time. They are able to stick to any surface, including wood, glass, plastic, metal, and ceramic. But remember that, a clean and smooth surface is always a great choice for optimal performance.
- Follow these simple steps to place vinyl stickers.
- Wipe the surface with a soft cloth before applying the adhesive.
- Gently peel off the sticker backing.
- Press the sticker down carefully after aligning it.
- To remove air bubbles, press outward from the center.
- Apply a squeegee or a card edge to smooth down the edges.
- Keep it away from water, heat, and direct sunlight for at least 24 hours.
- A clean application makes a big difference. When custom vinyl stickers are applied properly, they stay put for years and give your brand a professional, polished look every time. When applying stickers on window or glass surfaces, clean the surface with soapy water or any cleaning product for better application.

## Are Vinyl Stickers Waterproof?

- Yes. Vinyl stickers are waterproof as they are created from materials that are usually waterproof. This is the reason most businesses prefer them over paper labels. These waterproof labels easily handle rain, humidity, and moisture without peeling. Manufacturers apply a protective and water impermeable layer that shields these stickers against water and scratches. Avoid washing these stickers with dishwater as they can cause peel or fade. In some waterproof vinyl stickers, ink is not laminated so be careful about it.
