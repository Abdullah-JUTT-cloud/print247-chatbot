## Article

- Mylar bags are employed to pack a wide variety of products. Mylar bags are made of multiple layers that keep the content inside safe from light, moisture, and oxygen. These layers play a major role in long term preservation of products. One of these layers is a nylon layer. It acts as a high performance strength and gas barrier layer.
- This barrier layer is highly puncture resistant and can absorb physical stress. It helps stop oxygen and aromas from passing through. Let's discuss what role nylon (barrier film) plays in Mylar packaging.

## Why is Nylon Used in Mylar Bags?

- Nylon is primarily used in multi layer mylar bags. It provides remarkable puncture resistance, protection against external factors, and flexibility. This layer helps prevent sharp items from tearing the inner foil of mylar bags.
- That is the reason Nylon barrier film is used in mylar bags. But it is important to know that the Nylon layer is only added to the heavy duty and specialized industrial pouches for maximum puncture resistance.

## The Benefits of Using Nylon in Mylar Packaging

- Let's discuss the amazing benefits of using nylon (barrier film) in mylar packaging:.
- Nylon adds high tensile toughness to the mylar packaging. That's how it helps protect mylar pouches from sharp edges, industrial parts, or bones in meat. It prevents tears and punctures.
- It also acts as a molecular shield that works perfectly well with aluminum or PET layers. It helps block light, moisture, and air, which ultimately prevents contamination.
- This layer is highly flexible and durable and handles stress and thermoforming without losing its structural integrity under intense vacuum pressure.
