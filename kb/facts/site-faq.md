## Boxes & Packaging
- Q: What types of packaging materials are commonly used for boxes? A: Cardboard, corrugated cardboard, paperboard, plastic and metal; the choice depends on the product's nature, weight and shipping requirements.
- Q: Can I customize the design of my boxes? A: Box size, material and colours can be chosen, and branding added through logos and artwork.
- Q: Can I order the packaging in bulk? A: Bulk ordering is offered and can be cost-effective, subject to storage space, lead time and storage conditions.
- Q: How can I determine the correct box size for my product? A: Measure the product dimensions and weight, then choose a box with room for padding while minimising excess space.
- Q: Are there packaging solutions suitable for fragile items? A: Bubble wrap, foam inserts or air pillows add cushioning; double boxing is named for exceptionally fragile items.

## Mylar Bag
- Q: Can Mylar bags be used for vacuum sealing? A: Some Mylar bags are designed for vacuum sealing, but not all; the answer says to check the product specifications.
- Q: What are the advantages of using Mylar bags for packaging? A: Barrier properties, light weight, durability and flexibility, with resistance to punctures, tears and moisture.
- Q: Can I print my logo or design on Mylar bags? A: Many manufacturers offer custom printing of logos, branding or designs on Mylar bags.
- Q: What types of products are commonly packaged in Mylar bags? A: Dry foods, coffee, nuts, herbs, spices, pharmaceuticals, electronics and other items needing environmental protection.

## Labels & Stickers
- Q: What is the difference between labels and stickers? A: Labels are the broader category for information or branding applied to a product or packaging; stickers are usually removable and used on various surfaces.
- Q: Are labels and stickers waterproof? A: It depends on the material; vinyl and polyester are more water-resistant than paper, and materials designed for wet or outdoor use should be chosen for waterproof applications.
- Q: What materials are commonly used for labels and stickers? A: Paper, vinyl, polyester and others, chosen by application, durability requirement and environment.
- Q: Can labels and stickers be used for promotional purposes at events or on products? A: Yes, for promotional giveaways, event branding or highlighting special offers.

## Signs & Banners
- Q: What is the primary purpose of using signs and banners for businesses? A: Brand promotion and advertising, plus brand visibility.
- Q: Are your signs and banners suitable for both indoor and outdoor use? A: Options are offered for indoor events and for durable outdoor signage that withstands different environmental conditions.
- Q: Are your banners and signs eco-friendly? A: Eco-friendly options are offered, with guidance on sustainable materials and printing processes.
- Q: What sizes are available for signs and banners? A: A range of sizes from small yard signs to large event banners, matched to the promotional need.

## Business Cards
- Q: What types of business cards do you offer? A: Standard, premium, matte, glossy and specialty finishes.
- Q: What materials are used for your business cards? A: Standard cardstock, premium paper and eco-friendly options.
- Q: Can I order a sample of my business card before placing a larger order? A: Yes, a sample can be ordered to check design, materials and finishes.
- Q: Can I print double-sided business cards? A: Most options allow double-sided printing.

## Print Products
- Q: Do you offer environmentally friendly print options? A: Recycled paper, soy-based inks and other environmentally conscious printing practices are named.
- Q: What printing options are available for my print products? A: Full-colour printing, high-resolution graphics, and finishes such as foil stamping and embossing.
- Q: Can I customize the design of my print products? A: Yes, by uploading a design or working with the design team.
- Q: Can I cancel or modify my order after it's been placed? A: Once confirmed, an order enters production quickly, so modifications may not be possible; the order should be reviewed before confirming.
