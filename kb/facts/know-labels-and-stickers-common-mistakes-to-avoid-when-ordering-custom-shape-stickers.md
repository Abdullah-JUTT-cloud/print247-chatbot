## Article

- Stickers have been around for several years and play an indispensable role in marketing different products. While ordering custom-shaped stickers for your brand may seem simple. In reality, small mistakes can occur that can waste money and time. The most common issues come from wrong sizes, weak files, poor material choices, and rushed approvals. When you know what to avoid, you get clean, sharp custom stickers that match your brand, stick well, and support your marketing goals.

## What Are Custom Shape Stickers?

- Custom shape stickers are contour-cut decals manufactured to fit your specific needs. In contrast to plain squares or circles, they are cut to match your logo, product, or design outline. Often, these are referred to as die cut stickers. By adding them to branded packaging, mailers, bottles, jars, or tech devices, your logo will stand out and be easy to spot.

## 5 Common Mistakes to Avoid When Ordering Custom Shape Stickers

- In most cases, custom shaped stickers are more than just "fun extras." They're used as marketing materials for packaging, product samples, trade show kits, and retail displays. When specs are off, your team feels the impact in real time.
- Here are five common mistakes print buyers and brand managers make, and how to avoid them on your next sticker printing project.

## 1. Choosing the Wrong Size

- Many teams pick a size that "sounds right" without checking real-life use. The result is custom stickers that are too big for the box panel or too small to read from a shelf. Always measure the surface first, then mock up the size on paper. Share photos with your print partner. This helps you pick the right size for branded packaging, bottles, pouches, or shipper boxes.

## 2. Neglecting Design Details

- In sticker printing, low-quality files are a major problem. When a logo, text, or bleed is blurry, the results can be dull or delayed. You should use vector files (AI, EPS, PDF) with high resolution and keep small text bold and simple. It is important that die cut stickers have a clear cut line. If you are unsure about bleeds, safe areas, or CMYK colors, ask for help.

## 3. Lack Of Purpose

- In some cases, teams order custom shape stickers purely for the sake of looking cool, without any clear purpose. Consequently, sales and branding are not supported by the designs. Think about what you want this sticker to achieve before you design it. " It might be to push a QR code, highlight a claim, or support a launch." You might push a QR code, highlight a claim, or support a launch. Strong marketing materials earn their space when they have a clear purpose.

## 4. Ignoring Material & Finishing Option

- There are different kinds of vinyl stickers and papers. When you use the wrong stock, you can get peeling, fading, or ink rub-off issues. You should choose vinyl stickers that are laminated, matte, or glossy if your product will be exposed to moisture or used outside. Soft-touch lamination or spot UV is ideal for premium branded packaging. Consider conditions such as heat, cold, and shipping when designing your custom stickers.

## 5. Not Reviewing Proofs Before Printing

- Reprints are costly when proofs are skipped or rushed. The digital proofs reveal the size, layout, and cut line of die-cut stickers. The spelling, logo size, color callouts, and placement on the sheet need to be checked thoroughly. Ensure that your sticker labels are in line with your brand guidelines. Before approval for sticker printing, have at least one more member of the team review the proof.
