There is no cart, no configurator and no instant price on the
site. The quote flow is: Get a Quote / contact form → consult →
dieline and free artwork support → unlimited digital mockups and
physical prototype approval → production starts after approval →
quality checks → delivery.

Quotes for printed roll stock or custom-sized bags are made within
2 to 3 business days (custom-quote page).

The 7 quote fields collected in chat are: product, material,
sizes, finishes, quantity, extras, skus. Defaults that never block
a quote: skus is 1, extras is none. A quote is ready when product,
sizes and quantity are known and the minimum order is satisfied.

Free design support is offered, and design is prepared free of
cost after initial payment (homepage). A "Book an Appointment"
link exists on the contact page.
