## Article

- Three sided seal pouches are a common type of packaging. These flexible pouches are flat and are sealed on three sides, from left, right, and bottom, leaving only the top open for filling. After filling, the top is also sealed, forming a flat and safe pouch ideal for single serve items like snacks, spices, or sample products. These pouches are widely used to pack a variety of products, which include food, cosmetics, pharma, and household product samples. They are simple, reliable, versatile and cost effective packaging solutions.

## What Exactly is a Three Sided Seal Pouch?

- A 3-sided seal pouch is made using durable and flexible laminated packaging material. They are often combined with layers of plastic like PET, PE, or PP with kraft paper or aluminum foil. Three edges are heat sealed during manufacturing, forming a pocket. The product is filled from the open side, which is then closed using heat sealing devices. These pouches sometimes feature clear windows, which improve their visual charm.
- These pouches usually lie flat, unlike stand-up pouches that have a bottom gusset and are able to stand upright on the shelves. They are lightweight, easy to store, and appropriate for both manual and automatic filling.
