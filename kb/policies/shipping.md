Shipping Policy summary, from the published page.

Shipping is free nationwide (all-inclusive pricing, no extra
charges). UPS is the exclusive carrier. Tracking numbers are
emailed once the order ships.

Timeline: after design approval, production moves forward within
48 hours; manufacturing takes 4 to 5 business days; UPS transit
takes 6 to 7 business days across the USA, for a total of 12 to
15 business days after design approval.

Expedited orders can be completed in 5 to 7 business days, with
timelines varying by size, materials and complexity. A rush date
is never a commitment in chat; the team confirms it (see
facts/turnaround.md).

Weather delays and other logistics issues after the package
leaves the facility are outside the company's control. Support:
Support@print247.us, +1 (832) 807-3429.
