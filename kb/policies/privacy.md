Privacy Policy summary, from the published page.

Information collected: (a) information provided by the visitor —
name, email address, shipping address — when ordering, signing up
or communicating; (b) automatic information — IP address, device
information and site-interaction data via cookies or equivalent
technology.

Use: personalization, order fulfilment (shared with trusted third
parties such as shipping companies) and communication (order
updates, promotions; opt-out available). Mobile information is
never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing.

Protection: SSL encryption and access control — authorized
employees only, bound by confidentiality. Cookies are used for
basic functionality, performance and preferences. Data is retained
as long as needed to provide services and comply with law.

Limitation stated on the page: no website can guarantee immunity
from unauthorized access. The policy may change with notice;
changes do not affect previously collected data.
