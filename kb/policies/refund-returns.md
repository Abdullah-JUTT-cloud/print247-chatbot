Refund and Returns summary, from the published page.

Return-request window: 5 business days after receiving the order
to REQUEST a return. This is the request step, after the Terms
page's 3-working-day fault-notify step. Products must be in
original condition, unused, as received, with tags and inserts
intact, plus proof of purchase. Initiate at Support@print247.us;
once approved, shipping instructions are provided. Returns without
prior approval are not accepted. Return shipping is the customer's
responsibility unless the defect is verified as company-side.

Eligibility: returns only for defective or incorrect items — NOT
for fully customized or client-designed packaging made from
scratch. Returns are accepted if different materials were used
than confirmed at final design approval. Claims need clear photo
evidence within 5 business days of delivery. Transit-damaged
packaging must be refused at delivery with immediate photo proof.
Slight colour or measurement variations (CMYK vs RGB) are not
defects. Client-side issues (bad design files, mishandling,
misuse, poor storage) do not qualify.

Company-side defects: the affected quantity is reprinted free of
charge; the company's decision after a 5-business-day internal
review is conclusive.

Cancellation: allowed within 4 hours of proof approval; a 5 percent
service fee applies after processing but before production; once
in the press stage, no cancellations or refunds. Orders can be
held in complimentary warehouse storage until the shipping date
is confirmed.

Accepted payment methods on this page: ACH, Zelle, Wire Transfer,
Zoho Payments (credit and debit cards).
